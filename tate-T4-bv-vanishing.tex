\section*{BV-cohomology vanishing and the unreduced cotangent lift}

\emph{Closure-layer organizing question.}  Which finite-window
homological vanishing of the BV operator
\(\Delta=Q+\{I_0,-\}\), in degree \(+1\) on the local-functional
complex \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_w)\) of the formal
mixed habitat
\(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{0,\mathrm{hol}}\) with
brane \(L=\R_{\mathrm{brane}}\times\{0\}\times\{p\}\) and ordinary
\(\lie{gl}_N\) gauge data, would supply an endpoint-admissible
coefficient module for
Problem~\ref{prob:formal-factorization-center}, and to what extent does
the closed BCOV vanishing of \cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov}, or its
open-closed extension \cite{costello-li-open-closed-bcov}, control any
of those four named cocycle classes in this geometry?

\emph{Named obstruction tuple, with explicit cocycle representatives.}
The endpoint obstruction is the four-component class
\[
  \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{BV,end}}
    =\bigl(
      \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}},\,
      \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}},\,
      \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}},\,
      \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}
     \bigr)
\]
of Definition~\ref{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple}.  In order:
\(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\) is the residual class of the compatible
finite Casimir family
\((C^M_{\mathfrak h})_{M\ge1}\),
\(C^M_{\mathfrak h}=\sum_{d\le M}\sum_ie_{d,i}\otimes e^i_d\), in the
cokernel of the represented-kernel map into the strict pair
\(\mathfrak h_\Pi\widehat\otimes D_\oplus\)
(\ref{lem:tate-casimir-obstruction});
\(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\) is the obstruction to a brane-line
extension of the bulk Costello propagator
\(P_{\epsilon,L}\) of \cite{costello-renormalization}*{Ch.~2 \S2,
Ch.~5 \S6} and of bracket transport with ordinary \(\lie{gl}_N\)

\ref{thm:hadamard-mittag-leffler}~item~(2);

  W(P_{\epsilon,L},I)

      W_\Gamma(P_{\epsilon,L},I)

of \cite{costello-renormalization}*{Ch.~2 \S6}; and
\(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) is the degree-\(+1\) BV cocycle class
\[
  \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}
    \in
    H^1\!\bigl(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_w),
      Q+\{I_0,-\}\bigr)
\]
representing the local-functional anomaly of the mixed brane-defect
QME of Problem~\ref{prob:weighted-rg-locality}.  The BV degree
convention is the cotangent-coordinate grading of
\ref{def:app-unreduced-bv-degrees} together with the sign data of
\ref{def:app-sign-hamiltonian-cyclic} and
\ref{cor:app-sign-universal-dual-normalization}: \(|\Delta|=+1\), the
reduced Hamiltonian boundary \(P_0\)-bracket has degree \(0\), and the

\emph{Hypothesis comparison, five axes.}  The cited theorems and the
present local model differ along five independent axes which are
verified cocycle-wise below:
(S1)~compact Hodge-theoretic geometry vs.\ the formal
\(\widehat{\C^2}_0\) jet model;
(S2)~pure holomorphic-volume kinematics

coefficient-system Tate direction
\(\widehat{\mathfrak h}\widehat\otimes\widehat{\mathfrak
h}^{\vee}_{\mathrm{cont}}\) in which the Casimir must converge.

\emph{Unique survivor.}  Theorem~\ref{thm:costello-li-no-coefficient-endpoint}:
the closed BCOV vanishing of \cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov} and the

\subsection*{Hypothesis separation on \texorpdfstring{$\R^2\times\C^2$}{R2 x C2}
   with brane \texorpdfstring{$L=\R_{\mathrm{brane}}\times\{0\}\times\{p\}$}{L} and \texorpdfstring{$\lie{gl}_N$}{gl\_N}}

\begin{prop}[Costello--Li 2012/2015 hypothesis separation for the
   brane-defect endpoint]\label{prop:costello-li-2012-hypothesis-separation}
  Let \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{BV,end}}=(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}},
  \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}},\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}},
  \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}})\) be the four-component endpoint
  obstruction class of \ref{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple} for the
  formal local geometry
  \(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{0,\mathrm{hol}}\) with
  brane \(L=\R_{\mathrm{brane}}\times\{0\}\times\{p\}\) and ordinary
  \(\lie{gl}_N\) gauge data.  The hypotheses of
  \ref{stmt:costello-li-2012-restated} differ from this local model
  along five independent axes.  In each axis the failure is named at
  the level of the cocycle representative the Costello--Li theorem does
  not control:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item[(S1)] \emph{Compact Hodge-theoretic geometry vs.\ formal
      jet.}  The 2012 theorem requires compact Calabi-Yau \(X\) with
      Hodge-theoretic harmonic projection
      \(\Pi_{\mathrm{harm}}\colon\Omega^{0,\bullet}\to
      \mathcal H^{0,\bullet}\); on the formal jet
      \(\widehat{\C^2}_0\) there is no harmonic projector and
      \(H^*_{\mathrm{Dol}}(\widehat{\C^2}_0)=\C\) sits in degree zero
      only.  Hence the 2012 BV-cohomology vanishing
      \(H^*_{\mathrm{BV},\mathrm{BCOV}}(X)=0\) above degree zero is not
      a statement about
      \(\bigl(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_w),\Delta\bigr)\)
      on \(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{0,\mathrm{hol}}\),
      and the cocycle representative
      of \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) is not its image.  The 2015
      open-closed theorem replaces compact \(X\) by flat \(\C^d\) (\(d\)
      odd) but keeps the holomorphic-volume kinematics and the
      \(\lie{gl}(N\mid N)\) anomaly cancellation; it is a different
      geometric and coefficient setting.
    \item[(S2)] \emph{Pure holomorphic-volume vs.\ mixed
      topological--holomorphic kinematics.}  The 2012 BV differential is
      \(Q_{\mathrm{BCOV}}=\bar\partial+t\,\partial_\Omega\) on
      \(\mathrm{PV}(X)[[t]]\).  The kinematic differential of the
      present model is
      \(D=d_{\R^2}+\bar\partial_{\C^2}\) on
      \(\Omega^\bullet_{\R^2}(I)\widehat\otimes
      \Omega^{0,\bullet}_{\C^2}\widehat\otimes
      (\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1])\)
      (the native holomorphic \(E_2\) factorization algebra of the
      Repo-local CLAUDE).  The two complexes share neither generators
      nor differential, so the cocycle representative of any
      Costello--Li class on \((\mathrm{PV}(X)[[t]],Q_{\mathrm{BCOV}})\)
      is not transferable to a cocycle of \(\Delta\) on the mixed
      complex without a separate kinematic comparison map; none is
      supplied by the 2012/2015 theorems.  The protected sector of
      twisted M-theory \cite{costello-twistedM} carries this mixed
      kinematic shape as physical motivation, but no theorem from
      \cite{costello-twistedM} is used here as a closure.
    \item[(S3)] \emph{No brane vs.\ topological line defect.}  The 2012
      theorem has no brane sector; the propagator
      \(P^{\mathrm{2012}}_{\epsilon,L}\) on \(\mathrm{PV}(X)[[t]]\)
      carries no boundary condition along any submanifold.  The 2015
      theorem has an open-closed sector on flat \(\C^d\) with open gauge
      \(\lie{gl}(N\mid N)\), but the open boundary is the boundary of a
      worldsheet disk in \(\C^d\), not a transverse line
      \(L\subset\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2\) point-supported in the
      holomorphic direction.  Hence neither theorem extends
      \(P^{\mathrm{base}}_{\epsilon,L}\) across \(L\) with ordinary
      \(\lie{gl}_N\) brackets, and the obstruction
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\) of
      \ref{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple} is uncontrolled.
    \item[(S4)] \emph{Supertrace cancellation vs.\ ordinary
      \(\lie{gl}_N\) scalar contact.}  On \(\lie{gl}(N\mid N)\) the
      \cite{costello-li-open-closed-bcov} cancellation
      \(\mathrm{str}(I)=N-N=0\) kills the would-be U(1)-anomaly at
      every loop order.  On ordinary \(\lie{gl}_N\) the same closed
      exchange supplies the scalar contact term
      \(\hbar N\omega(f,g)\gamma_{\mathbf 1}\) of
      \ref{thm:app-scalar-contact-qme-class}, with cohomology class
      \(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)\).  This class
      is not killed by Costello--Li graph signs; it is the local
      trace-line avatar of the cancelled supertrace anomaly, not a
      graph-level identification with it.  Hence
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) carries the residual
      \(\hbar N[\bar c]\) summand for ordinary \(\lie{gl}_N\), and that
      summand is uncontrolled by either Costello--Li theorem in its
      stated form.
    \item[(S5)] \emph{Worldsheet \(t\)-Tate vs.\ coefficient-system
      Tate.}  The 2012 Tate parameter \(t\) is a worldsheet \(S^1\)
      rotation completion of the polyvector field complex.  The Tate
      direction required by
      Problem~\ref{prob:formal-factorization-center} is along the
      coefficient pair
      \[
        \widehat{\mathfrak h}\,\widehat\otimes\,
        \widehat{\mathfrak h}^{\vee}_{\mathrm{cont}},
        \qquad
        C_{\mathfrak h}=\sum_{d\ge1}\sum_ie_{d,i}\otimes e^i_d.
      \]
      By \ref{lem:tate-casimir-obstruction} the strict pair
      \((\mathfrak h_\Pi,D_\oplus)\) carries no represented kernel
      projecting to every finite Casimir
      \(C^M_{\mathfrak h}\): the dual side admits only finitely many
      degree components, while the compatible kernel has nonzero
      identity component in every \(H_d^\vee\).  Hence the cocycle
      representative
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}=[(C^M_{\mathfrak h})_M]\) is nonzero
      in the cokernel.  The 2012/2015 theorems are silent on this
      coefficient direction; their \(t\)-Tate completion is orthogonal
      to it.
  \end{enumerate}

  Each axis identifies a distinct uncontrolled component of
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{BV,end}}\): (S1)+(S2) prevent any direct
  identification of \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) with a Costello--Li
  cocycle; (S3) prevents control of \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\);
  (S4) names the residual \(\hbar N[\bar c]\) summand of
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\); (S5) names the cokernel class of
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\).  The remaining component
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}\) is the failure of the strict
  finite-window Mittag-Leffler tower to be preserved by the recursion
  \(W(P_{\epsilon,L},I)\) of \ref{rmk:t4-costello-renorm-datum}; this
  preservation is not asserted by any compact-target Costello--Li
  theorem.
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}
  Each axis is a direct comparison of the hypotheses of
  \ref{stmt:costello-li-2012-restated} (and its 2015 sequel) with the
  data of \ref{thm:main-local}, the linear-heat / Casimir analysis
  (\ref{rmk:linear-heat-versus-bv-kernel},
  \ref{lem:tate-casimir-obstruction}), the scalar contact theorem
  \ref{thm:app-scalar-contact-qme-class}, and the strict-window habitat
  \ref{defn:strict-ml-tate-habitat}.  Concretely:

  \emph{(S1).}  Compactness enters \cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov}
  through the Hodge-theoretic harmonic projection used to construct
  the BV propagator and to verify the QME at the parametrix limit
  \(\epsilon\to0\), \(L\to\infty\); on the formal jet
  \(\widehat{\C^2}_0\) this projection has no analogue and
  \(H^*_{\mathrm{Dol}}(\widehat{\C^2}_0)\) is one-dimensional.

  \emph{(S2).}  The 2012 BV differential is purely Dolbeault plus the
  divergence \(t\,\partial_\Omega\); the present
  \(D=d_{\R^2}+\bar\partial_{\C^2}\) carries an additional de Rham
  factor on the topological line.  No symmetric-monoidal map
  \((\mathrm{PV}(X)[[t]],Q_{\mathrm{BCOV}})\to(\Omega^\bullet_{\R^2}
  (I)\widehat\otimes\Omega^{0,\bullet}_{\C^2}\widehat\otimes\cdots,D)\)
  is supplied by the cited theorems.

  \emph{(S3).}  The brane \(L\) is transverse to the topological
  factor and point-supported in the holomorphic factor; the open
  boundary in \cite{costello-li-open-closed-bcov} is a worldsheet
  boundary on flat \(\C^d\), not a Wilson line on a transverse
  topological direction.  The bulk Hadamard parametrix
  \(P^{\mathrm{base}}_{\epsilon,L}\) of
  \cite{costello-renormalization}*{Ch.~3} on
  \(\R^2\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\) does not by itself decide the
  brane-line trace.

  \emph{(S4).}  By \ref{thm:app-scalar-contact-qme-class} the closed
  exchange between two Hamiltonian sources
  \(B_f,B_g\) on the brane line produces the universal scalar contact
  density
  \(\hbar N\,\omega(f,g)\,\gamma_{\mathbf 1}(t)\,a(t)b(t)\).  Its
  associated-graded scalar symbol is the Lie 2-cocycle
  \(\omega(f,g)\,\gamma_{\mathbf 1}\) on \(\bar A\), with
  \(\omega(z_1,z_2)=1\) by
  \ref{cor:app-sign-universal-dual-normalization}; this is a nonzero
  class in
  \(H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)\) by \ref{thm:main-local}~part~(f).
  On \(\lie{gl}(N\mid N)\) the same scalar is multiplied by
  \(\mathrm{sdim}(\C^{N|N})=N-N=0\), killing the representative.  On
  ordinary \(\lie{gl}_N\) it is \(\hbar N\), nonzero, and the
  Costello--Li sequel does not supply a counterterm absorbing it.

  \emph{(S5).}  The compatible Casimir family
  \((C^M_{\mathfrak h})_M\) projects nonzero into every
  \(H_d\otimes H_d^\vee\) for \(d\le M\), while the strict direct-sum
  dual \(D_\oplus=\bigoplus_dH_d^\vee\) has only finitely many nonzero
  degree components per element.  Hence
  \([C_{\mathfrak h}]\not\in\mathrm{im}(\mathfrak h_\Pi\widehat\otimes
  D_\oplus\to(\mathfrak h_\Pi\widehat\otimes D_\oplus)^{\wedge\bullet})\)
  by \ref{lem:tate-casimir-obstruction}; equivalently the cocycle
  representative of
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\) is nonzero, and the worldsheet
  \(t\)-Tate completion of \cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov} commutes
  with this coefficient direction trivially.

  Finally, \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}\) is the assertion that
  \(W(P_{\epsilon,L},-)\) sends the strict ML habitat
  \(\Cat{MLTateChain}_{\geq0}^{\mathrm{str}}\) of
  \ref{defn:strict-ml-tate-habitat} into itself with surjective
  finite-window transition maps; this is a statement about the
  algebraic-coefficient direction of the propagator, not the
  worldsheet direction, and is not part of either Costello--Li
  theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{thm}[Costello--Li endpoint coefficient criterion]
\label{thm:costello-li-no-coefficient-endpoint}
  The Costello--Li 2012 BCOV construction
  \cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov} and its 2015 open-closed extension
  \cite{costello-li-open-closed-bcov}, in the forms restated in
  \ref{stmt:costello-li-2012-restated}, supply an endpoint-admissible
  coefficient module of
  Definition~\ref{defn:endpoint-admissible-coefficient-module} for the

  classical data are added:
  \[
    \text{(E2) coefficient Casimir kernel},\quad
    \text{(E3) mixed brane-defect transport},\quad
    \text{(E5) endpoint RG preservation},
  \]
  with (E1) and (E4) the inherited finite-window and Mittag-Leffler

  vanishing of the QME component
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) of

  none of the four cocycle representatives in
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{BV,end}}\) is forced to vanish by either

\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
  An endpoint-admissible coefficient module requires a coefficient
  kernel
  \(C_\ast\in\mathfrak h_\ast\widehat\otimes D_\ast\) whose projection
  to every finite Hamiltonian window is the finite Casimir
  \(C^M_{\mathfrak h}\), together with mixed brane-defect transport of

  (Definition~\ref{defn:endpoint-admissible-coefficient-module}).  By
  Proposition~\ref{prop:costello-li-2012-hypothesis-separation}, each
  axis (S1)--(S5) names a distinct cocycle in
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{BV,end}}\) that the Costello--Li hypotheses
  do not control: (S5) for \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\), (S3) for
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\), the algebraic-coefficient direction
  for \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}\), and (S1)+(S2)+(S4) for
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\).  Hence (E2), (E3), (E5), and the QME
  vanishing are not consequences of the cited theorems.
\end{proof}

\begin{defn}[BV endpoint obstruction tuple, with explicit cocycle

\label{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple}
  For the formal mixed local geometry
  \(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{0,\mathrm{hol}}\) with
  brane \(L=\R_{\mathrm{brane}}\times\{0\}\times\{p\}\) and ordinary
  \(\lie{gl}_N\) gauge data, the BV endpoint obstruction tuple is
  \[
    \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{BV,end}}
      =\bigl(
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}},\,
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}},\,
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}},\,
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}
       \bigr).
  \]
  Each component is named with its habitat, BV degree, and explicit
  cocycle representative:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(O\arabic*)]
    \item \emph{Casimir cokernel class
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\)} --- habitat
      \(\mathrm{coker}\bigl(\mathfrak h_\Pi\widehat\otimes D_\oplus
      \to\varprojlim_M(\mathfrak h\otimes\mathfrak h^\vee)_{\le M}\bigr)\),
      degree \(0\); cocycle representative the compatible family
      \[
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}=
        \bigl[(C^M_{\mathfrak h})_{M\ge1}\bigr],
        \qquad
        C^M_{\mathfrak h}=\sum_{d\le M}\sum_ie_{d,i}\otimes e^i_d,
      \]
      with transition maps the standard truncations
      \(C^{M+1}_{\mathfrak h}\mapsto C^M_{\mathfrak h}\); nonzero in
      the strict pair by \ref{lem:tate-casimir-obstruction}.
    \item \emph{Brane-defect transport class
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\)} --- habitat
      \(H^0\!\bigl(\mathrm{Hom}^\bullet_{\mathrm{loc}}
        (P^{\mathrm{base}}_{\epsilon,L},
         P^{\mathrm{brane}}_{\epsilon,L}),\,\delta_L\bigr)\),
      degree \(0\); cocycle representative the brane-line restriction
      datum
      \[
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}=
        \bigl[\delta_L^*P^{\mathrm{base}}_{\epsilon,L}
          -P^{\mathrm{brane}}_{\epsilon,L}\bigr]
      \]
      together with the failure of bracket transport
      \(\{\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\}_{\mathrm{open}}\) of
      \ref{lem:app-sign-current-source} across \(L\) with ordinary
      \(\lie{gl}_N\) brackets, equivalently the Hadamard locality
      class of \ref{thm:hadamard-mittag-leffler}~item~(2).
    \item \emph{RG-window preservation class
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}\)} --- habitat the Mittag-Leffler
      \(\varprojlim^1H^0\) of the strict-window functional complex
      \(\Cat{MLTateChain}_{\geq0}^{\mathrm{str}}\) of
      \ref{defn:strict-ml-tate-habitat}, degree \(0\); cocycle
      representative the compatible family of recursion-induced
      transition failures
      \[
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}
        =\bigl[
          \mathrm{T}^{M,M-1}\circ
          W(P_{\epsilon,L},-)|_{\le M}
          -W(P_{\epsilon,L},-)|_{\le M-1}\circ\mathrm{T}^{M,M-1}
        \bigr]
        \in\varprojlim\nolimits^1_M H^0,
      \]
      where \(\mathrm{T}^{M,M-1}\) is the strict ML truncation of
      \ref{defn:strict-ml-tate-habitat} and
      \(W(P_{\epsilon,L},-)\) is the Costello recursion of
      \ref{rmk:t4-costello-renorm-datum}.
    \item \emph{QME anomaly class
      \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\)} --- habitat the local-functional
      cohomology
      \[
        H^1\!\bigl(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_w),\,
          \Delta\bigr),
        \qquad
        \Delta=Q+\{I_0,-\},\ \ |\Delta|=+1,
      \]
      degree \(+1\); cocycle representative the local functional
      \[
        \mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}
          =\bigl[QI_0+\hbar\Delta_LI_0
                 +\tfrac12\{I_0,I_0\}_L\bigr]
      \]
      produced by the quantum master equation of
      \cite{costello-renormalization}*{Ch.~5} at scale \(L\),
      restricted to the mixed brane-defect interaction \(I_0\) of
      Problem~\ref{prob:weighted-rg-locality}.  In the ordinary
      \(\lie{gl}_N\) branch this class carries the residual scalar
      contact summand
      \(\hbar N\,\omega(f,g)\,\gamma_{\mathbf 1}\) of
      \ref{thm:app-scalar-contact-qme-class}; in the balanced
      \(\C^{N|N}\) branch the same summand vanishes by
      \ref{thm:app-balanced-supertrace-qme-cancellation}.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{defn}

\begin{prop}[Endpoint construction is equivalent to vanishing of the
   tuple]
\label{prop:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple}
  An endpoint-admissible coefficient module of
  Definition~\ref{defn:endpoint-admissible-coefficient-module} for the
  brane-defect geometry of
  \ref{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple} exists if and only if all
  four cocycle classes
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\), \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\),
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}\), \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) of
  \ref{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple} vanish and a compatible
  choice of primitives is made.  In particular, a Costello--Li type
  BV-cohomology theorem closes the present coefficient endpoint only
  when it certifies vanishing of all four classes simultaneously, with
  primitives compatible with the strict finite-window Mittag-Leffler
  envelope of \ref{defn:strict-ml-tate-habitat}.
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}
  \emph{(O1)\(\Leftrightarrow\)(E2).}
  Definition~\ref{defn:endpoint-admissible-coefficient-module}(E2) is
  the existence of a coefficient kernel
  \(C_*\in\mathfrak h_*\widehat\otimes D_*\) whose finite-window
  projections are the \(C^M_{\mathfrak h}\); equivalently a primitive
  for the cocycle representative of
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{Cas}}\) under the represented-kernel map.

  \emph{(O2)\(\Leftrightarrow\)(E3).}  Condition (E3) requires
  transport of bracket
  \(\{\,\cdot\,,\,\cdot\,\}_{\mathrm{open}}\), propagator
  \(P^{\mathrm{base}}_{\epsilon,L}\), interaction \(I_0\), and
  counterterm representatives across the brane line \(L\) with ordinary
  \(\lie{gl}_N\) brackets; equivalently a primitive for the
  Hom-complex cocycle representative of \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{def}}\).

  \emph{(O3)\(\Leftrightarrow\)(E5).}  Condition (E5) is preservation
  of the strict finite-window Mittag-Leffler tower under the Costello
  recursion \(W(P_{\epsilon,L},-)\); equivalently vanishing of the
  \(\varprojlim^1H^0\) class of
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{RG}}\).

  \emph{(O4) and the quantum endpoint.}  The endpoint QME is the
  vanishing of the degree-\(+1\) local-functional class
  \(\mathfrak o_{\mathrm{QME}}\) in
  \(H^1\bigl(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_w),\Delta\bigr)\),
  which by \ref{def:app-unreduced-bv-degrees} has \(|\Delta|=+1\) on
  the cotangent-coordinate grading of the local-functional complex.

  Necessity is the same chain of equivalences in reverse: a nonzero
  component removes one of the endpoint data just listed.  The
  primitive-compatibility clause is the requirement that the chosen
  cobounding primitives be morphisms in the strict ML habitat
  \(\Cat{MLTateChain}_{\geq0}^{\mathrm{str}}\), so that the endpoint
  module sits in
  \(\Cat{TateChain}_{\geq0}^{\mathrm{adm}}\) (\ref{stmt:tate-model-envelope}).
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Costello--Li extension criterion}

\begin{thm}[Costello--Li extension criterion for the mixed coefficient endpoint]\label{thm:t4-four-missing}
  Any Costello--Li type theorem that proves an endpoint-admissible
  coefficient module for the setup here must supply the following four
  components simultaneously:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Non-compact / Omega-background BV-cohomology
      vanishing.} A version of \ref{stmt:costello-li-2012-restated}
      on $\R^2\times\C^2$, either via a separately anchored
      Omega-background route or via a compactification with Lagrangian
      boundary at infinity.
    \item \emph{Topological--holomorphic mixed kinematics.} The
      vanishing must hold on the kinematic differential
      $D=d_{\R^2}+\bar\partial_{\C^2}$, with the linearized
      complex of \ref{rmk:linear-heat-versus-bv-kernel}, and on
      the Hamiltonian sector with cotangent $[1]$ extension.
    \item \emph{Brane-defect extension.} The vanishing must allow
      the transverse one-real-dimensional defect \(L\) on
      which open gauge data $\lie{gl}_N$ live; not the flat-$\C^d$
      $\lie{gl}(N\mid N)$ setup of
      \cite{costello-li-open-closed-bcov}. It must also decide the
      scalar contact class of
      Theorem~\ref{thm:app-scalar-contact-qme-class}: closed
      exchange cancellation, supertrace removal, central-character
      normalization, or renormalized boundary generators.
    \item \emph{Coefficient-system Tate completion.} The
      coefficient direction
      $\widehat{\mathfrak h}\,\widehat\otimes\,
      \widehat{\mathfrak h}^{\vee}_{\mathrm{cont}}$ must support the
      Casimir as a convergent kernel. By
      \ref{lem:tate-casimir-obstruction} this is a category-level
      change, not a damping factor.
  \end{enumerate}
  These four inputs are the coefficient data of
  \ref{prob:formal-factorization-center}.  They are the necessary data
  for turning the Costello--Li convention template into the mixed
  brane-defect coefficient theorem used here.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
  Component (1) supplies the compactness/Hodge-theoretic replacement
  needed in (S1).  Component (2) supplies the mixed
  topological--holomorphic complex required by (S2).  Component (3)
  supplies the line-defect transport and ordinary \(\lie{gl}_N\) scalar
  anomaly decision required by (S3)--(S4).  Component (4) is precisely
  condition (E2) of
  Definition~\ref{defn:endpoint-admissible-coefficient-module}; together
  with the transport and RG requirements in (E3) and (E5), it is the
  coefficient endpoint datum.  Proposition~\ref{prop:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple}
  identifies these requirements with vanishing of the obstruction tuple.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Costello--Li convention boundary]\label{rmk:t4-external-comparison-boundary}
  The local Hamiltonian BF sector supplies the formal-disk coefficient

  sources is imported as a closure of the present coefficient
  endpoint; the closure data are precisely the named obstruction
  components in
  Definition~\ref{defn:bv-endpoint-obstruction-tuple}, and the
  endpoint-admissible coefficient module of
  Definition~\ref{defn:endpoint-admissible-coefficient-module} exists
  if and only if all four components vanish.
\end{rmk}
