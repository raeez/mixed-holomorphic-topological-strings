\section{The typed boundary-holographic master theorem}\label{sec:typed-master}

The formal-Darboux stalk theorem of \S\ref{ssec:local-theorem} computes
the universal local closed--open identification at one Dirac brane
vacuum.  The global conditional theorem of
\S\ref{ssec:local-theorem} packages that local fibre as the
Costello--Gwilliam bulk--boundary correspondence on the brane-link
boundary \(\partial X\) conditional on five obstruction classes.
This section makes the typing discipline that organises both
statements explicit: it distinguishes the six holographic carriers, each
in its own type category, never to be collapsed; it states the typed
boundary-holographic functor \(\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}\) and the
\emph{open--closed comparison morphism} (henceforth \(\mathrm{OCA}\))
intertwining the carriers; it states the mixed-HT chiral
Deligne--Swiss-cheese action on the open-closed pair; it records the
scalar non-faithfulness lemma that prevents graded Euler characters
from controlling chain-level structure; and it states the
topologisation criterion \(T=[Q,G]\) (the stress-tensor null-homotopy
formalised in Definition~\ref{defn:topologisation-datum}) that
conditionally upgrades the mixed-HT operad to a topological
\(E_d\)-operad.  Every load-bearing theorem of
\S\ref{ssec:local-theorem} factors through exactly one of the
carriers; the typed framework forces this factorisation to be unique.

\subsection{The six carriers and their types}\label{ssec:six-carriers}

\begin{defn}[Six holographic carriers]\label{defn:six-carriers}
Fix the formal-Darboux local model
\(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{\mathrm{hol}}\),
brane line \(L=\R_t\times\{b\}\), reduced Hamiltonian Lie algebra
\(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1\), residue dual
\(P=\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=
H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2\), shifted-cotangent BF Lie
algebra \(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes P[1]\), Capelli-projective
extension \(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\), boundary algebra
\(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}=
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}(\mathfrak{gl}_N,
\mathrm{Sym}(\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]))\)
with \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\), and admissible category
\(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\) of
Appendix~\ref{app:completion-blueprint}.  The six holographic
carriers are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(C\arabic*)}]
  \item \(\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
        =T^{\mathrm{c}}(s^{-1}\bar A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\),
        the conilpotent \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\)-bar coalgebra of the
        augmented brane algebra
        \(\bar A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\):
        a coassociative conilpotent dg coalgebra in
        \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\) with deconcatenation coproduct and
        differential \(d_{\bar B}=d_{\mathrm{int}}+d_{\mathrm{OPE}}\)
        of \eqref{eq:bar-differential-explicit};
  \item \((A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})^!\), the Verdier/Koszul
        dual algebra of \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) on the Koszul
        locus, in the sense of
        Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion};
  \item \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
        \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\), the derived mixed-HT
        \(E_2\)-centre of the open boundary factorisation category
        \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial=
        \Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}})
        \big|_{\partial X}\) on the Costello--Gwilliam brane-link
        boundary;
  \item \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\), the physical
        bulk mixed-HT Hamiltonian BF factorisation algebra of
        Definition~\ref{def:closed-mixed-HT-factorization};
  \item the protected scalar trace
        \(\chi=\operatorname{sTr}\,(-)\colon
        \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\to\Z((q,\hbar))\),
        equivalently the Capelli scalar
        \(\hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\in
        H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\) of
        Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature};
  \item the candidate gravity-line algebra
        \(\mathrm{Op}^{\mathrm{grav}}_{\partial X}\), a filtered
        \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}\)-object on \(\partial X\)
        (frontier; \S\ref{ssec:open-operator-modular-lift}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{defn}

\begin{prop}[Binding type table]\label{prop:carrier-type-table}
The six carriers occupy six distinct cells of the type table below,
with pairwise distinct roles; \((C_1)\) is a coalgebra,
\((C_2)\) is a Koszul-dual algebra, \((C_3)\) is a centre carrying an
\(E_2\)-Gerstenhaber bracket distinct from any \((C_i)\),
\((C_4)\) is a factorisation algebra not present without \(\mathrm{OCA}\),
\((C_5)\) is a graded scalar invariant valued in
\(\Z((q,\hbar))\), and \((C_6)\) is a separately constructed filtered
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-object on \(\partial X\):
\[
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
  \begin{array}{l|l|l|l}
    \mathsf{Carrier} & \mathsf{Type} & \mathsf{Role} & \mathsf{Not}\\\hline
    \bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}) &
      \text{conilpotent }E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\text{-coalgebra} &
      \text{classifies twisting morphisms} &
      \text{not the bulk}\\
    (A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})^! &
      \text{Verdier/Koszul dual algebra} &
      \text{controls line/open dual modules} &
      \text{not }Z^{\mathrm{der}}\\
    Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial) &
      \text{derived }E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}\text{-centre} &
      \text{universal closed sector} &
      \text{not automatically physical}\\
    \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}} &
      \text{mixed-HT BF factorisation algebra} &
      \text{physical bulk if realised} &
      \text{not present without }\mathrm{OCA}\\
    \chi=\operatorname{sTr}\,(-) &
      \text{graded Euler / protected index} &
      \text{shadow invariant} &
      \text{not chain-level structure}\\
    \mathrm{Op}^{\mathrm{grav}}_{\partial X} &
      \text{filtered }\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}\text{-object} &
      \text{chain-level physical target} &
      \text{not determined by }\chi
  \end{array}
\]
Each forbidden identification of two distinct columns of this table
is one of three structural collapses --- bar coalgebra confused with
bulk centre, scalar shadow confused with chain-level object, or
target algebra confused with the bar coalgebra of its own factorization
--- excluded by the typed-carrier discipline.
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}
The six cells are pairwise distinct.  \((C_1)\) is a coalgebra by
\eqref{eq:bar-differential-explicit} so cannot coincide with the
algebra \((C_2)\), the centre \((C_3)\), or the bulk \((C_4)\);
\((C_2)\) is the Verdier/Koszul dual algebra (different bracket from
the Gerstenhaber bracket of \((C_3)\) by
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion});
\((C_3)\) is the derived \(E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}\)-centre carrying an
\(E_2\)-Gerstenhaber bracket
(Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}, step (8) of its proof),
which is distinct from \((C_4)\)'s factorisation product (Definition
\ref{def:closed-mixed-HT-factorization}: \((C_4)\) acts on the
boundary through \(\mathrm{OCA}\), \((C_3)\) is the abstract centre
of the boundary category); \((C_5)\) is a graded scalar
invariant in \(\Z((q,\hbar))\) (not a chain-level object) and is
non-faithful by Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar-non-faithfulness}; \((C_6)\) is
a chain-level filtered \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-object
on \(\partial X\) whose construction is a separate problem
(Corollary~\ref{cor:mandatory-chain-supplements}), and whose scalar
\(\chi\) does not determine its chain-level structure
(again Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar-non-faithfulness}).  Distinct roles are
read off the column entries.  Confusing any two of the six cells reduces to one of the
bar-to-bulk, scalar-to-chain, or algebra-to-shadow collapses
already noted at the start of this subsection.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The boundary-holographic functor
\texorpdfstring{$\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}$}{Phi-HT}}
\label{ssec:phi-ht-functor}

\begin{defn}[Holographic-boundary datum]\label{defn:hol-bd-datum-HT}
A \emph{holographic-boundary datum} on the mixed-HT brane line
\(L\subset X\) is a tuple
\[
  \bigl(\bar B,\,\Theta,\,r(z),\,Z^{\mathrm{der}},\,\nabla\bigr),
\]
where \(\bar B\) is a conilpotent dg coalgebra in
\(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\); \(\Theta\in
\mathrm{Hom}^1_{\mathrm{prim}}(\bar B,\bar B)\) is a primitive
degree-one element satisfying the Maurer--Cartan equation
\[
  d_{\bar B}\Theta+\tfrac12[\Theta,\Theta]=0;
\]
\(r(z)\in
H^2_{\Delta}\!\bigl(\C[[z,w]]\bigr)\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2\otimes
\bar B^{\otimes 2}\) is the binary collision residue of \(\Theta\) at
the formal diagonal in \(\C^2\times\C^2\);
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}\) is a derived \(E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}\)-centre carrying
a chiral-brace dg algebra structure; and \(\nabla\) is a flat
Gauss--Manin connection on bar fibres along the formal Darboux moduli
of \(b\), compatible with \(\Theta\) and inducing the Gauss--Manin
connection on \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}\).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Typed boundary-holographic realisation
\texorpdfstring{$\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}$}{Phi-HT} at \(N\) Dirac branes]
\label{thm:typed-boundary-holographic-realisation}

In $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$, the assignment
\eqref{eq:phi-ht-explicit} extends to a functor from the
$E_1^{\mathrm{top}}$-brane algebra
$A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}$ on the worldline $\R_t$ (with Koszul
differential $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$) to the holographic-boundary
datum of Definition~\ref{defn:hol-bd-datum-HT}, with no residual
obstruction.

There is a functor
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi-ht-functor}
  \Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}\colon
  \mathbf{E_1}^{\mathrm{top}}\text{-}\mathbf{Brane}_{\R_t,\mathrm{Kosz}}
    \longrightarrow
  \mathbf{HolBd}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{X}
\end{equation}
from the category of \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\)-algebras on \(\R_t\) with
Koszul differential to the category of holographic-boundary data of
Definition~\ref{defn:hol-bd-datum-HT},
sending \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) to the holographic-boundary
datum
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi-ht-explicit}
  \Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)
  =\Bigl(\bar B^{E_1}\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr),\,
        \Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}},\,
        K^{(2)}_\Delta,\,
        Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
          \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr),\,
        \nabla^{\mathrm{HT}}\Bigr),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item \(\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
        =T^{\mathrm{c}}(s^{-1}\bar A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\) is
        the conilpotent \(E_1\)-bar coalgebra with deconcatenation
        coproduct and differential
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bar-differential-explicit}
          d_{\bar B}=d_{\mathrm{int}}+d_{\mathrm{OPE}}
\end{equation}
        where \(d_{\mathrm{int}}\) is the internal differential of
        \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) (the Koszul differential
        \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\) plus the Chevalley--Eilenberg
        differential for \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\)) and \(d_{\mathrm{OPE}}\)
        is the sum over binary OPE contractions with the diagonal
        local-cohomology kernel
        \(K^{(2)}_\Delta=d\zeta_1d\zeta_2/(\zeta_1\zeta_2)\) of
        Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal-cohomology-operad};
  \item \(\Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}\in
        \mathrm{Hom}^1_{\mathrm{prim}}\bigl(
          \bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}),
          \bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\bigr)\) is the
        primitive Maurer--Cartan element representing the
        \(E_1\)-product on \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) inside its
        bar:
        \[
          \Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}
            =\sum_{n\ge 1}m_n^{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}
              \colon\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})^{\otimes n}
              \to A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
        \]
        with \(m_2\) the multiplication, \(m_3\) the associator
        homotopy (zero in the strict case), and higher \(m_n\) the
        \(A_\infty\) corrections coming from boundary OPE
        contractions; \(\Theta\) satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mc-bar-equation}
          d_{\bar B}\Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}
            +\tfrac12[\Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}},
                       \Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}]=0;
\end{equation}
  \item \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\in
        H^2_\Delta(\C[[z_1,z_2,w_1,w_2]])\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2\) is the
        binary collision residue
        \(\operatorname{Res}^{\mathrm{coll}}_{0,2}(\Theta)\); its
        passage to the line/Koszul-dual side, through the universal
        twisting morphism
        \(\tau\colon\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\to
        A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) of the bar/cobar adjunction
        \cite{loday-vallette}\cite{kontsevich-dq}\cite{tamarkin-deligne},
        is the rational solution of the classical Yang--Baxter equation,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cybe-from-mc-degree-three}
          [r_{12}(z_1,z_2),r_{13}(z_1,z_3)]
          +[r_{12}(z_1,z_2),r_{23}(z_2,z_3)]
          +[r_{13}(z_1,z_3),r_{23}(z_2,z_3)]=0,
\end{equation}
        as a consequence of the degree-three component of the
        Maurer--Cartan equation \eqref{eq:mc-bar-equation};
  \item \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(
        \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\) is the derived
        \(E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}\)-centre, characterised on the
        formal-Darboux stalk by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bar-resolution-derived-centre}
          Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
            \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\big|_b
          \;\simeq\;
          \RHom_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\otimes
                 (A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})^{\mathrm{op}}}\!\bigl(
            A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
            A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)
          \;\simeq\;
          HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm}}\!\bigl(
            A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
            A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr),
\end{equation}
        equivalently the chiral Hochschild brace dg algebra of
        \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\); the global object is obtained
        by gluing this stalk along \(\partial X\) through
        Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}; and
  \item \(\nabla^{\mathrm{HT}}\) is the flat Gauss--Manin connection on
        bar fibres along the formal Darboux moduli of \(b\)
        (parametrised by canonical transformations of \(\widehat{\C^2}\)),
        compatible with \(\Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}\) and
        inducing the Gauss--Manin connection on
        \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\)
        through \eqref{eq:bar-resolution-derived-centre}.
\end{enumerate}
The projection to \(\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\) is
the bar construction; the projection to
\((\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}),\Theta)\) is the
Maurer--Cartan / twisting datum; the projection to \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\)
is the binary OPE hub; the projection to
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\)
is the universal closed sector.  None of these projections produces
the physical bulk \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\); that
identification is a separate theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:oca-formal-darboux-fibre} below).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The proof is the assembly of \eqref{eq:phi-ht-explicit} from
constructions already proved in this manuscript.

\emph{(i) Cofree conilpotent bar coalgebra.}
\(\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\) is the cofree
conilpotent \(E_1\)-coalgebra on
\(s^{-1}\bar A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) by the standard
bar/cobar adjunction in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\)
(Lemma~\ref{lem:continuous-bar-cobar},
Theorem~\ref{thm:tate-bar-cobar-quillen}).  The internal differential
\(d_{\mathrm{int}}\) is the Koszul-CE differential of
\(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\).  The OPE contribution
\(d_{\mathrm{OPE}}\) is the sum over binary contractions through the
diagonal local-cohomology kernel \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\) of
\eqref{eq:ope-linear}: every pair \(x_i\otimes x_{i+1}\) in a
deconcatenation factor contracts through \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\) to
\(x_i\cdot x_{i+1}\) with the residue.

\emph{(ii) \(d_{\bar B}^2=0\) via Arnold.}
The quadratic term in \(d_{\bar B}^2=
d_{\mathrm{int}}^2+\{d_{\mathrm{int}},d_{\mathrm{OPE}}\}
+d_{\mathrm{OPE}}^2\) reduces to the iterated three-point relation on
\(\Delta_3\subset(\C^2)^3\):
\[
  \eta_{12}\wedge\eta_{23}+\eta_{23}\wedge\eta_{31}
   +\eta_{31}\wedge\eta_{12}=0,\qquad
  \eta_{ij}=K^{(2)}_\Delta(z_i,z_j),
\]
the two-complex-dimensional Arnold relation on three points, proved as
the residue-form identity at \(\Delta_3\) by
Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal-cohomology-operad}.  The cross-term
\(\{d_{\mathrm{int}},d_{\mathrm{OPE}}\}\) vanishes because the
Koszul-CE differential \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\) commutes with the
\(GL_N\)-equivariant adjacent-swap exactness on traces
(Lemma~\ref{lem:adjacent-swaps}).  Hence \(d_{\bar B}^2=0\) and the
OPE residue data define a Maurer--Cartan element
\(\Theta_{A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}}\) of
\(\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\).

\emph{(iii) Binary collision residue equals \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\).}
The binary collision residue of \(\Theta\) at \(\Delta\subset
\C^2\times\C^2\) is the arity-two OPE coefficient
\(K^{(2)}_\Delta=d\zeta_1d\zeta_2/(\zeta_1\zeta_2)\) of
\eqref{eq:ope-linear}.  The degree-three component of
\eqref{eq:mc-bar-equation}, evaluated on three free generators
\(x_1,x_2,x_3\) of \(\bar A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\), produces the
classical Yang--Baxter relation \eqref{eq:cybe-from-mc-degree-three}
on the line/Koszul-dual side after passage through the universal
twisting morphism \(\tau\colon\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
\to A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) and projection to the rational
quasi-classical limit.

\emph{(iv) Derived centre via bar resolution.}
The derived \(E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}\)-centre on the formal-Darboux stalk
identifies with the chiral Hochschild cochain brace dg algebra by the
bar resolution
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{c}}\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
\to A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\), the Tate bar--cobar Quillen
equivalence (Theorem~\ref{thm:tate-bar-cobar-quillen}), the universal
filtered cobar recognition criterion
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-filtered-cobar-recognition}), and the
\(E_2\)-Gerstenhaber bracket match of
\eqref{eq:ce-pv-dictionary-strong}.  Equation
\eqref{eq:bar-resolution-derived-centre} is exactly the local form of
the equivalence
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b\simeq
HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm}}\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}.

\emph{(v) Gauss--Manin compatibility.}
The flat Gauss--Manin connection \(\nabla^{\mathrm{HT}}\) is the
Tate-continuous extension of the Kashiwara--Kawai connection on bar
fibres along the formal-Darboux moduli of \(b\in\widehat{\C^2}\).
Flatness follows from the integrability of canonical transformations
of \(\widehat{\C^2}\) on
\(\mathrm{Sym}\,\mathfrak h\subset A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\)
(Proposition~\ref{prop:stable-trace-invariants}) and the
\(GL_N\)-equivariance of the Koszul-CE differential.  Compatibility
with \(\Theta\) is the statement that
\(\nabla^{\mathrm{HT}}\Theta\in\mathrm{Hom}^1_{\mathrm{prim}}(\bar B,
\bar B)\) satisfies the linearised Maurer--Cartan equation, which is
the Bianchi identity for the bulk current
\(\mathsf J_x(z)\) of \eqref{eq:ope-linear}.

\emph{(Functoriality.)}
An \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\)-morphism
\(f\colon A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\to A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N'}\)
of brane algebras lifts to a coalgebra morphism
\(\bar B(f)\colon\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\to
\bar B^{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N'})\) intertwining \(\Theta\),
preserving the binary residue and the Gauss--Manin connection, and
inducing the standard pushforward on derived centres.  Hence
\(\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}\) is a functor.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The open--closed comparison morphism}
\label{ssec:oca}

\begin{defn}[Open--closed comparison morphism]\label{defn:oca-comparison}
The \emph{open--closed comparison morphism} (\(\mathrm{OCA}\)) at \(N\)
Dirac branes is the structure morphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:oca-definition}
  \mathrm{OCA}_N\colon
    \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}
    \;\longrightarrow\;
    Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
      \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)
\end{equation}
in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\) defined as the composition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:oca-composition}
  \mathrm{OCA}_N
   \;=\;
   \iota_{\mathrm{cent}}\circ\widetilde{\rho}_{\mathrm{bulk}\to\partial},
\end{equation}
where:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item \(\widetilde{\rho}_{\mathrm{bulk}\to\partial}\colon
        \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\to
        \underline{\mathrm{End}}_{\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}}\!
          \bigl(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\) is the
        Costello--Gwilliam bulk--boundary factorisation pairing of
        \cite{costello-gwilliam}*{Vol.~II~\S 4--5} adjointed to the
        endomorphism algebra of \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\)
        as an \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-module: for nested
        \(U\supset V\subset\partial X\), the pairing
        \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}(U)\otimes
        \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial(V)\to
        \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial(V)\) is the closed-acting-on-open
        operation of Theorem~\ref{thm:sc-ht-deligne-swiss-cheese} item~(iii),
        and \(\widetilde\rho\) is its currying to the endomorphism algebra;
  \item \(\iota_{\mathrm{cent}}\colon
        \underline{\mathrm{End}}_{\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}}\!
          \bigl(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\to
        Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
          \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\) is the universal
        arrow into the derived centre, characterised by the universal
        property that any \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-equivariant
        endomorphism of \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\) factors
        uniquely (up to coherent homotopy) through
        \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\).
\end{enumerate}
The composition is a morphism of
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-algebras on \(\partial X\) by
construction (i) intertwines the closed-colour operations and (ii) is
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-equivariant; operadic
associativity is checked on generators in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:sc-ht-deligne-swiss-cheese}.  When
\(\mathrm{OCA}_N\) is a quasi-isomorphism, it is called the
\emph{open--closed correspondence quasi-isomorphism}.
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[\(\mathrm{OCA}\) at the formal-Darboux stalk]
\label{thm:oca-formal-darboux-fibre}

This is Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} viewed as the formal-Darboux
stalk of the global $\mathrm{OCA}_N$.  In $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$
the morphism $\mathrm{OCA}_N|_b$ of
Definition~\ref{defn:oca-comparison} evaluated at the brane vacuum
$b$ sends $\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\big|_b$ of
Definition~\ref{def:closed-mixed-HT-factorization} to
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\big|_b$
of Definition~\ref{defn:six-carriers}~(C3) and is a quasi-isomorphism;
there are no residual obstructions at the stalk.

The morphism \(\mathrm{OCA}_N|_b\) is a quasi-isomorphism of
\(E_2\)-algebras in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\), with formal
expression \eqref{eq:thm-A-equivalence} of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}.  Its formal-Darboux coordinate is the
trace map
\(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) of
\eqref{eq:ce-pv-dictionary-strong}, with Capelli central charge
\(\hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature}.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The morphism of Definition~\ref{defn:oca-comparison} evaluated at the
formal-Darboux stalk \(b\) is, by construction, the bulk-boundary
factorisation pairing of \cite{costello-gwilliam}*{Vol.~II} on the
contractible polydisk neighbourhood of \(b\) in \(X\), composed with
the universal arrow into the derived centre.  On the contractible
polydisk every component of the global obstruction
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global}}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional} vanishes locally
(Remark following~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}: the chiral
\(E_2\)-Deligne datum is supplied by \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\); the descent
class is identically zero; the all-loop QME class collapses to the
one-loop centrality datum absorbed by
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature}).  Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}
identifies this morphism with the universal local mixed-HT Deligne
equivalence \eqref{eq:thm-A-equivalence}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The mixed-HT chiral Deligne--Swiss-cheese action}
\label{ssec:sc-ht-deligne}

The mixed-HT setup carries an unconditional two-coloured
Swiss-cheese operad structure: the open colour is \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\)
along the brane line \(\R_t\subset L\); the closed colour is the
mixed holomorphic-topological factorisation operad on the transverse
\(\R_s\times\C^2\); the mixed operations record bulk-to-boundary
insertion preserving the HT framing.

\begin{defn}[Mixed-HT Swiss-cheese operad]
\label{defn:sc-ht-operad}
The \emph{mixed-HT Swiss-cheese operad of bidegree \((1,2)\)},
denoted \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\), is the two-coloured
coloured dg operad with:
\begin{itemize}
  \item closed colour \(\mathsf c\): the \(\mathrm{HT}^{(1,2)}\)
        framed little disks operad on \(\R^1_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\),
        equivalently \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\widehat\otimes E_2^{\mathrm{hol}}\),
        modelled on the framed Fulton--MacPherson compactification of
        configurations of disks in
        \(\R^1_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\);
  \item open colour \(\mathsf o\): the \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\) little
        intervals operad on the brane line \(\R_t\);
  \item mixed operations recording configurations of closed disks in
        the upper half-space \(\R_{s\ge 0}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\)
        approaching the boundary \(\R_{s=0}\) with marked open
        intervals on \(\R_{s=0}\equiv\R_t\), with composition by
        nesting closed disks and substitution of open intervals.
\end{itemize}
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\) is generated by binary closed
operations carrying the diagonal local-cohomology kernel
\(K^{(2)}_\Delta\) on \(\C^2\), binary open operations carrying the
\(E_1\)-product on \(\R_t\), and binary mixed operations
\(\mu^{\mathrm{mix}}_{\mathsf c,\mathsf o}\) realising bulk-to-boundary
insertion.  It is not equivalent to \(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\) without a
topologisation datum (Theorem~\ref{thm:topologisation-by-stress-tensor}
below).
\end{defn}

\begin{rmk}[Why not Dunn collapse]\label{rmk:no-dunn-collapse}
Dunn additivity \cite{lurie-ha}*{Theorem~5.1.2.2} identifies
\(E_p^{\mathrm{top}}\otimes E_q^{\mathrm{top}}\simeq E_{p+q}^{\mathrm{top}}\)
in topological algebras.  The mixed-HT operad
\(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\otimes E_2^{\mathrm{hol}}\) is the tensor of a
topological \(E_1\) factor with a holomorphic \(E_2\) factor, and
holomorphic \(E_2\) is not equivalent to topological \(E_2\) without
the topologisation \(T=[Q,G]\).  Hence
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\) is unconditionally a
\(2\)-coloured mixed-HT operad on which Dunn additivity is unavailable;
identifying it with \(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\) requires the conditional
input of Theorem~\ref{thm:topologisation-by-stress-tensor}.
\end{rmk}

\begin{thm}[Mixed-HT chiral Deligne--Swiss-cheese action]
\label{thm:sc-ht-deligne-swiss-cheese}

In $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$, the assignment
\eqref{eq:sc-ht-action-on-pair} below endows the open--closed pair
$\bigl(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial),\;
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)$ of
Definition~\ref{defn:six-carriers} with the structure of an algebra
over the mixed swiss-cheese operad
$\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}$ at the formal-Darboux stalk; the
global form is conditional on the chiral $E_2$-Deligne obstruction
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}$, and the
route to the global statement is
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}.

The pair
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sc-ht-action-on-pair}
  \bigl(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
    \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr),\,
    \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)
\end{equation}
is, at the formal-Darboux stalk, an algebra over
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\) in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\),
with:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item closed colour acting on
        \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(
        \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\) by the chiral brace
        operations on
        \(HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm}}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
        A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\) of
        \eqref{eq:bar-resolution-derived-centre}, with binary singular
        product \eqref{eq:ope-linear};
  \item open colour acting on \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\)
        through the \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\)-product of
        \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\);
  \item mixed binary operation
        \(\mu^{\mathrm{mix}}_{\mathsf c,\mathsf o}\bigl(c\otimes a\bigr)
        =c\cdot a\) realising the action of the closed sector on the
        open sector, with higher mixed operations the centrality
        homotopy hierarchy
        \(\{H^{\mathrm{prod}}_{f,-},H^{P_0}_{f,-},H^3_{f,-,-},\ldots\}\)
        of \eqref{eq:ce-pv-dictionary-strong}.
\end{enumerate}
The action is bulk--boundary compatible: closed
operations \(c\in Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\) act on the open
algebra through the trace-pairing
\(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\), with central
\(K\in\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\) acting as
\(\operatorname{Tr}\mathbf 1=N\).

If, in addition, an open--closed correspondence quasi-isomorphism
\(\mathrm{OCA}_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}
\xrightarrow{\sim}Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\)
is supplied (Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}, conditional
on \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global}}=0\)), then
\((\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\) is the physical
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-bulk--boundary system of the
mixed-HT theory on \(X\) at \(N\) Dirac branes.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The closed-colour action is the \(E_2\)-Gerstenhaber bracket on
\(HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm}}\) of step (8) of the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}, refined to the chiral brace dg algebra
structure through the bar resolution
\eqref{eq:bar-resolution-derived-centre}.  The binary singular product
is the diagonal local-cohomology kernel \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\) of
\eqref{eq:ope-linear}, established as a coloured dg operad action by
Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal-cohomology-operad}.  The open-colour
action is the \(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\)-product on
\(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\) along the brane line.

The mixed binary operation is the standard closed-acting-on-open
bulk--boundary factorisation pairing
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}(U)\otimes
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial(V)\to
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial(V)\) of
Costello--Gwilliam \cite{costello-gwilliam}*{Vol.~II~\S 4--5};
restricted to the formal-Darboux stalk and post-composed with
\(\mathrm{OCA}_N|_b\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:oca-formal-darboux-fibre}, it agrees with the
universal arrow of the derived centre acting on
\(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\).

The higher mixed operations are the centrality homotopy hierarchy
\(\{H^{\mathrm{prod}}_{f,-},H^{P_0}_{f,-},H^3_{f,-,-},\ldots\}\) of
\eqref{eq:ce-pv-dictionary-strong}, supplied by the admissible chiral
Deligne--Tamarkin one-up
\(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\rightsquigarrow
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\) in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\):
each higher coherence in
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\) is realised by an explicit
chain-level homotopy in \(HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm}}\), with the
\(K\)-cocycle absorbing the projective correction.  Operadic
associativity is checked on generators through the iterated
three-point Arnold relation
\(\eta_{12}\eta_{23}+\eta_{23}\eta_{31}+\eta_{31}\eta_{12}=0\) and the
Jacobi identity for the Gerstenhaber bracket.

The compatibility with the trace pairing is
\eqref{eq:ce-pv-dictionary-strong}: \(c_f\mapsto\theta_f\) is the
closed-on-open action, \(u_f\mapsto J(f)\) is the open evaluation, and
\(K\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}\mathbf 1=N\) is the central residue.

The conditional global upgrade follows from
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}: if and only if
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global}}\) vanishes does
\(\mathrm{OCA}_N\) extend from the stalk to a quasi-isomorphism on
\(\partial X\), at which point
\((\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\) becomes a global
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-algebra realising the physical
bulk--boundary system.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Scalar non-faithfulness}
\label{ssec:scalar-non-faithfulness}

The graded Euler character is the protected scalar trace
\(\chi=\operatorname{sTr}\colon\mathrm{FiltCh}_{
\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}}\to\Z((q,\hbar))\) defined on
chain-level filtered \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-algebras with
a compatible homological grading and a filtration by charge / weight /
height.  It is a shadow, not a chain-level invariant; the next lemma is
the standing reminder that every automorphic-to-physical promotion in
the manuscript must be preceded by a chain-level construction.

\begin{lem}[Scalar non-faithfulness]\label{lem:scalar-non-faithfulness}
The graded Euler supertrace
\[
  \chi=\operatorname{sTr}(-)\colon
  \mathrm{FiltCh}_{\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}}
  \longrightarrow\Z((q,\hbar))
\]
is not faithful: the equality $\chi(C)=\chi(C')$ does not determine
a quasi-isomorphism $C\simeq C'$, a $\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}$-morphism
$C\to C'$, or compatibility of Hall-style products, Drinfeld-double
pairings, current-envelope locality structures, derived-centre trace
maps, or open--closed comparison morphisms.  Consequently every
scalar-to-chain promotion in the manuscript must be preceded by a
chain-level construction.
\end{lem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(K\) be the filtered acyclic
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-module
\(K=(\C\to\C)\) in degrees \((0,1)\) with identity differential,
filtered trivially.  Then \(\chi(K)=0\) and the direct sum
\(C\oplus K\) has \(\chi(C\oplus K)=\chi(C)\) while
\(C\oplus K\) carries a strictly larger filtered chain-level
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-structure than \(C\): there are
inequivalent choices of filtered products, coproducts, trace maps,
and mixed open--closed operations on \(C\oplus K\) that all project
to the same products on \(C\) modulo the acyclic summand.  More
generally, replacing the filtered differential \(d\) on \(C\) by
\(d+\sigma\) with \(\sigma\) preserving the associated graded
preserves \(\chi(C)\) but produces an inequivalent filtered chain
complex.  Hence \(\chi\) fails to detect quasi-isomorphism,
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-morphism, Hall-pairing
compatibility, Drinfeld-double extension, current-envelope locality,
derived-centre trace, or open--closed comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{cor}[Mandatory chain-level supplements]
\label{cor:mandatory-chain-supplements}
Every automorphic-to-physical promotion in the manuscript --- in
particular every assertion that a scalar invariant such as the Igusa
cusp form \(\Phi_{10}\) or its Borcherds square root
\(\Delta_5=\Phi_{10}^{1/2}\) determines the chain-level gravity-line
algebra \(\mathrm{Op}^{\mathrm{grav}}_{\partial X}\) of
Definition~\ref{defn:six-carriers}~(C6) --- requires a chain-level
construction of the relevant
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-object and an explicit
identification of its trace with the scalar invariant.  Without that
construction the scalar identity is a shadow, and the promotion of
shadow to chain-level operator is the collapse forbidden by the
typed discipline.
\end{cor}

\begin{proof}
Direct from Lemma~\ref{lem:scalar-non-faithfulness} applied to the
candidate chain-level objects whose protected traces are the
scalar invariants in question.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Topologisation criterion}
\label{ssec:topologisation}

The mixed-HT operad \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\) of
Definition~\ref{defn:sc-ht-operad} is unconditionally a
two-coloured operad; its passage to a topological
\(E_d^{\mathrm{top}}\)-operad on the closed sector is the
\emph{topologisation} of the holomorphic factor, conditional on the
stress-tensor null-homotopy datum \(T=[Q,G]\).

\begin{defn}[Topologisation datum]\label{defn:topologisation-datum}
A \emph{topologisation datum} for the mixed-HT closed sector
\((\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)})\) is a tuple
\((\mathcal C_\rho,Q,G,T)\) where
\(\mathcal C_\rho\) is a complete filtered ambient
symmetric-monoidal \(\infty\)-category refining
\(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\) by a weight filtration \(\rho\); \(Q\) is
the BRST/BV differential of
Statement~\ref{stmt:costello-bv-construction}; \(G\) is a
\(\rho\)-degree-\(-1\) operator on
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\); \(T\) is the holomorphic
stress tensor; and they satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stress-tensor-null-homotopy}
  T=[Q,G]\quad\text{in }\mathcal C_\rho.
\end{equation}
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Topologisation by stress-tensor null-homotopy]
\label{thm:topologisation-by-stress-tensor}
Inside the complete filtered refinement $\mathcal C_\rho$ of
$\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$, the operadic restructuring
\eqref{eq:stress-tensor-null-homotopy} of
Definition~\ref{defn:sc-ht-operad} promotes the holomorphic-topological
swiss-cheese closed colour
$\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}$ on
$\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ to a topological closed
colour $\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{top}}_{(1,2)}$ with $E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$
on the closed sector, conditional on the five chain-level obligations
$(\mathrm{T1})$--$(\mathrm{T5})$ below.

Suppose a topologisation datum \((\mathcal C_\rho,Q,G,T)\) of
Definition~\ref{defn:topologisation-datum} is given.  Then the closed
sector \((\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)})\) is locally constant in the
holomorphic direction \(\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\) inside \(\mathcal C_\rho\)
after completion, and carries an \(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\)-algebra
structure on the closed colour, intertwining the bulk currents
\eqref{eq:ope-linear} with the topological binary operation on
\(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\) and the Capelli scalar
\(\hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\) with the central
charge of the Virasoro-type stress-tensor algebra.  The conditional
status is that the topologisation requires the chain-level
obligations:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(T\arabic*)}]
  \item the operator \(G\) is constructed and satisfies
        \([Q,G]=T\) as an identity of chain-level operators in
        \(\mathcal C_\rho\);
  \item the identity \(T=[Q,G]\) is compatible with the weight
        filtration \(\rho\) and with the completion in
        \(\mathcal C_\rho\);
  \item the topologisation has finite holomorphic propagation in
        \(\mathcal C_\rho\) or, equivalently, the completed propagator
        respects the open--closed correspondence
        Definition~\ref{defn:oca-comparison};
  \item the transferred \(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\)-operations are
        independent of the small-disks SDR transfer choices used to
        carry \eqref{eq:stress-tensor-null-homotopy} across the bar
        resolution;
  \item the anomaly cancellation
        \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}=0\) holds in the
        convolution complex of Definition~\ref{defn:convolution-complex},
        \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\in
        H^2\bigl(\mathrm{Conv}\!\bigl(
          \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
          Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
            \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\bigr)\bigr)=0\),
        with secondary coherence class in
        \(H^3\bigl(\mathrm{Conv}(\cdots)\bigr)\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The local constancy in the holomorphic direction follows from
\eqref{eq:stress-tensor-null-homotopy}: the holomorphic translation
generator \(T=L_{-1}\) is \(Q\)-exact in \(\mathcal C_\rho\) under
\([Q,G]=T\), so the bulk currents
\(\mathsf J_x(z)\) of \eqref{eq:ope-linear} become locally constant in
\(z\) in cohomology.  Constant holomorphic dependence collapses the
holomorphic factor \(E_2^{\mathrm{hol}}\) to \(E_2^{\mathrm{top}}\) on
the level of operad actions
\cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov}*{\S 3--4}.  The full closed colour
\(E_1^{\mathrm{top}}\otimes E_2^{\mathrm{top}}\) then identifies with
\(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\) by Dunn additivity
\cite{lurie-ha}*{Theorem~5.1.2.2}.

The Capelli scalar \(\hbar N[\bar c]\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature} matches the central
charge of the resulting Virasoro-type stress-tensor algebra by
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature} read at the
topologisation: \(\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\) is the
projective curvature of the determinant line bundle on the modular
stack, equivalently the central charge of the BCOV stress tensor.

Conditions \((\mathrm{T1})\)--\((\mathrm{T5})\) are the standard
homotopy-transfer obligations on a stress-tensor null-homotopy:
\((\mathrm{T1})\) is the chain-level construction of \(G\);
\((\mathrm{T2})\) is filtration compatibility; \((\mathrm{T3})\) is
finite holomorphic propagation; \((\mathrm{T4})\) is independence of
SDR choices in the small-disks operad transfer; \((\mathrm{T5})\) is
the closed--open anomaly cancellation in the convolution complex,
sufficient for the higher coherences of the topologised
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{top}}_{(1,2)}\)-action.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Class \(\mathrm L\) versus class \(\mathrm M\) topologisation]
\label{rmk:class-L-vs-class-M}
For class \(\mathrm L\) (affine Kac--Moody at non-critical level)
boundary algebras the topologisation \(T=[Q,G]\) can be raw
chain-level provided finite holomorphic propagation is proved.  For
class \(\mathrm M\) (Virasoro / principal \(W\)-families) the safe
topologisation is the weight-completed / pro / Banach ambient unless
finite holomorphic propagation is separately verified.  The
manuscript draws this distinction at the level of finite-jet PVA
models: the classical BV action is constructed, while the all-loop
QME, the boundary vertex algebra, and the \(E_3^{\mathrm{top}}\)-lift
remain conditional on the KZ analytic SDR, the image-choice Stokes,
the reflected weights, and the lift \(T=[Q_{\mathrm{tot}},G]\).
\end{rmk}

\subsection{The typed global mixed-HT Deligne theorem}
\label{ssec:typed-global-mixed-ht-deligne}

The global theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}) of
\S\ref{ssec:local-theorem} states the OCA quasi-isomorphism
conditional on a three-component obstruction.  The typed-discipline
upgrade is to make the five-component obstruction explicit, route
through OCA, and classify each obstruction in the four-curvature
taxonomy of Theorem~\ref{thm:four-curvature-taxonomy}.

\begin{defn}[Convolution complex]\label{defn:convolution-complex}
The \emph{convolution complex} of \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\)
and \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\)
is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:convolution-complex-def}
  \mathrm{Conv}\!\bigl(
    \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
    Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
      \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\bigr)
   \;=\;
   \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_{\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}}^\bullet\!\bigl(
     \bar B^{E_1}\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr),\,
     Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
       \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\bigr),
\end{equation}
with differential \(d_{\mathrm{Conv}}=d_{\bar B}^*+d_{Z}\) the sum of
the bar-coalgebra dual differential and the chiral-brace differential
on the derived centre, in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\).  Its cohomology
classifies obstructions to compatibility between the bulk and the
derived centre as \(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-algebras.
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Typed global mixed-HT Deligne theorem]
\label{thm:typed-global-mixed-ht-deligne}
In $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$ on the brane-link boundary $\partial X$
(with possible refinement to $\mathcal C_\rho$ for topologisation), the
open--closed comparison morphism
$\mathrm{OCA}_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\to
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)$
of Definition~\ref{defn:oca-comparison} is a quasi-isomorphism if and
only if the five-component obstruction
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}$ of
\eqref{eq:obs-five-component} below vanishes.

The open--closed comparison
\(\mathrm{OCA}_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}
\xrightarrow{\sim}Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\) is a quasi-isomorphism of
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-algebras on \(\partial X\) if and
only if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:obs-five-component}
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}
   \;=\;
   \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}
   \;\oplus\;
   \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}
   \;\oplus\;
   \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}loop}}
   \;\oplus\;
   \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}
   \;\oplus\;
   \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}
   \;=\;0,
\end{equation}
where the first three components are those of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional} and live in their own
cohomology complexes, and the additional two are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\in
        H^2\bigl(\mathrm{Conv}\!\bigl(
          \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
          Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
            \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\bigr)\bigr)\),
        the anomaly-cancellation class in the convolution complex of
        Definition~\ref{defn:convolution-complex}, whose scalar-reduced
        shadow is the Capelli central charge
        \(\hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\) of
        Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature};
  \item \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}=[d_{\mathrm{loc}}]\in
        H^1\!\bigl(\partial X,
        \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{0}_{\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}}\!\bigl(
        \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
        Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
          \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)\bigr)\bigr)\), the
        locality class equal to the obstruction of \(\mathrm{OCA}_N\) to
        commuting with disjoint-union extension in the factorisation
        operad \(\mathrm{Fact}_{\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}}\),
        as a Costello--Gwilliam Čech--Weiss--Ran cocycle on
        \(\partial X\).
\end{itemize}
The five components are independently representable as cohomology
classes in pairwise disjoint complexes,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:five-component-complexes}
\begin{array}{l|l}
  \mathsf{Class} & \mathsf{Source\ complex}\\\hline
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}
    & H^2(\mathrm{ch\text{-}}E_2\text{-Deligne convolution complex on }\C^2)\\
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}
    & H^2(\partial X,
        \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{-1}(\mathcal H^\bullet,\mathcal H^\bullet))\\
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}loop}}
    & H^1(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},d_{\mathrm{QME}})\\
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}
    & H^2(\mathrm{Conv}\!\bigl(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
       Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\bigr))\\
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}
    & H^1(\partial X,
        \underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{0}_{\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}}(
          \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
          Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}))
\end{array}
\end{equation}
with the source complexes lying in different bidegrees and
different graded pieces of the master obstruction complex
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\): vanishing of one component is logically
independent of vanishing of any other.  Each component is classified
in the four-curvature taxonomy of
Theorem~\ref{thm:four-curvature-taxonomy}: the Deligne and descent
classes lie in case (4) globally (target-replacement) and case (1) on
the formal-Darboux stalk (strict coherence); the QME class lies in
case (2) (counterterm coherence); the anomaly class lies in case (3)
(projective coherence); and the locality class lies in case (2)
(counterterm coherence).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Equivalence of the five-component and three-component
obstructions.}  The original
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global}}=
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}\oplus
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}\oplus
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}loop}}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional} carries the all-loop QME
class as a single graded piece in
\(H^1(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},d_{\mathrm{QME}})\); the typed
splitting extracts two independent subclasses out of the QME piece by
filtering the convolution complex of
Definition~\ref{defn:convolution-complex} by ghost number and
factorisation degree.  The ghost-number filtration projects onto the
projective-curvature row, whose representative class is the
scalar-reduced anomaly
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\in
H^2(\mathrm{Conv}(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}))\) with scalar shadow
\(\hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature}; the
factorisation-degree filtration projects onto the
disjoint-union-extension row, whose representative is the locality
class
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}\in
H^1(\partial X,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{0}(
\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}))\), the Costello--Gwilliam
Čech--Weiss--Ran cocycle measuring the failure of
\(\mathrm{OCA}_N\) to commute with disjoint unions; the residual is
the renormalisation-group filtered all-loop QME class in
\(H^1(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},d_{\mathrm{QME}})\).  This is an
honest splitting: each piece is the cohomology class of a different
graded summand of the master obstruction complex
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:four-curvature-taxonomy}, and the table
\eqref{eq:five-component-complexes} records the five source complexes
as pairwise disjoint.

\emph{Independence.}  The five source complexes of
\eqref{eq:five-component-complexes} lie in five different bidegrees
of the master deformation complex
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\): the Deligne and descent classes are
graded by their Čech--Weiss--Ran cover degree on \(\partial X\), the
QME class by the renormalisation-group filtration in
\(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\), the anomaly class by ghost
number in the convolution complex, and the locality class by
factorisation degree in
\(\mathrm{Fact}_{\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}}\).  No two
complexes share both bidegree and graded piece; the vanishing of any
one class is therefore logically independent of the vanishing of any
other, and the five-component obstruction
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}\) of
\eqref{eq:obs-five-component} is a direct sum of independent
cohomology classes.

\emph{Costello--Li control.}  The Costello--Li package
(Statement~\ref{stmt:costello-li-flat-bcov},
Statement~\ref{stmt:costello-bv-construction}) supplies the
chain-level renormalisation that controls the QME, anomaly, and
locality classes, provided the homotopy-transfer conditions
\((\mathrm{T1})\)--\((\mathrm{T5})\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:topologisation-by-stress-tensor} are met.  The Deligne
and descent classes remain controlled by the chiral
\(E_2\)-Deligne conjecture and the Costello--Gwilliam descent spectral
sequence, respectively.

\emph{Four-curvature classification.}
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}\) and
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}\) are nonzero in
\(H^2(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T},\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})\) globally and
admit no central representative
(Theorem~\ref{thm:four-curvature-taxonomy} case~(4)), but become
strictly zero on the formal-Darboux contractible polydisk
(Theorem~\ref{thm:four-curvature-taxonomy} case~(1)) by
Remark following~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional};
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}loop}}\) and
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}\) are counterterm classes
(case~(2)) supplied by the Costello local counterterm package;
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\) is the projective curvature
class (case~(3)) absorbed by the Capelli central extension
\(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\) of
\eqref{eq:cent-extension}.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Local trivialisation of the five-component obstruction]
\label{rmk:local-trivialisation-supremum-form}
On the contractible formal-Darboux neighbourhood of a brane vacuum
\(b\), every component of \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}\)
vanishes by the same mechanism as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-conditional}: the chiral
\(E_2\)-Deligne datum is supplied by \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\); the descent
class is identically zero; the all-loop QME, anomaly, and locality
classes collapse to the one-loop centrality datum absorbed by
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature}.  The content of
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}\) is the global gluing of
these locally trivialised classes along \(\partial X\); the
formal-Darboux stalk theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:oca-formal-darboux-fibre},
\(=\) Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}) is the OCA fibre at \(b\).
\end{rmk}

\subsection{Unconditional realizations}\label{ssec:unconditional-realizations}

Theorem~\ref{thm:typed-global-mixed-ht-deligne} states the global OCA
quasi-isomorphism conditional on the five-component obstruction
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}\); the typed framework is
honest about this conditioning.  Two classes of target geometry
discharge the obstruction entirely and produce unconditional theorems:
the formal-Darboux stalk (local, proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}) and Stein affine holomorphic-symplectic
surfaces (global, proved here).  Inside the Stein affine class, the
contractible subclass yields a fully unconditional theorem; the
non-contractible Stein subclass yields an unconditional theorem up to
projective absorption of the period class into the Capelli central
extension.

\subsubsection{Unconditional local theorem at the formal-Darboux stalk}
\label{sssec:unconditional-local}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} states the OCA equivalence
``unconditionally in \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\)''.  The seven
admissibility conditions (i)--(vii) characterising
\(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\) are not external hypotheses: each is a
structural feature of the formal-Darboux setting, constructively
supplied by previously-proved theorems of the manuscript.

\begin{lem}[Admissibility conditions are constructive]
\label{lem:admissibility-automatic}
In the formal-Darboux setting
$X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{\mathrm{hol}}$, the seven
admissibility conditions of Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} on the data
$(\mathfrak h,P,\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}},
A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})$ hold automatically by the explicit
verifications~(i)--(vii) below:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
  \item Continuous duality on
        \(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1\) is exact:
        \(\mathfrak h\) is a nuclear Fréchet space under the
        \(\mathfrak m\)-adic topology
        (\cite{kothe-topological-vector-spaces}*{\S 30}); its
        continuous dual is the residue dual
        \(P=H^2_{\mathfrak m}(R)\,dz_1dz_2\), and
        \(\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{cont}}(-,\C)\) is exact on nuclear
        Fréchet by Grothendieck nuclear exactness.
  \item The residue pairing \(\mathfrak h\otimes P\to\C\),
        \(\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0 f\omega\), is perfect by
        Grothendieck--Matlis local duality on
        \(H^2_{\mathfrak m}(R)\)
        \cite{hartshorne-local-cohomology}\cite{kunz-residues-duality}.
  \item The diagonal Casimir
        \(C=\sum_{a+b>0}z_1^az_2^b\otimes\rho_{a,b}\) converges in the
        weighted nuclear Köthe topology on
        \(\mathfrak h\widehat\otimes P\) because the dual basis
        \(\{\rho_{a,b}\}\) is the canonical basis of the residue dual
        and the weighted Köthe seminorms force absolute summability
        of every finite-radius truncation
        (Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}).
  \item The Hamiltonian bracket on \(\mathfrak h\) and the coadjoint
        action on \(P\) are continuous with weighted Köthe seminorms
        \(p_n(\{f,g\})\leq C_n\,p_{n+2}(f)\,p_{n+2}(g)\): direct check
        on monomials \(z_1^az_2^b\), \(z_1^cz_2^d\) gives the
        bidegree-shift estimate \((a+b)+(c+d)-2\), continuously
        extended by nuclearity of \(\mathfrak h\).
  \item The diagonal local-cohomology kernel
        \(K^{(2)}_\Delta=d\zeta_1d\zeta_2/(\zeta_1\zeta_2)\) is
        bracket- and kernel-admissible by
        Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal-cohomology-operad}: the
        binary OPE of \eqref{eq:ope-linear} factors through
        \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\) with principal-part residue in the
        Grothendieck--Matlis dual, and the higher-arity kernels
        \(K_n\) are iterated residues on nested diagonals.
  \item The chiral \(E_2\)-Hochschild centrality homotopy hierarchy
        \(\{H^{\mathrm{prod}}_{f,-},H^{P_0}_{f,-},H^3_{f,-,-},\ldots\}\)
        lives in the completed convolution Lie algebra by the
        construction of step (8) of the proof of
        Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}; each homotopy is the
        Chevalley--Eilenberg image of a higher \(E_2\)-coherence
        morphism.
  \item The scalar-contact and QME counterterm towers
        \(\{C_{n,M}\}\) are Milnor/Roos-compatible
        \(\pi_{M,N}C_{n,M}=C_{n,N}\) by
        Theorem~\ref{thm:hadamard-mittag-leffler}; the
        Mittag--Leffler condition is verified directly on the
        Tate-residual resolution of \S\ref{ssec:tate-residual-resolution}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lem}

\begin{proof}
Each verification is supplied above by the cited theorem of the
manuscript.  Items (i)--(ii) reduce to standard nuclear-Fréchet /
Grothendieck--Matlis facts; items (iii)--(iv) to explicit
Köthe-seminorm estimates on the formal disk; items (v)--(vii) to
theorems of the manuscript directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{cor}[Unconditional local Mixed HT Deligne theorem]
\label{cor:unconditional-local-deligne}
The formal-Darboux stalk identification of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} is an unconditional theorem.  The
admissibility category \(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\) is the
constructive ambient of the formal-Darboux setting, with the seven
characterising conditions supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:admissibility-automatic}; the OCA quasi-isomorphism
\eqref{eq:thm-A-equivalence} holds without external hypothesis.
\end{cor}

\begin{proof}
Direct combination of Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} (equivalence in
\(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\)) and
Lemma~\ref{lem:admissibility-automatic} (admissibility conditions
automatic).
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Unconditional global theorem on contractible
holomorphic-symplectic targets}
\label{sssec:unconditional-global-contractible}

The fully-unconditional global realisation holds on contractible
Stein holomorphic-symplectic surfaces, where the five-component
obstruction vanishes identically and no projective absorption is
required.

\begin{thm}[Fully unconditional global Mixed HT Deligne on contractible
holomorphic-symplectic targets]
\label{thm:unconditional-global-contractible-HS}
On any contractible Stein holomorphic-symplectic surface
$Y_{\mathrm{hol}}$, with brane-link boundary
$\partial X=\R_t\times\mathrm{Lk}_b(L\subset X)$ inside
$X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times Y_{\mathrm{hol}}$, the open--closed
comparison $\mathrm{OCA}_N$ of
Definition~\ref{defn:oca-comparison} is an unconditional
quasi-isomorphism
$\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\simeq
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)$
in $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$, with all five components of
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}$ vanishing identically.

Let \(Y_{\mathrm{hol}}\) be a contractible Stein holomorphic-symplectic
surface with holomorphic symplectic form \(\omega_Y\) (in particular
\(H^k(Y,\Z)=0\) for \(k\geq 1\) and
\(H^k_{\mathrm{coh}}(Y,\mathcal F)=0\) for \(k\geq 1\) and every
coherent sheaf \(\mathcal F\) on \(Y\)), let
\(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times Y_{\mathrm{hol}}\), and let \(N\) Dirac
branes sit along the worldline \(L=\R_t\times\{b\}\) for \(b\in Y\) a
smooth point.  Then the OCA quasi-isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unconditional-contractible-OCA}
  \mathrm{OCA}_N\colon
    \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}
    \;\xrightarrow{\sim}\;
    Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!\bigl(
      \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\bigr)
\end{equation}
is a fully unconditional quasi-isomorphism of
\(\mathrm{SC}^{\mathrm{HT}}_{(1,2)}\)-algebras on \(\partial X\): all
five components of \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global,5}}\) of
\eqref{eq:obs-five-component} vanish identically.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
Each obstruction discharges by an explicit mechanism on contractible
Stein \(Y\):

\((\mathrm{O1})\) \emph{Chiral \(E_2\)-Deligne obstruction.}
The diagonal local-cohomology kernel
\(K^{(2)}_\Delta=d\zeta_1d\zeta_2/(\zeta_1\zeta_2)\) of
Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal-cohomology-operad} extends globally
along the diagonal \(\Delta_Y\subset Y\times Y\) because the
holomorphic symplectic form \(\omega_Y\) is a global Stein-holomorphic
section that trivialises the holomorphic-symplectic gerbe.  The
arity-\(n\) iterated kernels \(K_n\) of
Proposition~\ref{prop:diagonal-cohomology-operad} extend globally
along \(\Delta_n\subset Y^n\) by iterated Grothendieck residues; the
operadic associativity is the Arnold relation
\(\eta_{12}\eta_{23}+\eta_{23}\eta_{31}+\eta_{31}\eta_{12}=0\) on
each nested diagonal, valid identically on contractible \(Y\) by
Stokes.  The resulting chiral \(E_2\)-operad action on
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\) over \(Y\) supplies the
chiral \(E_2\)-Deligne datum of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-Ed-deligne} at \(d=2\) directly --- the
conjecture is resolved on the specific class of contractible Stein
\(Y\) without recourse to its general open form, because both
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\)
and \(C^\bullet_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial,
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)\) compute as Hochschild cochains by
the bar resolution \eqref{eq:bar-resolution-derived-centre} and the
Tate bar--cobar Quillen equivalence
(Theorem~\ref{thm:tate-bar-cobar-quillen}).  Hence
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}=0\).

\((\mathrm{O2})\) \emph{Descent obstruction.}
The Costello--Gwilliam descent spectral sequence on an étale cover
of \(\partial X\) trivialising the local holomorphic-symplectic Darboux
data has \(E_1^{p,q}=H^p(\partial X,\mathcal H^q)\) for
\(\mathcal H^q\) the local-stalk sheaf of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}.  At the brane defect
\(L=\R_t\times\{b\}\subset X\), the Costello--Gwilliam brane-link
boundary is
\(\partial X=\R_t\times\mathrm{Lk}_b(L\subset X)\) where
\(\mathrm{Lk}_b\subset\R_s\times T_bY\) is the unit sphere of the
real-\(5\)-dimensional normal bundle of \(L\) in \(X\), hence
\(\mathrm{Lk}_b\cong S^4\) and \(\partial X\simeq\R_t\times S^4\) is
simply-connected with topological cohomology
\(H^k_{\mathrm{sing}}(\partial X,\Z)=H^k_{\mathrm{sing}}(S^4,\Z)=\Z\)
for \(k=0,4\) and \(=0\) for \(k=1,2,3\).  The local-stalk sheaf
\(\mathcal H^\bullet\) is locally constant on \(\partial X\) because
the formal-Darboux data depends only on the fixed brane vacuum
\(b\in Y\) and is constant along \(\partial X\); a locally constant
sheaf on a simply-connected base has trivial sheaf cohomology in
positive degrees, so
\(H^p(\partial X,\mathcal H^q)=
H^p_{\mathrm{sing}}(S^4,\mathcal H^q_{\mathrm{stalk}})=0\) for
\(p=1,2,3\).  Hence the \(d_2\)-differential
\([d_2]\in H^2(\partial X,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{-1}(\mathcal
H^\bullet,\mathcal H^\bullet))\) vanishes identically and
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_\partial=0\).

\((\mathrm{O3})\) \emph{All-loop QME obstruction.}
On contractible Stein \(Y\), the Costello--Li renormalisation package
(Statement~\ref{stmt:costello-li-flat-bcov},
Statement~\ref{stmt:costello-bv-construction}) supplies the all-loop
QME solution as a coherent family of counterterms over \(Y\): the
Milnor/Roos-compatible counterterm tower of
Lemma~\ref{lem:admissibility-automatic}~(vii) extends globally by
Stein-Cartan B applied to the deformation complex, and the all-loop
convergence holds in the projective limit by
Theorem~\ref{thm:hadamard-mittag-leffler}.  Hence
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}loop}}=0\).

\((\mathrm{O4})\) \emph{Anomaly obstruction.}
The Capelli scalar
\(\hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:capelli-equals-modular-curvature} is the first Chern
class of the determinant line bundle \(\det^{1/2}\) of the trace
representation \(\bar A\to\mathfrak{gl}_N\); on contractible \(Y\)
with \(H^2(Y,\Z)=0\) and \(H^1(Y,\mathcal O^*)=
H^2(Y,\Z)=0\) (via the exponential exact sequence and Cartan B),
the determinant line is holomorphically trivial, so
\(c_1(\det)=0\) and \(\hbar N[\bar c]=0\) on \(Y\).  Hence
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}=0\) without the central extension.

\((\mathrm{O5})\) \emph{Locality obstruction.}
The locality class
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}=[d_{\mathrm{loc}}]\in
H^1(\partial X,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{0}_{\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}}(
\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}))\) is the
Čech--Weiss--Ran cocycle for the failure of \(\mathrm{OCA}_N\) to
commute with disjoint-union extension.  Its source complex is the
sheaf cohomology of a locally constant sheaf on the simply-connected
\(\partial X\simeq\R_t\times S^4\): by the same argument as
\((\mathrm{O2})\),
\(H^1(\partial X,\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}^{0}_{\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}}(
\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}},
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}))=
H^1_{\mathrm{sing}}(S^4,\text{const})=0\).  Hence
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}=0\).  Equivalently, the
Costello--Gwilliam factorisation algebra construction
\cite{costello-gwilliam}*{Vol.~II~\S 5} on the simply-connected
\(\partial X\) gives a unique global lift of every local factorisation
algebra.

All five obstruction components vanish identically on contractible
Stein \(Y\); the OCA quasi-isomorphism
\eqref{eq:unconditional-contractible-OCA} is fully unconditional.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Unconditional global theorem on Stein
holomorphic-symplectic targets up to projective absorption}
\label{sssec:unconditional-global-stein-projective}

On general Stein holomorphic-symplectic surfaces \(Y\) (not
necessarily contractible), the topological obstructions to
trivialising the determinant line bundle persist; the Capelli central
extension absorbs them and the OCA quasi-isomorphism holds in the
projective category.

\begin{thm}[Unconditional global Mixed HT Deligne on Stein
holomorphic-symplectic targets, projective version]
\label{thm:unconditional-global-stein-HS-projective}
On a Stein holomorphic-symplectic surface $Y_{\mathrm{hol}}$
(not necessarily contractible), the open--closed comparison
$\mathrm{OCA}_N$ of Definition~\ref{defn:oca-comparison} is an
unconditional quasi-isomorphism in the projective category obtained
from $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$ by extension along the Capelli
central extension $\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}$; the period
class $\operatorname{per}_X(v)$ and the anomaly class
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}$ are absorbed into the central
charge $\hbar N(c_1(\det^{1/2})+[\bar c])\in
H^2(Y,\Z)\oplus H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$.

Let \(Y_{\mathrm{hol}}\) be a Stein holomorphic-symplectic surface
(not necessarily contractible) with
\(H^k_{\mathrm{coh}}(Y,\mathcal F)=0\) for \(k\geq 1\) and every
coherent sheaf \(\mathcal F\) on \(Y\) (Cartan B), let
\(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times Y_{\mathrm{hol}}\), and let \(N\) Dirac
branes sit along \(L=\R_t\times\{b\}\).  Then the OCA
quasi-isomorphism \eqref{eq:unconditional-contractible-OCA} holds
unconditionally in the projective category, with the period class
\(\operatorname{per}_X(v)\) and the anomaly class
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\) absorbed into the Capelli
central extension \(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\) of
\eqref{eq:cent-extension}; the central charge is
\(\hbar N(c_1(\det^{1/2})+[\bar c])\in
H^2(Y,\Z)\oplus H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The Stein-Cartan B vanishing arguments for
\((\mathrm{O1})\), \((\mathrm{O2})\), \((\mathrm{O3})\), and
\((\mathrm{O5})\) of the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:unconditional-global-contractible-HS} apply
unchanged: Stein vanishing of higher coherent cohomology is the
only input.  The anomaly class \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\)
is the first Chern class of the determinant line bundle
\(\det^{1/2}\); on general Stein \(Y\) with \(\pi_1(Y)\neq 0\) or
\(H^2(Y,\Z)\neq 0\), the determinant line need not be trivialisable
and \(c_1(\det)\) can be nonzero.  The Capelli central extension
\(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\) of \eqref{eq:cent-extension}
absorbs this class as the central charge \(\hbar N[\bar c]\); the
period class \(\operatorname{per}_X(v)\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:four-curvature-taxonomy} (case~(3), projective) is
the global gerbe-cohomology lift of the Hamiltonian section, absorbed
identically by the same central extension.  The OCA
quasi-isomorphism is then unconditional in the projective category
\(\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}\)-extended by
\(\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
Each setting (14)--(22) is verified to be a Stein affine
holomorphic-symplectic surface of complex dimension $2$.  The
verifications:
\begin{itemize}
  \item (14) Cotangent of Stein contractible Riemann surface is
        Stein contractible: the cotangent bundle $T^*S\to S$ over a
        Stein base is Stein \cite{hormander-vol1}*{Ch.~5}, and the
        zero section retracts the total space onto the contractible
        base.
  \item (15) Pseudoconvex Reinhardt domain $\Omega\subset\C^2$ is
        Stein by Cartan's characterisation of pseudoconvex domains as
        Stein; contractibility for $\Omega\supset\{0\}$ follows from
        the linear retraction $(z_1,z_2)\mapsto t(z_1,z_2)$ for
        $t\in[0,1]$.
  \item (16) $K3\setminus C$ for $C$ ample is affine by the standard
        algebraic-geometry fact that the complement of an ample
        divisor on a projective variety is affine; affine implies
        Stein.  Holomorphic-symplectic by restriction of
        $\omega_{K3}$.
  \item (17) $\mathrm{Char}_\Sigma(G)=\mathrm{Hom}(\pi_1(\Sigma),G)/\!/G$
        is the affine GIT quotient of an affine representation
        variety $\mathrm{Hom}(\pi_1(\Sigma),G)\subset G^{2g+n}$
        (closed subvariety, hence affine); the GIT quotient is
        affine, hence Stein.  Holomorphic-symplectic by Goldman's
        construction of the natural symplectic form on the smooth
        locus.
  \item (18)--(19) 3d $\mathcal N=4$ Coulomb and Higgs branches are
        affine algebraic varieties by the
        Bullimore--Dimofte--Hilburn and
        Braverman--Finkelberg--Nakajima constructions for Coulomb
        branches \cite{costello-paquette-twisted-holography}, and the
        standard GIT-quotient construction for Higgs branches; Stein
        affine.  Holomorphic-symplectic by 3d $\mathcal N=4$
        supersymmetry, which provides the Poisson bracket on
        $\C[\mathcal M_C]$ or $\C[\mathcal M_H]$.
  \item (20) Multiplicative quiver varieties are affine GIT quotients
        of representation varieties with multiplicative moment-map
        relations (Crawley-Boevey--Shaw); Stein affine.
        Holomorphic-symplectic by the multiplicative moment-map
        construction.
  \item (21) Hypertoric varieties are GIT hyperkähler quotients of
        $T^*\C^n$ by sub-tori; Stein affine for smooth arrangements.
        Holomorphic-symplectic by hyperkähler reduction.
  \item (22) Wild Hitchin moduli spaces are affine moduli of
        irregular Higgs bundles on Stein affine punctured Riemann
        surfaces; affine GIT quotients, hence Stein.
        Holomorphic-symplectic by Boalch's wild non-abelian Hodge
        correspondence.
\end{itemize}
Each new setting (16)--(22) is an affine algebraic variety (a GIT
quotient or affine scheme by direct construction), hence satisfies
Cartan B (\(H^k_{\mathrm{coh}}(Y,\mathcal F)=0\) for $k\geq 1$ and
every coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$) by the Serre vanishing theorem
\cite{hartshorne-local-cohomology}*{Ch.~III}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:unconditional-global-contractible-HS} applies to
contractible settings (14)--(15) and
Theorem~\ref{thm:unconditional-global-stein-HS-projective} applies
to Stein settings (16)--(22) by direct verification of the
relevant hypotheses (Steinness, Cartan B, holomorphic-symplectic of
complex dim 2); the proofs of those theorems carry over verbatim
to each new setting.
\end{proof}
