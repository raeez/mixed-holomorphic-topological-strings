\begin{abstract}
\noindent\itshape
The local Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa field theory on
$\bR^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\bC^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$, truncated to its
Hamiltonian BF sector, poses at each brane point a mixed
holomorphic--topological Deligne problem: compare the bulk
factorization algebra with the Morita $E_2$-centre of the boundary
category. This volume proves exactly one formal-local stalk of that
problem, and maps with type-theoretic precision everything that
separates the stalk from the global theory. The proved stalk: at $N$
Dirac branes the closed local algebra is the continuous
Chevalley--Eilenberg algebra of
$\fg=\fh\ltimes\fh^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$,
$\fh=\bC[[z_1,z_2]]/\bC$, the boundary chart is the Koszul algebra of
the derived commuting variety, and the dictionary
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f$, $u_f\mapsto J(f)=\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)$ is the
unique formal-Darboux coordinate of the scalar-reduced stable trace
sector. The removed scalar line returns as the Capelli anomaly
$\hbW N[\bar c]$. This companion states the stalk theorem, the
uniqueness axioms, the completion analysis with its proved
obstructions, the typed ledger, and the three conjectures that point
at the compact world --- ending with the operator lift of the Igusa
form.
\end{abstract}

\section{The seed}\label{sec:seed}

A holomorphic--topological theory in complex dimension two is the
simplest habitat where the chiral world of the curve volumes meets the
Calabi--Yau world of the threefold volumes: two real topological
directions, two holomorphic ones, and at a worldline defect the
question every holography asks --- do the bulk observables equal the
centre of the boundary? The volume's discipline is to refuse the
question in the large and answer it exactly in the small. In the
small: at a formal holomorphic-symplectic brane point of
$X=\bR^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\bC^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$ with symplectic
form $dz_1\wedge dz_2$, the Hamiltonian vector fields form
$\fh=\bC[[z_1,z_2]]/\bC\cdot1$ with bracket
$\{f,g\}=\partial_{z_1}f\,\partial_{z_2}g
-\partial_{z_2}f\,\partial_{z_1}g$; the shifted-cotangent BF theory
has fields valued in $\fg=\fh\ltimes\fh^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$; and
$N$ Chan--Paton branes present the boundary as the derived commuting
variety $[\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)/GL_N]$, $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$.
The trace map
\[
J(f)\;=\;\overline{\Tr\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}
\]
is the whole local dictionary: it is the formal-Darboux coordinate of
the scalar-reduced stable trace sector, it satisfies
$\{J(f),J(g)\}=J(\{f,g\})+N[\{f,g\}]_0$, and the scalar defect
$[\{f,g\}]_0$ --- the constant the quotient by $\bC\cdot1$ discarded
--- returns as a projective anomaly, the Capelli scalar
$\hbW N[\bar c]$: on linear generators,
$\{\Tr\phi_1,\Tr\phi_2\}=N$. Nothing else about the global theory is
claimed. The volume's real product is the honest map: a typed ledger
of the obstructions --- descent, quantum master equation, anomaly,
locality, centre lift --- that separate this stalk from a compact
target, so that every global statement in the constellation must
either supply the named data or remain a criterion.

\section{The objects}\label{sec:objects}

\begin{definition}[The local pair]
Closed: the continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg algebra
$C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}(\fg)$, with
$\fh^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}$ the Grothendieck--Matlis residue dual
spanned by $\rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}dz_1dz_2$ with
$\langle\rho_{a,b},z_1^mz_2^n\rangle=\delta_{am}\delta_{bn}$. Open:
$A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
=C^\bullet_{\CE}(\fgl_N,\mathrm{Sym}(\fgl_N\oplus\fgl_N
\oplus\fgl_N[1]))$ with Koszul differential $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$;
the moment map is traceless, so the effective presentation replaces
$\fgl_N[1]$ by $\fsl_N[1]$. Stable: the trace algebra
$A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}\cong
\widehat{\mathbf S}_{\mathrm{Tate}}(\fh)$, reached by scalar
reduction, degreewise stable Procesi--Razmyslov trace passage, and the
Roos limit convention.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The admissible habitat]
All completions live in the admissible host category: filtered
nuclear-Tate complexes with a \emph{declared} BV coefficient dual
\textup{(distinct from the strict dual)}, declared operation and
kernel lists, and every inverse limit taken as a Roos complex with
$\varprojlim^1$ tracked as obstruction data. The verified carrier is
the weighted Tate envelope $B_w$, $w(d)=q^d$, $q>1$, the minimal
complete filtered subspace closing both Costello's heat-kernel BV
calculus and the Schouten bracket; its Casimir
$C_{\fh,w}=\sum_d\sum_i(w(d)e_{d,i})\otimes(w(d)^{-1}e^i_d)$
converges, while the strict product--sum pair is a proved
\emph{non-object}: the diagonal coevaluation diverges.
\end{definition}

Three operads are kept distinct --- topological little disks
$E_2^{\mathrm{top}}$, the holomorphic factorization operad
$E_2^{\mathrm{ch,hol}}$ in complex dimension two, and the mixed
Morita-centre structure $E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}$ --- with no identification
asserted without a topologisation or restriction datum. Four
deformation parameters are kept distinct: $\hbQME$ \textup{(Costello
loops)}, $\hbW$ \textup{(Moyal--Weyl--Capelli)}, $\hbar_\omega$
\textup{(equivariant)}, $\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}$. Four map symbols
are kept distinct: the generator boundary value, the
Hochschild-centre lift \textup{(a Maurer--Cartan datum, not a
construction)}, the proved coordinate isomorphism, and the quantum
map \textup{(never asserted)}. The native singular kernel is
two-dimensional: $K^{(2)}_\Delta=d\zeta_1\,d\zeta_2/(\zeta_1\zeta_2)$;
a curve vertex algebra exists only after a restriction datum with a
transverse boundary condition --- without the restriction functor, no
vertex algebra is constructed.

\section{The theorems}\label{sec:theorems}

\begin{theorem}[The formal-Darboux trace-sector stalk; proved]
\label{thm:stalk}
Under the coordinate tier \textup{(exact continuous duality, perfect
residue pairing, continuity of bracket and coadjoint action)}, the
continuous local-observable algebra of the formal current stalk is
$C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}(\fg)$; the closed-to-open boundary
value at finite $N$ is
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f$, $u_f\mapsto J(f)\cdot(-)$ with
$\theta_f(\phi_1)=-\partial_{z_2}f$,
$\theta_f(\phi_2)=\partial_{z_1}f$; and after scalar reduction,
stable trace passage and the Roos convention this assignment is a
dg-commutative isomorphism
\[
C^\bullet_{\CE,\mathrm{cont}}(\fg)
\;\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\;
\bigl(\PV_{\mathrm{adm}}(A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}),d_\pi\bigr)
\;\xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{HKR}^{\mathrm{cont}}_{\mathrm{adm}}\ }\;
\HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm,HKR}}
(A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H},A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}).
\]
Under the kernel tier \textup{(the weighted Casimir and K\"othe
seminorm estimates)} the arity-two singular product holds:
$\mathsf J_f(z)\,\mathsf J_g(w)\sim
K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)\,\bigl(\mathsf J_{\{f,g\}}(w)
+\hbW N[\{f,g\}]_0\,\mathbf 1\bigr)$. The centre tier and the quantum
tier are extra data, not consequences. The target is the admissible
stable-trace Hochschild model --- explicitly \emph{not} identified
with the chiral Hochschild complex of the curve volumes.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Uniqueness and rigidity of the trace coordinate;
proved]\label{thm:unique}
The category of continuous maps $\widetilde J\colon\fh\to A_\partial$
satisfying stable $GL_N$-invariance in matched windows, the Dirac
constraint \textup{(descent modulo $[\phi_1,\phi_2]$ through the
explicit homotopy)}, scalar reduction, Darboux naturality under formal
symplectomorphisms, linear normalisation
$\widetilde J(z_i)=\overline{\Tr\phi_i}$, and primitive block-sum
additivity, has exactly one object:
$J(f)=\varprojlim_W J_{\le W}(f\bmod\fm^{W+1})$. Dropping any
hypothesis admits counterexamples. The stable window contract is
quantitative: the trace identification is valid exactly for
$N\ge W$ \textup{(word degree)}, and with a length-$L$
Loday--Quillen--Tsygan primitive projection for
$N\ge\max(W,L+2)$; the first finite-$N$ residue appears at
$N=2$, degree $3$, by Cayley--Hamilton.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The brane algebra and the one-$\psi$ homology; proved]
\label{thm:brane}
The stable brane operator algebra is
$\mathbf S(\bar A\oplus\bar A_{\mathrm{desc}}[1])$ ---
Berest--Khachatryan--Ramadoss with
$\overline{HC}_0(\bC[x,y])=\bar A$,
$\overline{HC}_1\cong\bar A_{\mathrm{desc}}$, higher cyclic homology
vanishing. The new proof computes the one-$\psi$ Koszul homology
directly: $H_1$ is one-dimensional on the cyclic word class
$\Psi_{k,l}=\sum_{w}\Tr(\psi w)$, established by a cyclic-word cell
complex and an equivariant Abel map to the circle with
convex-polytope fibres; the higher-$\psi$ homology vanishes in
degrees $\ge2$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The Capelli quartet; proved]\label{thm:capelli}
The scalar class has four proved faces. Closed: $[\bar c]$ is the
extension class of $0\to\bC\cdot1\to R\to\fh\to0$. Open:
$\{\Tr\phi_1,\Tr\phi_2\}=N$, the $U(1)$ centre with charge $N$.
Moyal: the star-commutator on trace generators truncates,
$[X,Y]_\star=-\hbW N$ modulo the moment ideal --- the projective
identity, not the impossible matrix equation $[\phi_1,\phi_2]=\hbar I$.
Anomaly: $\kappa_{\mathrm{Cap}}(J)=\hbW N[\bar c]$ in continuous Lie
cohomology, with four representative constructions proved to agree.
The interpretation of this class as determinant-line or modular-line
curvature requires a line, a connection, and an Atiyah-class
comparison that are not constructed; the Quillen curvature of the
determinant line on the trace chart is a named open problem. The
$\fgl(N|N)$ supertrace kills the class --- the Costello--Li
cancellation, re-proved locally.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The obstruction calculus; proved case division]
\label{thm:obstruction}
For a transported local datum in a filtered $L_\infty$ target there
are four mechanisms --- strict coherence, counterterm coherence,
projective coherence \textup{(central inexact class, solved in a
central extension: the Capelli case)}, and target replacement --- a
case division, promoted to a partition only after the
comparison-unifying datum is supplied. Six native obstruction classes
are typed, two of them proved nonzero: the Capelli class, and the
pro-Matlis window leak $z_1^{N+2}\cdot\rho_{N+1,0}=-(N+2)\rho_{0,1}$,
which survives every finite Matlis cutoff and forces the pro-object
replacement tower. A third structural obstruction: $\fh$ contains
$\fsl_2$ with $[\fsl_2,\fsl_2]=\fsl_2$, so lower-central pronilpotence
fails on all of $\fh$ --- the bar--cobar route requires the cubic
nilpotent truncation or a relative model.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The Tate tower; proved on its stated windows]
\label{thm:tate}
The weighted envelope $B_w$ is the minimal admissible completion
closing the BV calculus \textup{(with heat-kernel propagator
$P^w_{\epsilon,L}$ and $[Q,P^w_{\epsilon,L}]=K^w_\epsilon-K^w_L$)};
the cubic-ideal nilpotent truncation is honestly Tate-pronilpotent and
carries the truncated Chevalley--Eilenberg--polyvector isomorphism;
the bar--cobar adjunction between conilpotent Tate coalgebras and
augmented Tate algebras is Quillen, a derived equivalence on the
admissible subcategory; Costello's Hadamard parametrix extends
graph-by-graph to the Mittag--Leffler limit with uniform wave-front
bounds \textup{(summability over graph topologies excluded, a separate
obstruction)}; and every object of the protected-Hamiltonian moduli
admits a formal-Darboux normal form, unique up to the Darboux torsor.
A Costello--Li-type BV-vanishing theorem for this endpoint requires
five named data, of which the brane-defect scalar contact term is
decided by one of four cancellations, including the $\bC^{N|N}$
supertrace.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The global criterion; near-definitional, stated as
such]\label{thm:global}
The open--closed comparison $\mathrm{OCA}_N$ exists at morphism stage
if and only if the ten-row typed equivalence datum exists ---
Swiss-cheese Maurer--Cartan lift, Morita centre lift, admissible
Hochschild-centre lift, flat Darboux transport, cotangent-current
realisation, Weiss--Ran descent, the order-indexed quantum
master-equation tower, anomaly and locality null-homotopies, secondary
primitives, and a contracting homotopy of the comparison cone. The
trace-sector stalk theorem contributes exactly one row. The value of
the criterion is its organisation --- the proof-order directed graph
and the separation of morphism stage from cone stage --- not an
existence claim.
\end{theorem}

\begin{conjecture}[The three compact conjectures]\label{conj:three}
\textup{(i)} The chiral $E_d$ Deligne conjecture at $d\ge2$: the
derived chiral $E_d$-centre carries a chiral $E_{d+1}$-structure ---
the single bottleneck through which every global statement funnels;
$d=1$ is the Beilinson--Drinfeld case, proved in Volume I.
\textup{(ii)} The Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds spectral sequence on
compact Calabi--Yau threefolds, whose graded at $K3\times E$ contains
the five-tuple $(0,0,3,5,24)$. \textup{(iii)} The operator modular
lift: an operator-valued cocycle on $K3\times E$ whose protected trace
is $\Phiten$, the genus-two weight-ten section of the Hodge
determinant line $\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}
=\det\pi_*\Omega^1$ on $\overline{\mathcal A}_2$, with
$Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{BPS}}=\Phiten^{-1}$ and
$\Dfive=\Phiten^{1/2}$ on the paramodular branch.
\end{conjecture}

The frontier volume attached to the manuscript keeps the enumerative
world at arm's length and correctly attributed: the quintic
Donaldson--Thomas/stable-pairs factorisation
$Z_{\mathrm{DT}}=M(-q)^{\chi}Z_{\mathrm{PT}}$ with
$\chi(Q)=-200$ and $M$ the MacMahon function is an external theorem;
the Gopakumar--Vafa expansion with integral $n^g_\beta$ is a

\section{The garden}\label{sec:garden}

One logarithmic $1$-form generates the programme. On a smooth complex
curve $X$, the form $\eta=d\log(z_1-z_2)$ is the unique
coordinate-free $1$-form with a residue along the diagonal of
$\Conf_2(X)$; the Arnold relation generates all relations among its
higher companions. From $\eta$ one builds, for an augmented chiral
algebra $\cA$ on $X$, the ordered bar complex
$\barB^{\mathrm{ord}}_X(\cA)=T^c(s^{-1}\bar\cA)\otimes
\Omega^\bullet_{\log}(\FM_\bullet(X))$; its differential squares to
zero exactly when $\cA$ satisfies the Borcherds identity. Assembled
over $\Mbar_{g,n}$, the total differential is a Maurer--Cartan element
$\Theta_\cA=D_\cA-d_0$; genus~$\ge1$ flatness fails by the curvature
$d^2_{\mathrm{fib}}=\kappa(\cA)\,\omega_g$, with
$\kappa(\Vir_c)=c/2$, $\kappa(V_k(\fg))=\dim\fg\,(k+h^\vee)/2h^\vee$
the central charge read as modular curvature; the Verdier--Koszul dual
obeys the conductor laws $c(\cA)+c(\cA^!)=K$ with $K(\Vir)=26$,
$K(\cW_N)=4N^3-2N-2$, $K(V_k(\fg))=2\dim\fg$.

Every object sits on one chain: primitive factorization category
$\to$ chart algebra $\to$ bar shadow $\to$ derived chiral centre
\textup{(the bulk)} $\to$ acting line operators $\to$ scalar modular
shadow. Identifying stages without the licensing datum --- shadow $=$
object --- is the master error; this volume is the chain's local
microscope, and its seventeen-site catalogue of exactly such
collapses, each with its corrected form, is the sharpest statement of
the discipline anywhere in the programme.

The five gardens are five readings of the chain. \emph{Volume I}
(\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar}) builds the engine on algebraic curves ---
in particular the $d=1$ chiral Deligne input that this volume's
central conjecture generalises. \emph{Volume II}
(\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2}) reads the bar complex as BV/BRST
physics on $\bC\times\bR$: its curve--line theorems apply here only
after the named restriction datum, since the native geometry is
$\bR^2\times\bC^2$. \emph{Volume III}
(\texttt{calabi-yau-quantum-groups}) is the compact target world ---
its two-stage functor and Hall doubles are exactly what the typed
firewalls of this volume keep at criterion distance until the compact
data exist. \emph{This volume} computes the local germ: the trace
coordinate at $N$ Dirac branes, its uniqueness, its anomaly, and the
exact list of data separating germ from globe --- including the
holomorphic de Rham obstruction $H^1(K3\times E,\cO)\neq0$ that
blocks the naive local-to-global route. \emph{The Igusa cusp form}
(\texttt{igusa-cusp-form}) is the terminal scalar this volume's third
conjecture points at: $\Dfive$ as the genus-two section of the
modular line whose operator lift is the shared frontier.

The single question underneath: characterise the modular convolution
algebra $\fg^{\mathrm{mod}}_\cA$ as a geometric object. The proposed
answer --- endomorphisms of a Lagrangian in a $(-2)$-shifted
symplectic stack of vacua --- has Volume I computing its Taylor jets,
Volume II its local composition law, Volume III its geometric source,
and this volume its formal-local microscope: the one stalk where
every structure is computed to the end, so that the global
obstructions can be named rather than guessed.

\section{The frontier}\label{sec:frontier}

In order of depth:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The chiral $E_d$ Deligne conjecture at $d\ge2$ --- the single
bottleneck; all ten rows of the global datum presuppose it.
\item The all-order quantum master-equation tower for the
brane-defect coupling, with the anomaly and locality null-homotopies
--- the quantum tier the stalk theorem deliberately excludes.
\item The determinant-line problem: construct the line, connection
and Atiyah comparison that read the Capelli class $\hbW N[\bar c]$ as
curvature; then the Quillen curvature of the trace chart.
\item The operator lift of $\Phiten$ --- shared with the Igusa volume
and Volume II's gravity-line construction problem; the modular line
$\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}$ is defined, its operator-valued cocycle is
not.
\item The Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande centre-lift: the
correspondence as a canonical $E_2$-centre automorphism, with
cohomological-Hall orientation data as prerequisite.
\end{enumerate}

What stands unconditionally: the stalk isomorphism with its
uniqueness axioms; the brane algebra and one-$\psi$ homology; the
Capelli quartet with its four faces and its supertrace cancellation;
the weighted-Tate completion theory with its proved non-objects and
window leaks; and the typed ledger itself --- the volume's real
invention, which converts ``local implies global'' from a slogan into
a finite list of named data.
