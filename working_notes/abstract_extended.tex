\begin{abstract}

\noindent
The Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa B-model topological string
\cite{bcov} is the B-twist of the \(\mathcal N=(2,2)\) worldsheet
sigma model on a Calabi--Yau target.  In the Costello--Li BV /
factorization renormalisation
\cites{costello-renormalization}{costello-li-quantum-bcov}{costello-li-open-closed-bcov},
the Costello holomorphic--topological sector
\cite{costello-twistedM} localises the theory on
\(X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\), topological on
\(\R^2\) and holomorphic on \(\C^2\).  At a formal
holomorphic-symplectic Dirac brane vacuum, the retained closed-string
sector is the holomorphic-topological Hamiltonian BF truncation whose
fields are
$\mathcal A\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h$ valued in the
reduced Hamiltonian Lie algebra
$\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1$ of the formal symplectic
polydisk, antifields $\mathcal B$ valued in the
Grothendieck--Matlis residue dual
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=
H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2$ paired by
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0 f\omega$, and master action
\[
  S=\int_X\bigl\langle\mathcal B,\,
        (d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
        +\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\bigr\rangle.
\]
The open sector is a stack of $N$ Dirac branes on the worldline
$\R_t\subset X$ with matrix transverse position
$(\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N$,
Chan--Paton substitution $z_i\mapsto\phi_i$ subject to the
moment-map constraint $[\phi_1,\phi_2]=0$, and antifield
$\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$ with $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$ giving the
Koszul resolution of the derived zero fibre.

\medskip\noindent
At a brane vacuum $b\in\C^2$ the formal Darboux chart is
$A_b^{\mathrm{coord}}=\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\C^2,b}\cong
\C[[z_1,z_2]]$ with symplectic form $\omega_b=dz_1\wedge dz_2$; the
classical open-string algebra of observables on $\R_t$ is the
Chevalley--Eilenberg cochain complex
of the Chan--Paton gauge algebra with values in the symmetric
algebra of the matrix coordinates, the antifield, and the
moment-map antifield,
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
   =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}\bigl(\mathfrak{gl}_N,\,
       \mathrm{Sym}(\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
                    \oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N[1])\bigr),
\]
with Koszul differential $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$.  A closed
Hamiltonian $f\in\mathfrak h$ acts on this brane sector through
the trace map
\[
  J\colon\mathfrak h\longrightarrow
       \mathcal O\bigl(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)\bigr)^{GL_N},
  \qquad
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)},
\]
the single-trace \(GL_N\)-invariant selected by cyclic trace, Dirac
descent, Darboux naturality, and
\(J(z_i)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,\phi_i}\).

\medskip\noindent
The closed observables form the mixed-HT factorization algebra
$\mathcal F^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}$ valued in CE chains of
the shifted-cotangent BF Lie algebra
$\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$;
its pushforward along $\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}
\to\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$ is a holomorphic factorization algebra
$\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ on $\C^2$ valued in
$E_2$-algebras.  It is the two-complex-dimensional holomorphic
factorization algebra of Hamiltonian currents.  Its singular product
is the two-variable diagonal expansion on
$\Delta\subset\C^2_z\times\C^2_w$: for $x\in\mathfrak g$ the
linear holomorphic current $\mathsf J_x(z)$ in the CE
factorization algebra
$B\mapsto C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}
(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g)$ satisfies
\[
  \mathsf J_x(z)\mathsf J_y(w)
  \sim K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)\,\mathsf J_{[x,y]_{\mathfrak g}}(w),
  \qquad
  K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)
   =\frac{d\zeta_1\,d\zeta_2}{\zeta_1\zeta_2},
  \quad \zeta_i=z_i-w_i,
\]
with the diagonal local-cohomology principal-part module
$H^2_\Delta(\C[[z_1,z_2,w_1,w_2]])\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2$ as its
singular coefficient module.  A curve chiral algebra is obtained from
this surface factorization algebra after choosing a curve
$C\hookrightarrow\C^2$, a pushed-forward bracket, the induced BV
pairing, the image of the brane, and the anomaly normalisation; under
these data the diagonal principal part restricts to the one-dimensional
curve Cauchy kernel
$K^{(1)}_C(z,w)=dz/(z-w)$.

\medskip\noindent
The Mixed Holomorphic--Topological Deligne conjecture asks for a
typed open--closed quasi-isomorphism from
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\) to the mixed-HT chiral
derived \(E_2\)-centre of the primitive boundary open factorisation
category \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\) on the
Costello--Gwilliam brane-link boundary \(\partial X\) of the Dirac
brane defect \(L=\R_t\times\{b\}\subset X\).  This is the
two-complex-dimensional (\(d=2\)) extension of the \(d=1\) chiral
Deligne theorem for chiral algebras on curves in the
Beilinson--Drinfeld formalism
\cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}*{\S 4}.  At \(N\) Dirac branes the
formal-Darboux fibre of this conjecture contains the finite-\(N\)
trace-sector generator boundary value
\[
  \Phi_N^{\mathrm{gen}}\in
  \operatorname{Hom}^{\mathrm{gr}}_\C\!\left(
  \operatorname{Gen}_{\mathrm{CE}}
  \bigl(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak g)\bigr),
  HH^\bullet\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
                 A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)
  \right),
  \qquad
  \Phi_N^{\mathrm{gen}}(c_f)=\theta_f,\quad
  \Phi_N^{\mathrm{gen}}(u_f)=J(f).
\]
After stable scalar reduction and admissible completion the resulting
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{coord}}^{\mathrm{st}}\) is the CE/PV coordinate
isomorphism on the trace algebra.  The full
derived-centre quasi-isomorphism and the \(E_2\)-algebra identification
require the typed ledger: Swiss-cheese action tower, Hochschild-centre
lift, finite-\(N\) trace-relation control, flat Darboux-torsor
transport, unreduced cotangent-current realization, descent, QME data,
anomaly/locality primitives, and cone contraction.  The Capelli class
\(\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}N[\bar c]\) is the projective
Lie anomaly of the trace-sector action.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian line $\C\cdot 1\subset\C[z_1,z_2]$ lies
in the kernel of the closed Hamiltonian Lie algebra reduction but
acts on the brane as $c\mapsto c\cdot I$, producing the finite-$N$
central term.  With $\Phi_{\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}}\colon\C[z_1,z_2]\to M_N(\C)$ the
Weyl-symmetric quantisation map and $\star$ the Moyal star-product,
this term has three equivalent expressions:
\[
  \omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0,\qquad
  \{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N,\qquad
  [\Phi_{\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}}(z_1),\Phi_{\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}}(z_2)]_\star
    =\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}N\cdot I,
\]
the Lie 2-cocycle on $\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1$, the Poisson
bracket of trace coordinates, and the Moyal commutator in
$M_N(\C)$.  They realise one class
$\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}]]$, the
Capelli scalar of the trace centre-of-mass.  If a determinant line
with connection exists and its Atiyah class pulls back to
$[\bar c]$, its curvature evaluates at the trace generator as
\[
  [\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}]=\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}N[\bar c],
\]
in the sense of \cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}*{\S 2.5}.

\medskip\noindent
The local model is two-complex-dimensional and is not itself a vertex
algebra.  Under a separate curve-restriction datum
\(\mathfrak R_L\) with functor \(\mathsf R_{L,\mathcal B_\perp}\), it has a
residue-pairing comparison with the Heisenberg vertex algebra: the
Kac--Moody central-pairing module $\Omega_K$ of a curve $K$ is replaced by the Matlis
principal-part module
$\mathcal P=\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=
H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2$, regular functions on $K$
by $\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1$, and the curve residue
$\Res_p$ by the two-variable Grothendieck residue
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0 f\omega$, with central cocycle the
Capelli scalar $\hbar_{\mathrm{W}}N[\bar c]$.  It is a formal local
Hamiltonian-BF sector.  Comparison with Costello's twisted M-theory
\cite{costello-twistedM} requires an explicit reduction and anomaly
matching.  On the cyclic framed stable branch
$\C[\phi_1,\phi_2]\cdot v=\C^N$ the formal-Darboux chart recovers
the Nakajima ADHM presentation of $\mathrm{Hilb}^N(\C^2)$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:cyclic-framing-hilbert-branch}); the Moyal/Weyl
deformation of the brane Poisson algebra restricts to the
Nekrasov--Schwarz noncommutative Hilbert scheme
\cite{nekrasov-schwarz-noncommutative} only after the cyclic framing,
ADHM stability, and the projective central extension are included.

\medskip\noindent
The formal-Darboux stalk theorem supplies the trace-sector fibre for a
global open--closed comparison datum on the Costello--Gwilliam
brane-link boundary \(\partial X\).  The global comparison is the typed
open--closed Deligne problem: Swiss-cheese action tower,
Hochschild-centre lift, finite-\(N\) trace-relation control, flat
Darboux-torsor transport, unreduced cotangent-current realization,
descent, QME data, anomaly/locality primitives, and cone contraction.
Its global obstruction shadow has five displayed components,
\[
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global},5}=
  \bigl(
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}},
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial},
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}order}},
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}},
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}
  \bigr)
\]
where \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}\)
is the all-arity chiral \(E_2\)-operadic extension of the arity-two
diagonal kernel \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\);
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}\) is the Čech class in
the Weiss descent obstruction complex on \(\partial X\);
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}order}}$ is the
all-order quantum master-equation class of the closed-to-open coupling;
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}}\) is the operadic anomaly class; and
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}\) is the Costello--Gwilliam
\v{C}ech--Weiss--Ran locality class.
This tuple becomes one curvature class only after a common
deformation complex and comparison maps have been constructed.

\medskip\noindent
Two compact comparison problems lie beyond the formal-Darboux theorem
and are governed by the class
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{comparison}}\): the operator-level lift of the
modular line on $K3\times E$ --- whose scalar shadow is the Igusa cusp
form \(\Phi_{10}\) with Borcherds square root \(\Delta_5=\Phi_{10}^{1/2}\),
carrying the class \(\operatorname{Ob}^V_{\mathrm{mod}}\) in the
operator-valued cocycle complex for \(\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}\) ---
and the
Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds comparison on compact non-toric Calabi--Yau
threefolds.  In either case the local theorem contributes only the
formal trace-sector fibre of a separate matched-conventions theorem.

\end{abstract}
