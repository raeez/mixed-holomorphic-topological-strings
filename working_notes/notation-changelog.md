## Current Draft

- The open primitive is \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\).  A
  chosen boundary vacuum \(b\) gives the algebra
  \(A_b=\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial}(b)\).
- The formal coordinate algebra at the brane is
  \(A_b^{\mathrm{coord}}=\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\mathbb C^2,b}\).
  It is not the primitive open object.
- The local boundary algebra at \(N\) Dirac branes is
  \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\), modeled by the
  Chevalley--Eilenberg/Koszul algebra of the derived commuting stack.
- The closed Hamiltonian algebra is
  \(\mathfrak h=\mathbb C[[z_1,z_2]]/\mathbb C\cdot 1\), with
  cotangent module \(P=\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\).
- The CE/PV generator assignment is
  \(c_f\mapsto\theta_f\), \(u_f\mapsto J(f)\), with
  \(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\).
- The stable scalar-reduced trace target is
  \(A_{\partial,H}^{\mathrm{st}}\), with finite-rank representatives
  used only inside the declared stable windows.
- The Weyl/Moyal deformation parameter is \(\hbar_{\mathrm W}\).  The
  Costello quantum master equation parameter is
  \(\hbar_{\mathrm{QME}}\).  The string coupling \(g_s\) is not used
  for these two local deformation parameters.
- The Capelli scalar is the projective anomaly
  \(\hbar_{\mathrm W}N[\bar c]\).  A determinant-line or modular-line
  interpretation requires the line, connection, and Atiyah-class
  pullback as additional data.
- The local theorem identifies the trace-sector formal-Darboux stalk.
  The global mixed-HT Deligne quasi-isomorphism is a supplied-data
  criterion with obstruction and cone-contraction rows.
