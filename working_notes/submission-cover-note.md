## arXiv Abstract

We prove a formal local trace-sector theorem for the Hamiltonian BF
sector of the Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa B-model topological
string at \(N\) Dirac branes on
\(\mathbb R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\mathbb C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\).  The
closed coefficient Lie algebra is
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\),
\(\mathfrak h=\mathbb C[[z_1,z_2]]/\mathbb C\cdot1\), and the brane
chart is the derived commuting stack with Koszul differential
\(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\).  After scalar reduction, stable
Procesi--Razmyslov trace passage, and the admissible completion, the
boundary assignment
\[
  c_f\mapsto\theta_f,\qquad u_f\mapsto
  \overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}
\]
is the continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg/polyvector coordinate
isomorphism for the scalar-reduced stable formal-Darboux trace sector.
We also identify the finite-rank Capelli scalar
\(\hbar_{\mathrm W}N[\bar c]\) as the projective anomaly at the trace
generator.  The full fixed-\(N\) Hochschild-centre complex, the global mixed-HT
Deligne quasi-isomorphism, one-dimensional curve vertex-algebra
restrictions with datum \(\mathfrak R_L\), compact Calabi--Yau
comparisons, and Igusa/Hall/Borcherds operator lifts are separated as
supplied-data criteria or matched-conventions problems.

## Exact Contribution

The manuscript proves the trace-sector formal-Darboux stalk theorem for
the mixed holomorphic-topological Deligne problem at \(N\) Dirac branes
on \(\mathbb R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\mathbb C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\).
The local closed sector is the Hamiltonian BF shifted-cotangent Lie
algebra
\[
  \mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
  \qquad
  \mathfrak h=\mathbb C[[z_1,z_2]]/\mathbb C\cdot1.
\]
The finite-rank brane chart is the derived commuting stack with Koszul
differential \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\).  The trace coordinate is
\[
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}.
\]
After scalar reduction, degreewise stable trace passage, and the
admissible Roos/strict-limit completion, the CE/PV generator assignment
\[
  c_f\mapsto\theta_f,\qquad u_f\mapsto J(f)
\]
becomes the completed trace-sector coordinate isomorphism from
continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains to the admissible
polyvector/Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg trace-sector model.

The manuscript also identifies the rank-\(N\) Capelli scalar
\(\hbar_{\mathrm W}N[\bar c]\) as the projective anomaly at the trace
generator.  It is stated as determinant-line or modular-line curvature
only under the additional line, connection, and Atiyah-class pullback
data.

## Limitations To State Explicitly

The theorem is local and formal-Darboux.  The full fixed-\(N\)
Hochschild centre requires finite trace relations, quotient-stack
stabilizer cohomology, Koszul residue, and centrality homotopies outside
the stable trace coordinate.

The finite-\(N\) generator assignment is not by itself a Hochschild
cochain map.  A Hochschild-centre cochain map requires a
Maurer--Cartan lift in the completed centrality convolution algebra and
compatible centrality primitives.

The paper does not prove the global mixed holomorphic-topological
Deligne quasi-isomorphism.  That statement is given as an explicit
criterion with all-arity centre action, descent, QME counterterm,
anomaly, locality, and comparison-cone data.

Curve vertex-algebra reductions require a separate restriction datum
\(\mathfrak R_L\) with functor \(\mathsf R_{L,\mathcal B_\perp}\).
Compact Calabi--Yau, Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds, Igusa, and
operator-level modular lifts are matched-conventions comparison
problems, not consequences of the formal local theorem.

## Suggested Cover Paragraph

This submission proves a formal local theorem in mixed
holomorphic-topological string field theory: the trace-sector
formal-Darboux stalk of the mixed-HT Deligne problem at \(N\) Dirac
branes on
\(\mathbb R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\mathbb C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\).  The
proof identifies the Hamiltonian BF closed CE algebra with the
scalar-reduced stable CE/PV trace coordinate on the derived commuting
brane stack, and isolates the rank-\(N\) Capelli scalar as the
projective anomaly.  The paper deliberately separates this local
coordinate theorem from the full finite-\(N\) Hochschild-centre complex, the
global mixed-HT Deligne quasi-isomorphism, curve restrictions, and
compact-target comparison problems; those appear only as explicit
criteria or conjectural matched-conventions targets.
