\section{Compact comparison obstructions}\label{sec:compact-comparison-obstructions-notes}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} supplies the trace-sector
formal-Darboux fibre for a global open--closed comparison datum.  Such
a datum has target
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}\!
  (\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial)
\]
and is governed by Theorem~\ref{thm:main-global-deligne-criterion}.
Its native obstruction contains the five components
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global},5}=
\bigl(
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}},
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial},
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}order}},
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{anom}},
\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{loc}}
\bigr)$, with
an additional modular-line component $\operatorname{Ob}^V_{\mathrm{mod}}$
for the operator-level lift on $K3\times E$.  Four open problems
record these components: the chiral $E_d$ Deligne theorem at $d\geq 2$
(\S\ref{ssec:open-chiral-Ed-Deligne}), which contains
$\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}$; the global
descent and all-order QME problem for the mixed-HT target, whose classes
are \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}\) and
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}order}}\), together
with the anomaly and locality classes; the
Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds filtered comparison complex on compact
non-toric CY$_3$ (\S\ref{ssec:open-hall-drinfeld-borcherds}), which
requires compact comparison data not contained in the local theorem;
the operator-level lift
of the modular line on $K3\times E$
(\S\ref{ssec:open-modular-lift}), which contains
$\operatorname{Ob}^V_{\mathrm{mod}}$.  Three comparison loci anchor them:
MNOP / framing on $S^3$ and Abel--Jacobi growth
(\S\ref{ssec:comparison-mnop-framing}); the
Hall--Drinfeld realisation on compact CY$_3$
(\S\ref{ssec:comparison-hall-drinfeld}); the operator-level lift of
$\Phi_{10}$ (\S\ref{ssec:comparison-phi10-operator}).  The
cross-target obstruction class
$\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{comparison}}(\mathfrak T)$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-target-restriction},
Theorem~\ref{thm:matched-conventions-transfer}) is the obstruction
each problem encounters in transit to a compact target.  The compact
Calabi--Yau comparison data used below are the quintic DT/PT
factorisation of Theorem~\ref{thm:mnop-quintic}, the \(S^3\)-framing
torsor of Proposition~\ref{prop:s3-framing-quintic}, the Abel--Jacobi
growth calculation of Proposition~\ref{prop:chiral-volume-quintic}, the
\(K3\times E\) Fourier--Mukai comparison of
Theorem~\ref{thm:fm-self-duality-k3e}, and the BCOV non-perturbative
closure datum of \S\ref{sec:bcov-np-closure}.

\subsection{Open problem: chiral
  \texorpdfstring{$E_d$}{Ed} Deligne conjecture at
  \texorpdfstring{$d\geq 2$}{d>=2}}\label{ssec:open-chiral-Ed-Deligne}

The Deligne conjecture in its $E_d$-form asserts an equivalence
\[
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_d}(A)
    \;\simeq\;
    C^\bullet_{E_d}(A,A)
\]
between the derived $E_d$-centre of an $E_d$-algebra $A$ and the
$E_d$-Hochschild cochain complex.  At $d=1$ the topological form
is Lurie \cite{lurie-ha}*{Thm.~5.3.1.14} (after Tamarkin); for a
chiral algebra on a curve \(C\), the \(d=1\) chiral form is proved in
\cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}*{\S 4}, with the chiral centre
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\simeq C^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(A,A)$
realised through the chiral envelope and chiral cobar; the
factorization-algebra reformulation is Costello--Gwilliam
\cite{costello-gwilliam}*{Vol.~I \S 5--6}.  At $d\geq 2$ the
topological $E_d$-centre is constructed by Lurie
\cite{lurie-ha}*{\S 5.3} via $E_d$-module categories; the chiral
extension requires three constructions beyond the current
literature.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{A chiral $E_d$-operad on $\C^d$.}  The little
    holomorphic disks operad $E_d^{\mathrm{ch}}$ on $\C^d$ must
    carry the holomorphic factorization structure of chiral algebras
    on curves, extended to higher-dimensional affine space.
    Costello--Gwilliam
    \cites{costello-gwilliam}{costello-gwilliam-vol2} establish
    holomorphic factorization algebras on $\C^d$ as a well-defined
    symmetric-monoidal $\infty$-category; a chiral $E_d$-operad
    action on this category, refining the topological $E_d$-action
    via right $\overline\partial$-modules, is the missing object.
  \item \emph{A centre-of-module-category construction in the
    chiral setting.}  At $d=1$ the chiral centre
    $Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)$ is the endomorphism object
    in chiral $A$-modules
    (\cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}*{\S 3.4}); at $d\geq 2$ the
    analogous construction must take a chiral $E_d$-algebra to its
    derived chiral $E_d$-centre, compatibly with factorization
    homology.
  \item \emph{Compatibility with factorization homology
    $\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}$.}  The composite
    functor $\Phi_d=\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ
    \Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d$
    (Lurie--Ayala--Francis factorization homology
    \cite{lurie-ha}*{\S 5.5}) must intertwine the chiral
    $E_d$-centre on its source with the chiral Hochschild centre
    on its target.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{conj}[Chiral $E_d$ Deligne]\label{conj:chiral-Ed-deligne}
  Let $\mathcal A$ be a chiral $E_d^{\mathrm{ch}}$-algebra on $\C^d$
  in the sense of (1).  There is a natural equivalence
  \[
    Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_d^{\mathrm{ch}}}(\mathcal A)
    \;\simeq\;
    C^\bullet_{E_d^{\mathrm{ch}}}(\mathcal A,\mathcal A)
  \]
  in chiral $E_{d+1}^{\mathrm{ch}}$-algebras, lifting the
  topological Deligne conjecture of Lurie
  \cite{lurie-ha}*{Thm.~5.3.1.14} along the chiral refinement.
\end{conj}

The $d=1$ case is proved (Beilinson--Drinfeld
\cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}; Costello--Gwilliam
\cite{costello-gwilliam}*{Vol.~I}).  At $d\geq 2$ partial results
exist for the topological $E_d$-centre
(\cite{lurie-ha}*{\S 5.3.1}) and for chiral algebras on smooth
schemes (Hennion's chiral Lie algebroids; Francis' factorisable
co-operads); the chiral $E_d$-operad on $\C^d$ at $d\geq 2$ in the
sense of~(1) is open globally.  The formal-Darboux stalk theorem of this
manuscript (Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local}), together with
Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-two-dimensional-holomorphic-ope}, gives the
arity-two \(d=2\) formal-coordinate chiral operation
\[
  H^2_\Delta(\C[[z,w]])\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2
  \otimes\mathfrak g^{\otimes2}\longrightarrow\mathfrak g,\qquad
  x\otimes y\longmapsto [x,y]_{\mathfrak g}.
\]
At the formal-Darboux stalk the theorem constructs the trace-sector map
\[
  \Phi_N:C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak g)
  \longrightarrow
  HH^\bullet(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
             A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}),
  \qquad c_f\mapsto\theta_f,\quad u_f\mapsto J(f).
\]
After stable scalar reduction and admissible completion this is the
CE/PV coordinate isomorphism on the trace algebra.  The full
derived-centre equivalence requires centrality homotopies, all-arity
chiral \(E_2\) data, QME data, and descent.
The chiral $E_2$-Deligne conjecture
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:chiral-Ed-deligne} at $d=2$) is the underlying
purely chiral statement on the bulk side; the local theorem gives the
formal-Darboux stalk calculation.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Open problem: $W_\infty[\lambda] / E_\infty$ admissible endpoint}{Open problem: W-infinity[lambda] / E-infinity admissible endpoint}}\label{ssec:open-w-infinity-admissible-endpoint}

The Hamiltonian Lie algebra
\(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1\) of the closed sector is the
formal-stalk realisation of the area-preserving Lie algebra of Hamiltonian
vector fields on \(\C^2\), the $E_\infty$ endpoint of the universal
\(W\)-algebra family \(W_\infty[\lambda]\) of
\cites{pope-romans-shen}{bakas-w-infinity}.  Under the controlled curve
reduction of Remark~\ref{rmk:native-bulk-object-c2}, the brane-side
derived \(E_1\)-Hochschild
\(HH^\bullet(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\)
restricts to a one-dimensional chiral algebra on the chosen curve
\(C\hookrightarrow\C^2\) whose large-\(N\) realisation, if it exists, is
\(W_\infty[\lambda]\) at an admissible boundary parameter.

\begin{conj}[\texorpdfstring{$W_\infty[\lambda]/E_\infty$ admissible endpoint identification under four hypotheses}{W-infinity[lambda] / E-infinity admissible endpoint identification under four hypotheses}]
\label{conj:w-infinity-admissible-endpoint}
Subject to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(H\arabic*)}]
  \item \emph{Prochazka triangular truncation
        \cite{prochazka-w-infinity}.}
        \(W_\infty[\lambda]\) admits a triangular truncation realising
        \(W_N\) as a quotient, with the truncation map compatible with
        the cyclic-trace generators \(\{O_{a,b}\}_{a+b\geq 1}\) of the
        manuscript.
  \item \emph{Creutzig--Kanade--Linshaw parafermion compatibility
        \cite{creutzig-kanade-linshaw-coset}.}
        The parafermion coset construction extends through the
        truncation, with finite-spin generators surviving the
        admissible-level reduction.
  \item \emph{Pope--Romans--Shen / Bakas finite-spin window
        \cites{pope-romans-shen}{bakas-w-infinity}.}
        The finite-spin OPE checks at spins
        \(s\leq s_{\max}\) extend to all spins inside the prescribed
        weight window.
  \item \emph{Arakawa admissible weight window
        \cite{arakawa-admissible-w}.}
        The admissible-level highest-weight representations carry a
        coherent weight window on which \(\lambda\) takes its
        \(E_\infty\) endpoint without finite-window collisions.
\end{enumerate}
Under (H1)--(H4) the curve-restricted brane sector realises the chiral
algebra \(W_\infty[\lambda]\) at an admissible endpoint
\(\lambda_*(N)\), with the area-preserving \(E_\infty\) limit recovering
the Lie algebra of Hamiltonian vector fields on \(\C^2\) as
\(N\to\infty\).
\end{conj}

The four hypotheses are independent: (H1) and (H4) control truncation
and weight-window admissibility; (H2) and (H3) extend the finite-spin
OPE checks to all spins.  This is item~(14) of the seventeen-site
catalogue; a theorem without these hypotheses requires constructions beyond
the present literature.  Curve restriction reduces the manuscript's
native two-complex-dimensional bulk to the one-dimensional curve
setting in which \(W_\infty[\lambda]\) is defined; the obstruction
class governing the restriction is the OPE-matching residue identified
in Remark~\ref{rmk:native-bulk-object-c2}.

\begin{rmk}[Base-case consistency of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:w-infinity-admissible-endpoint} at \(N=1\)]
\label{rmk:w-infinity-N1-consistency}
At \(N=1\) the matrices commute, the trace map degenerates to evaluation
\(J(z_1^az_2^b)=z_1^az_2^b\)
(Example~\ref{exa:point-evaluation-N1},
\S\ref{sssec:ex-heisenberg}), and Weyl quantisation gives the rank-one
Heisenberg relation
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar\) in
\(M_1(\C)=\C\).  The curve-restricted brane sector is therefore the
rank-one Heisenberg vertex algebra.  This is the predicted base case of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:w-infinity-admissible-endpoint}: Heisenberg lies
in the Prochazka truncation family \(W_\infty[\lambda]\) at the
endpoint \(\lambda_*(1)\), with (H2)--(H4) trivially satisfied at rank
one (no parafermion extension, single-spin window, single admissible
weight).  The content beyond the scalar table begins at \(N\geq 2\),
where the brane sector and the \(W_\infty[\lambda]\) family separate
through the higher trace generators \(J(z_1^pz_2^q)\) and the matching
requires (H1)--(H4) in full force.
\end{rmk}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Open problem: Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds filtered comparison complex on compact non-toric CY$_3$}{Open problem: Hall-Drinfeld-Borcherds filtered comparison complex on compact non-toric CY3}}\label{ssec:open-hall-drinfeld-borcherds}

On $K3\times E$ five comparison anchors carry distinct numerical
weights:
\[
  \kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}(K3\times E)=0,\qquad
  \kappa^{\mathrm{Hodge}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(K3\times E)=0,
\]
\[
  \kappa^{\mathrm{Heis}}_{\mathrm{ch}}=3,\qquad
  \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5)=5,\qquad
  \mathrm{rk}\,\widetilde H^*(K3,\Z)=24,
\]
the categorical Euler invariant
$\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}=\chi(\mathcal O_{K3\times E})$, the chiral
Hodge supertrace
$\kappa^{\mathrm{Hodge}}_{\mathrm{ch}}=\chi(\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_2(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{Hodge}}))$,
the chiral Heisenberg weight
$\kappa^{\mathrm{Heis}}_{\mathrm{ch}}$ of the boundary Heisenberg
factor, the Borcherds--Kac--Moody (BKM) denominator weight
$\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Delta_5)=5$ recording the square-root branch
$\Delta_5=\Phi_{10}^{1/2}$ in the Gritsenko paramodular row; the Igusa
cusp form $\Phi_{10}=\chi_{10}$ has weight \(10\).  The final entry is
the extended Mukai-lattice rank of $K3$, while the fibre Euler datum is
\(\chi(\mathcal O_{K3})=2\).  These numbers occupy distinct comparison
data: the compact CY-to-chiral target
matched-conventions data of \S\ref{ssec:cross-target-restriction} and
the Fourier--Mukai comparison of
Theorem~\ref{thm:fm-self-duality-k3e}.

\begin{conj}[Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds filtered comparison complex on compact non-toric
  CY$_3$]\label{conj:hall-drinfeld-borcherds-ss}
  On a compact non-toric Calabi--Yau threefold $X$ with chosen
  Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds datum of compatible conventions
  (the compact CY-to-chiral target; Appendix~\ref{app:comparison-conventions},
  Theorem~\ref{thm:matched-conventions-transfer}), one must construct a
  filtered comparison complex
  \(K^\bullet_{\mathrm{HDB}}(X)\) whose associated graded contains
  the comparison summands defined by the chosen compact-target datum.  For
  \(K3\times E\), or for a target carrying an explicitly specified
  \(K3\)-Mukai component, these include
  \[
    \bigl(\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}},\,\kappa^{\mathrm{Hodge}}_{\mathrm{ch}},\,
    \kappa^{\mathrm{Heis}}_{\mathrm{ch}},\,
    \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}},\,\kappa_{\mathrm{Mukai}}\bigr)(X)
  \]
  with differentials represented by the images of the ordered
  matched-conventions obstruction pair
  \((\operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{Igusa/BKM}},
  \operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{CY\mbox{-}ch}})\) of
  Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-target-restriction}.  Until this complex, its
  filtration, bidegrees, and abutment are defined, \((0,0,3,5,24)\)
  is a comparison table, not an \(E_2\)-page.
\end{conj}

The construction sketch on \(K3\times E\) is the Fourier--Mukai
partner datum of Theorem~\ref{thm:fm-self-duality-k3e}: under the
coprimality hypothesis~\((\star)\),
the universal sheaf gives a derived equivalence
$D^b(K3)\simeq D^b(M)$ with $M$ a $K3$ partner; the Mukai-lattice
side feeds the $\kappa_{\mathrm{cat}}=0$, fibre Euler $=2$, and
extended-rank $=24$ entries through K\"unneth.  The
Heisenberg/BKM entries require a morphism from the
chiral specialisation data and the Borcherds product data to the
defined Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds comparison complex; no such map is
constructed here.  Placing the five numbers in one filtered comparison
object requires data beyond the Fourier--Mukai theorem alone; the
spectral-sequence interpretation requires an additional filtered complex.
Convergence on a generic compact
non-toric CY$_3$ requires the Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds
construction of the compact CY-to-chiral target and the matched-conventions obstruction of
Theorem~\ref{thm:matched-conventions-transfer}.

\subsection{Open problem: operator-level lift of the modular line
  on \texorpdfstring{$K3\times E$}{K3 x E}}\label{ssec:open-modular-lift}

The Capelli scalar \(\hbar N[\bar c]\) of
\S\ref{subsec:scalar-anomaly} is the local projective Lie anomaly.
It becomes the curvature of a modular determinant line on
$\overline{\mathcal A}_g$ only after the operator-level determinant
line and its connection have been constructed.  At
$g=2$ the level-$2$ scalar section in the intended lift is the
Igusa cusp form $\Phi_{10}=\chi_{10}$, and
$\Delta_5=\Phi_{10}^{1/2}$ is its square-root branch governing the
paramodular Borcherds product.  An operator-level lift consists of
an operator on the compact-target bulk theory together with an identification of
its protected trace with this modular section.  The formal-Darboux
theorem contributes only the formal trace-sector fibre at \(b\).

\begin{conj}[Operator-level lift of $\Phi_{10}$]\label{conj:operator-modular-lift}
  Let \(L\subset K3\times E\) be a Dirac brane defect, let
  \(\partial_{\mathrm{link}}N_L\) be the boundary of a tubular
  neighbourhood of \(L\), and let \(b\) be a Dirac brane vacuum on this
  brane-link boundary.  Choose a two-complex-dimensional formal-normal
  chart and comparison maps identifying the restriction with the local
  Hamiltonian stalk.  Suppose the compact-target
  holomorphic-topological bulk theory and its boundary trace have
  been constructed.  The operator-level lift is a lift of the
  level-$2$ projective curvature
  $\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}\bigl|_{\overline{\mathcal A}_2}$ to an
  operator-valued cocycle on that bulk theory,
  $\widehat\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}^{\,(2)}$ whose protected
  scalar trace
  $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial}\bigl(\widehat\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}^{\,(2)}\bigr)$
  recovers the normalized modular section
  \(\Phi_{10}=\chi_{10}\in H^0(\overline{\mathcal A}_2,\lambda^{10})\).  The
  Hall--Borcherds comparison residual of
  Conjecture~\ref{conj:hall-drinfeld-borcherds-ss} is an obstruction class
  only after the operator-valued cocycle complex, filtration, bidegrees,
  differential, and abutment have been defined.
\end{conj}

The construction is a lift of the projective curvature
class $\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}$ from the period domain to an
operator on the compact-target bulk module.  The local class of
\S\ref{subsec:scalar-anomaly} is the formal restriction of this
problem; the modular line bundle on \(\overline{\mathcal A}_g\) is the
geometric target; the Hall--Borcherds residual becomes an obstruction
only after the comparison complex has been constructed.  The
level-$2$ scalar section in this comparison is \(\Phi_{10}\) by the
Gritsenko paramodular row and the Borcherds product realisation; the operator
lift is the remaining bulk-side obstruction.  The
cross-target obstruction theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-target-restriction}; matched-conventions
class $\operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{Igusa/BKM}}$) records the data
needed for the lift to descend from the bulk
module to a section of the modular line bundle.  $\Delta_5$ is the
chosen square-root branch on the paramodular cover; the
operator-level lift remains the chain-level obstruction.

\subsection{MNOP and framing on
  \texorpdfstring{$S^3$}{S3}}\label{ssec:comparison-mnop-framing}

On a compact Calabi--Yau threefold \(X\), Donaldson--Thomas (DT),
stable-pair Pandharipande--Thomas (PT), and Gromov--Witten (GW)
theories give scalar virtual-counting series.  The
Maulik--Nekrasov--Okounkov--Pandharipande correspondence compares these
series after the reduced DT convention, the variable change
\(-q=e^{iu}\), and the multiple-cover normalization.  A chain-level
lift requires compact comparison data
\[
  (\mathcal F_X,M_{\DT},M_{\PT},M_{\GW},
  \operatorname{Tr}_{\DT},\operatorname{Tr}_{\PT},
  \operatorname{Tr}_{\GW},\sigma_X),
\]
and no scalar MNOP statement constructs \(\sigma_X\).  The smooth
quintic carries the DT/PT factorisation by the MacMahon power off the
degree-zero locus of Theorem~\ref{thm:mnop-quintic}, by Bridgeland's
Hall-algebra theorem and the Behrend--Fantechi virtual-cycle formalism.

The formal-Darboux stalk gives the formal trace coordinate at one Dirac
brane vacuum: the identification
$J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:main-local} is the formal restriction of a compact
comparison trace only after
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{comparison}}=0\) and matched conventions have
been fixed on a brane chart of \(X\).  The global gluing
across $X$ requires a framing datum on the Lagrangian boundary;
for $L\cong S^3\hookrightarrow X$ the framing torsor is
$\pi_3(SO(3))=\Z$ by Proposition~\ref{prop:s3-framing-quintic}.  The
K\"ahler form identifies \(N_{L/X}\simeq T^*L\), hence
\(N_{L/X}\) is topologically trivial.  Its trivialisations form a
\([S^3,SO(3)]\cong\pi_3(SO(3))=\Z\)-torsor.  The Calabi--Yau volume
form does not select an integer framing; choice of integer framing class
$[f]\in\pi_3(SO(3))=\Z$ together with the Costello-compatible
BV/$A_\infty$ chain homotopy $h_f$ trivialising the framing
obstruction class $\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{frame}}(L,f)$ is the
global gluing data.  The required construction is the
matched-conventions obstruction of
Theorem~\ref{thm:matched-conventions-transfer} restricted to the
GW/DT/PT/MNOP target with $S^3$-framing datum.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Hall--Drinfeld realisation on compact CY$_3$}{Hall-Drinfeld realisation on compact CY3}}\label{ssec:comparison-hall-drinfeld}

The compact CY$_3$ comparison requires the chiral specialisation
$\Phi_d=\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ
\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d$ at $d=3$ and the completed Hall--Drinfeld
identification
$\mathcal G(X)\simeq D(Y^+(X))$ on the spherical Hall double, after
Hall pairing, completion, integral form, stable-envelope transport,
and descent.  These compact comparison data are not consequences of the
formal-Darboux stalk theorem.  The matched-conventions class
$\operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{CY\mbox{-}ch}}$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-target-restriction} records the construction
data of the compact CY$_3$ comparison; a compact theorem requires its
own comparison maps and null-homotopies.

\subsection{Operator-level lift of
  \texorpdfstring{$\Phi_{10}$}{Phi10}}\label{ssec:comparison-phi10-operator}

The Igusa cusp form $\Phi_{10}=\chi_{10}\in
S_{10}(\mathrm{Sp}_4(\Z))$ is the level-$2$ section of
$\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}$ on $\overline{\mathcal A}_2$, realised
as the Gritsenko paramodular Borcherds product, with square-root
branch $\Delta_5=\Phi_{10}^{1/2}$.  An operator-level lift on
\(K3\times E\) is an operator-valued cocycle on the
compact-target bulk module whose protected scalar trace recovers
$\Phi_{10}$ as a normalized modular section
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:operator-modular-lift}).  The cross-target
obstruction of Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-target-restriction} records the
matched-conventions class
$\operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{Igusa/BKM}}$ which must vanish for the
lift to descend; these compact comparison data are independent of the
formal-Darboux stalk theorem.
