\section{Master deformation complex of local-to-global coherence}\label{app:master-deformation-complex}

The trace substitution \(J\) produces one Maurer--Cartan datum.
Quantization, globalization, factorization-centre lift, modular
clutching, and CY-categorical specialisation are five transport
problems for that single datum into five filtered \(L_\infty\)
targets.  Each obstruction class
(\(\hbar N[\bar c]\), \(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\),
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\),
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\),
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\),
\(\operatorname{per}_X(v)\)) is the image of \(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}\)
under the corresponding curvature decomposition.

\subsection{Filtered \texorpdfstring{$L_\infty$}{Linfty} target deformation complex}\label{ssec:master-deformation-target}

The data of a target deformation problem are
\((\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^\bullet,F^\bullet,d,\ell_2,\ell_3,\dots)\):
a filtered \(L_\infty\)-algebra in cohomological degree, with
filtration
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}=F^0\supseteq F^1\supseteq F^2\supseteq\cdots\)
that is exhaustive and complete in the sense that
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\xrightarrow{\sim}\varprojlim_n
\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}/F^n\), with each \(\ell_k\) of weight at
least \(k-1\) for the filtration so that the curvature series of an
element of \(F^1\) converges in the filtered topology.

Given a degree-one element \(\Theta\in F^1\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^1\),
the curvature is the formal series
\[
  F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta)
  =d\Theta
   +\tfrac12\ell_2(\Theta,\Theta)
   +\tfrac1{3!}\ell_3(\Theta,\Theta,\Theta)+\cdots,
\]
convergent in the filtered topology.  The Maurer--Cartan equation
\(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta)=0\) cuts out the strict
\(\mathcal T\)-coherences.  Twists are filtered cochains
\(\tau\in F^1\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^1\) (or, when the necessary
extension is structural rather than first-order, central extensions of
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\) by an abelian filtered cochain
complex):
\[
  \mathrm{TwGlob}_{\mathcal T}(\Theta)
  =\bigl\{\,\tau\in F^1\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^1
   \,:\,F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta+\tau)=0
   \,\bigr\}\big/\mathrm{gauge} .
\]
A morphism of target deformation complexes
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\to\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T'}\) is a filtered
\(L_\infty\)-morphism, sending Maurer--Cartan elements to
Maurer--Cartan elements.

\subsection{The transported local datum}\label{ssec:master-deformation-local-datum}

The trace substitution \(J\) produces canonical degree-one elements
in five complexes.  In each case the construction is forced by the
brane-probe hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-local-universal-property}: the Chan--Paton
substitution \(z_i\rightsquigarrow\phi_i\), the Dirac constraint
\([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\), and the trace measurement
\(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) determine the
underlying datum; the choice of target adds the controlling
deformation algebra.

\paragraph{Global Hamiltonian descent.}  For a holomorphic-symplectic
ambient \((X,\omega)\) with a brane \(L\) and a Darboux cover
\(\{U_i\}\) symplectomorphic to \(\widehat{\C^2}_0\), the local data
are charts \(\Phi_i:U_i\to\widehat{\C^2}_0\) with local Hamiltonians
\(h_i\); the deformation complex is
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{glob}}=
\mathrm{Tot}\,C^\bullet_{\check C/\mathrm{WR}}(\{U_i\},
\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{loc}})\) -- the totalization of the
\v{C}ech--Weiss--Ran complex of local brane-probe coefficients.
The transported element is
\(\Theta_{\mathrm{glob}}=(\Theta_i,\alpha_{ij},\beta_{ijk},\dots)\),
with curvature
\(F_{\mathrm{glob}}(\Theta_{\mathrm{glob}})\) carrying the Hamiltonian
period \(\operatorname{per}_X(v)=[h_i-h_j]\in H^1(X,\C_X)\) as one
component.

\paragraph{Costello quantum master equation.}  The Costello
deformation complex is the filtered local-functional complex
\(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\) with differential
\(d_{\mathrm{QME}}=Q+\{I,-\}+\hbar\Delta\); the transported element is
the classical interaction \(I_{\mathrm{loc}}\) of the open Hamiltonian
BF theory, completed in the weighted-Tate envelope of
Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}.  After scalar-symbol
projection \(\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\), the reduced non-scalar
curvature is
\([F_{\mathrm{QME}}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})]
 =[\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]
 \in H^1(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},d_{\mathrm{QME}})\).

\paragraph{Unreduced \(P_0\)-factorization-centre lift.}  The
deformation complex is the mapping complex
\(\mathfrak J_{\mathrm{fact}}=\mathrm{RHom}(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{closed},H}\big|_L,
Z^{P_0}_{\mathrm{fact}}(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{open},N}))\),
augmented by BV/QME and stratified Weiss--\v{C}ech--Roos descent
directions.  The transported element is the reduced CE/PV cochain
morphism of Theorem~\ref{thm:reduced-ce-pv-central-operation}
restricted to the brane.  The curvature decomposes as the four-component
vector
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}=
(\mathrm{ob}^{\mathrm{prod}}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\mathrm{ob}^{P_0}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\mathrm{ob}^{\mathrm{bd}}_{\mathrm{QME}},
\delta^{\mathrm{Weiss}}_{\check C/R})\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:app-unreduced-cotangent-lift-obstruction}.

\paragraph{Decorated radial Stokes / Weyl normal form.}  The
deformation complex is the finite-dimensional radial bicomplex of
Definition~\ref{def:app-radial-bicomplex} with decoration generators
for PBW/Stokes corrections.  At each bidegree \((a,b)\) the transported
element is the radial comparison vector
\(v_{a,b}=(T_{a,b},E^+_{a,b})\); the curvature is the per-bidegree class
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=[(T_{a,b},E^+_{a,b})]\in
\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\), and the failure dual is
\((\phi,-\lambda)\in\ker B^*_{a,b}\) with
\(\lambda(E^+_{a,b})-\phi(T_{a,b})\ne0\).

\paragraph{Strict all-window Bochner--Martinelli current transfer.}
The deformation complex is the tower of finite Matlis windows
\(P_{\le N}=\bigoplus_{a+b\le N}\C\rho_{a,b}\) with action of
\(\mathfrak h\) by Hamiltonian vector fields; the transported element
is the brane current pair
\((P_{\le N},\rho_{0,1})\).  The curvature is the residue-coadjoint
leak \(z_1^{N+2}\cdot\rho_{N+1,0}=-(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\), encoded as the
obstruction \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\).

\paragraph{Modular open-closed clutching (placeholder).}  The seventh
target in this taxonomy is structurally distinct from the previous
six: its deformation complex requires data that the local manuscript
does not construct.  The intended deformation complex is the cyclic
factorization dg category
\(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\) of the brane line, equipped with cyclic
trace \(\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathcal C}\colon\mathrm{RHom}_{\mathcal C}(b,b)\to k[-d]\)
on a chosen boundary vacuum object \(b\) and modular twisting
morphism
\(\Theta_{\mathcal C}^{oc,\log}\colon
 \mathfrak C^{oc,\log}_{\mathrm{mod}}(X,D,\tau)\to
 \mathrm{End}\bigl(Z^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\mathrm{der}}(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}),
 \mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\bigr)\); the transported element would be
the local CE/PV cochain on the formal Darboux disk under the
factorization-homology pushforward
\(\int_{\Sigma_{d-1}}\colon\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\to HH_*(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}})\);
the strict modular coherence law would be
\(d\Theta_{\mathcal C}+\tfrac12[\Theta_{\mathcal C},\Theta_{\mathcal C}]=0\);
and the twisted modular law
\(d\Theta_{\mathcal C}+\tfrac12[\Theta_{\mathcal C},\Theta_{\mathcal C}]
=\Omega_{\mathrm{mod}}\) would carry the central determinant-line
twist appearing in the projective clutching law
\(\mathrm{Cl}^*\mathrm{Tr}_{g+1,n-2}
=\mathrm{contract}_{\mathcal C}(\mathrm{Tr}_{g,n})
\otimes e^{\Omega_{\mathrm{node}}}\).  Each of \(b\),
\(\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathcal C}\), \(\Theta_{\mathcal C}^{oc,\log}\), and
\(\Omega_{\mathrm{mod}}\) is external input from the companion
programme (Vol~II 3d HT bulk-boundary realisation; Vol~III
Calabi--Yau cyclic trace and CY-to-chiral specialisation).  Modular
coherence is recorded here as a target deformation problem to fix
terminology, with no obstruction class assigned locally;
cross-volume use of any modular clutching statement passes through the
matched-conventions firewall of \S\ref{app:convention-contract} and
is governed by the relevant target-specific coordinate of
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{tar}}}^{\mathrm{tar}}\).

\subsection{Twist taxonomy: three mechanisms}\label{ssec:master-deformation-twist}

A nonvanishing curvature class is repaired in exactly three
mechanically distinct ways.

\begin{description}
\item[Null-homotopy twist.]  The class is exact in the original target:
\(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta)=d_\Theta\tau\).  The corrected element
\(\Theta+\tau\) satisfies the strict Maurer--Cartan equation in the
same target.  This is the language of QME counterterms,
homotopy-centrality witnesses, decorated radial Stokes corrections,
Weiss/Ran descent homotopies, and many finite-window radial
adjustments.  The model example is the \(\theta_3\) finite-window
\(H^1\): bare \(H^1=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\ne0\) under the cleared
QME differential, but supplied-counterterm \(H^1=0\) under the unique
local counterterm \(dC=-E\), \(c=1\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-theta-three-finite-window-H1}).
\item[Structural / central twist.]  The class is closed and central:
\(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta)=\omega\), \(d\omega=0\), \(\omega\) central.
The repair is to enlarge \(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\) by a central
extension carrying \(\omega\) on the trace line.  The corrected
element satisfies the master equation in the extended target, and
\(\Theta\) becomes a section of a projective rather than a strict
structure.  The scalar anomaly
\(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\) is the
prototype: no internal counterterm cancels the ordinary
scalar-reduced Costello obstruction in the \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\)
branch, and the only admissible repair is the central extension
\(0\to\C\!\cdot\!\mathbf 1\to\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\to\bar A\to 0\)
under which the brane carries a projective Hamiltonian action
\([\Phi_\hbar(f),\Phi_\hbar(g)]_\star
 =\hbar\Phi_\hbar(\{f,g\})+\hbar N\bar c(f,g)\mathbf 1\).  The same
mechanism produces the global gerbe-valued descent of Hamiltonian
sections in the period class \(\operatorname{per}_X(v)\), where the
gerbe is the central extension of the \v{C}ech--Weiss--Ran complex by
the descent cocycle.
\item[Target-replacement twist.]  The class is neither exact nor
central: no admissible \(\tau\) lies in any extension of
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\) for which the corrected curvature is
zero.  Coherence requires a target substitution
\(\mathcal T\rightsquigarrow\mathcal T'\) along a forced
embedding.  The strict all-window Bochner--Martinelli current
transfer is the prototype: in every fixed finite Matlis window
\(\mathcal P_{\le N}\), the residue-coadjoint Jacobiator
\((N+1)(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\ne 0\) is non-absorptive in the strict
multiplication structure, so no internal counterterm restores
coherence.  The pro-Matlis tower
\(\widehat{\mathcal P}_\Pi=\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}\)
(Corollary~\ref{cor:promatlis-retract-as-supremum}) is the supremum
form selected by the leak: the modified coherence law is
\(\pi_N(f\cdot\xi)=\pi_N(f\cdot\pi_{N+r(f)}\xi)\), Hamiltonian action
is coherent only in the pro-tower, and any fixed finite current
window is a measurement truncation, not a closed dynamical
subsystem.
\end{description}

\subsection{Theorem schema: obstruction--twist dichotomy}\label{ssec:master-deformation-dichotomy}

\begin{thm}[Obstruction--twist dichotomy schema]\label{thm:obstruction-twist-dichotomy}
Let \(\mathcal T\) be a target deformation problem with filtered
\(L_\infty\) algebra
\((\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T},F^\bullet,d,\ell_k)\), and let
\(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}\in F^1\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^1\) be the
transported local brane-probe datum.  Exactly one of the
following four cases obtains.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Strict coherence.}  \(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})=0\)
in \(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^2\).  The strict
\(\mathcal T\)-coherence exists and is unique up to gauge.
\item \emph{Counterterm coherence.}  The class
\([F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})]\) is exact in
\(H^2(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T},\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})\): there exists
\(\tau\in F^1\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}^1\) with
\(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}+\tau)=0\) in
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\).  The corrected element gives a
\(\tau\)-twisted \(\mathcal T\)-coherence in the same target.
\item \emph{Projective / gerbe coherence.}  The class is central but
not exact: \([F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})]=[\omega]\) with
\(\omega\) central in \(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\).  The corrected
element gives a coherence in a central extension of
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\) by \(\omega\); the global structure is
projective rather than strict.
\item \emph{Target replacement.}  The class is neither exact nor
central in any admissible extension of \(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\).
Coherence exists after a substitution
\(\mathcal T\rightsquigarrow\mathcal T'\) supplied as external data.
\end{enumerate}
The local manuscript theorems decompose as follows.
\[
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
  \begin{array}{l|l|l}
    \text{Class} & \text{Curvature meaning} & \text{Mechanism}\\\hline
    \hbar N[\bar c]
      & \text{scalar-reduced Hamiltonian action}
      & \text{central extension, projective}\\
    \Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}
      & \text{Weyl/radial all-bidegree normal form}
      & \text{decorated PBW/Stokes counterterm}\\
    {[\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]}
      & \text{reduced non-scalar QME}
      & \text{Costello local counterterm}\\
    \operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}
      & \text{unreduced factorization-centre lift}
      & \text{derived centre, homotopy-centrality}\\
    \operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}
      & \text{strict finite-current transfer}
      & \text{target replacement, pro-Matlis}\\
    \operatorname{per}_X(v)
      & \text{global Hamiltonian descent}
      & \text{Hamiltonian gerbe, projective}\\
  \end{array}
\]
The mechanism column is read off the schema, not declared by fiat:
each entry is the corresponding clause of (1)--(4) under the
obstruction theorem of \S\ref{ssec:tate-residual-resolution} and the
appendices.
\end{thm}

The local theorem constructs \(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}\); each
obstruction class is a curvature component of a transport problem in
the sense of \S\ref{ssec:master-deformation-target}; stronger global,
quantum, modular, or CY-categorical statements live in the spaces
\(\mathrm{TwGlob}_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})\) of admissible
twists.  A nonvanishing curvature
\([F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}})]\) is corrected by exactly one
of three mechanisms: a null-homotopy twist
\(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta+\tau)=0\) inside the same target; a central
extension by the cocycle representative; or a target replacement
\(\mathcal T\rightsquigarrow\mathcal T'\) under an \(L_\infty\)-morphism
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T'}\to\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\).  Where no
mechanism applies the class is a no-go selector, and the substitution
is named as external input.
