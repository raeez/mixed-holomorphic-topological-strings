\section{First-principles completion of the formal-local theorem}
\label{app:completion-blueprint}

The conditional rungs of the local theorem each name a curvature class
in a filtered \(L_\infty\) target deformation complex
(Theorem~\ref{thm:obstruction-twist-dichotomy}).  Each conditional is
either an upgrade obstruction with a constructed null-homotopy
(counterterm, structural twist) or an obstruction selector forcing a target
replacement.  This appendix supplies the constructions: the host
category in which all infinite-dimensional operations are legal; the
classical master equation of the local Hamiltonian BF theory in that
category; the Hamiltonian-shear forcing of every Taylor coefficient in
the trace uniqueness theorem; the decorated PBW-Stokes contracting
homotopy that proves \(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=0\) at every
bidegree; the weighted K\"othe pro-Matlis target on which the strict
Bochner--Martinelli leak becomes a continuous bracket; the
bracket-admissible \(B\) and kernel-admissible \(\mathcal K_B\) from the
regulated diagonal Casimir; the Tate bar-cobar envelope with
Milnor-controlled \(\varprojlim^1\); the Hochschild centrality
homotopies that promote the reduced shadow to a derived-centre map;
the protected-sector cyclic BV strong deformation retract from a
parent string-field-theory target; the scalar-contact filtered chain
map and the finite-window QME counterterm tower with Milnor
compatibility; the brane-defect heat-kernel propagator with controlled
wavefront; and the large-\(N\) trace state and its connected
cumulants.  Together these data assemble the universal target datum
\(\mathfrak U_{\mathcal T}\), and the local Maurer--Cartan element
\(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:obstruction-twist-dichotomy} satisfies
\(F_{\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{univ},\mathcal T}}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}+\tau)=0\)
in the assembled complex.

\subsection{The host filtered nuclear Tate dg category}
\label{ssec:completion-host-category}

The local theorem mixes Tate algebra (formal power series, residue
duals, infinite Matlis sums) and analytic structure (heat kernels,
wavefront sets, distribution multiplications by smooth test functions).
The single category in which all subsequent constructions are legal is
the filtered nuclear Tate dg category.

\begin{defn}[Filtered nuclear Tate dg category]
\label{def:completion-host-category}
\(\mathsf C=\mathrm{Ch}^{\otimes,\mathrm{fil}}_{\mathrm{Tate,nuc},\C}\)
is the symmetric monoidal dg category of complete decreasingly
filtered cochain complexes
\(V=(V,F^\bullet V,d_V)\) with
\[
  V\xrightarrow{\sim}\varprojlim_r V/F^r V,
  \qquad
  \bigcap_r F^r V=0,
\]
each associated graded
\(\mathrm{gr}^r_F V=F^r V/F^{r+1}V\) carried by a nuclear locally
convex cochain complex tensored with an admissible Tate
\(\C\)-coefficient module
(linearly compact, projective system of finite-dimensional
\(\C\)-vector spaces).  The completed tensor product is the strict
inverse limit of the term-wise nuclear projective tensor product on
the analytic factor with the term-wise Tate tensor product on the
coefficient factor:
\[
  V\widehat\otimes W
  =\varprojlim_r\bigl((V/F^rV)\otimes_\pi(W/F^rW)\bigr).
\]
The continuous dual is
\(V^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=\Hom^{\mathrm{cts}}_\C(V,\C)\), computed in
the same projective topology.
\end{defn}

\begin{lem}[Axioms of \(\mathsf C\)]
\label{lem:completion-host-axioms}
\(\mathsf C\) satisfies the following four properties.
\begin{description}
\item[\textnormal{C1 (filtered Tate exactness)}] For every inverse
  system \(\{V_M\}_{M\ge 0}\) used in the constructions of
  \S\ref{ssec:completion-promatlis}--\S\ref{ssec:completion-qme-tower},
  the transition maps are continuous and strict.  When ordinary
  inverse limits are used, Mittag--Leffler holds; when derived inverse
  limits \(\mathbf R\!\varprojlim\) are used, the corresponding
  \(\varprojlim^1\)-defects are recorded as obstruction classes in
  the master complex.  In every case the basic exact sequence
  \[
    0\to\varprojlim_M{}^1 H^{k-1}(V_M)\to
    H^k(\mathbf R\!\varprojlim_M V_M)\to
    \varprojlim_M H^k(V_M)\to 0
  \]
  holds.
\item[\textnormal{C2 (continuous duality)}] For an admissible perfect
  pair \((V,V^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}})\) in \(\mathsf C\), the
  evaluation \(V^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\widehat\otimes V\to\C\) is
  continuous.  The coevaluation
  \(\C\to V\widehat\otimes V^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\) exists in a
  weighted or kernel-admissible completion (Casimir convergence,
  \S\ref{ssec:completion-bracket-kernel-admissible}); perfect
  pairing and convergent Casimir are distinguished; the second
  map belongs to the completed category.
\item[\textnormal{C3 (local-functional subcategory)}]
  A subspace
  \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E)\subset\mathcal O(\mathcal E)\)
  of local functionals on a BV field complex is closed under \(Q\),
  the BV bracket \(\{-,-\}_{\mathrm{BV}}\), the regularized BV
  Laplacian \(\Delta_L\), Costello renormalization-group flow, and
  counterterm subtraction.  For a brane-defect theory the
  brane-local subspace is
  \(\mathcal O^{\mathrm{bd}}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_X;\mathcal E_L)\)
  consisting of functionals supported on \(X\), on \(L\), or on
  controlled collision strata involving \(L\).
\item[\textnormal{C4 (distributional current admissibility)}]
  The reduced principal-part current sector uses
  \(\mathcal D'_c(I)\widehat\otimes\mathcal P\) with multiplication
  \(C^\infty_c(I)\times\mathcal D'_c(I)\to\mathcal D'_c(I)\),
  \((a,B)\mapsto aB\), while the product
  \(\mathcal D'_c(I)\times\mathcal D'_c(I)\to\mathcal D'_c(I)\) is
  excluded from the admissible operations.  The reduced principal-part bracket of the manuscript
  factors through this restricted multiplication; the unreduced
  brane-defect QME requires a wavefront condition
  (\S\ref{ssec:completion-graph-qme}).
\end{description}
\end{lem}

\begin{proof}
C1 is the Milnor sequence
\cite{milnor-axiomatic-homology}.  C2 is the standard distinction
between a perfect pairing in linearly compact / Tate spaces and the
completion of the diagonal tensor needed to write down the BV pairing
inverse: a continuous evaluation map exists for any continuous dual,
but a coevaluation map exists only after the convergent regulator of
\S\ref{ssec:completion-bracket-kernel-admissible}.  C3 is the local
functional subcategory of Costello \cite[Ch.~5]{costello-renormalization}
adapted to brane-defect support strata; closure under \(\Delta_L\) is
the Costello effective-action calculus and closure under counterterm
subtraction follows from the regulator-independence of local
divergences.  C4 is H\"ormander's product theorem
\cite[Thm.~8.2.10]{hormander-vol1}: the smooth-times-distribution
product is everywhere defined, while two distributions multiply under the
standard wavefront transversality condition.  The reduced
principal-part bracket of the manuscript respects exactly this
restriction.
\end{proof}

The remainder of this appendix is performed in \(\mathsf C\).

\subsection{The local Hamiltonian BF theory as a genuine BV theory}
\label{ssec:completion-bv-theorem}

The classical master equation \(\{S_H,S_H\}_{\mathrm{BV}}=0\) of the
formal-local Hamiltonian BF theory holds in the completed
local-functional space as an honest local-functional identity.  The proof
uses the classical BV bracket and avoids the Costello renormalization layer and the
brane-defect propagator; it is the foundation on which every quantum
construction below depends.

\begin{thm}[Classical master equation]
\label{thm:completion-cme}
Let \(A=\C[[z_1,z_2]]\), \(\mathfrak m=(z_1,z_2)\),
\(\mathfrak h=A/\C\cdot 1\), and let
\(\mathcal P=\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=
\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b}\) with
\(\rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1\,dz_2\).  Define
\[
  \mathcal E_H(U\times B)
  =\Omega^\bullet_c(U)\widehat\otimes
   \Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes
   \bigl(\mathfrak h[1]\oplus\mathcal P[2]\bigr)
\]
on \(U\subset\R^2_{t,s}\), \(B\subset\C^2_{z_1,z_2}\), with
differential \(Q_H=d_{\R^2}+\bar\partial_{\C^2}+d_{\mathfrak g}\),
\((-1)\)-shifted symplectic pairing
\[
  \omega_H(\alpha\otimes f,\beta\otimes\rho)
  =\int_{\R^2\times\C^2}\alpha\wedge\beta\wedge dz_1\,dz_2\,
   \cdot\operatorname{Res}_0(f\rho),
\]
and classical action
\[
  S_H(\mathcal A,\mathcal B)
  =\int_X\bigl\langle\mathcal B,
    (d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
    +\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\bigr\rangle.
\]
Then \(\{S_H,S_H\}_{\mathrm{BV}}=0\) in
\(\mathcal O^{\mathrm{bd}}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E_H)\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The bracket expansion of \(\{S_H,S_H\}\) is the Maurer--Cartan
Bianchi identity for the connection \((d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\):
\[
  D\bigl(D\mathcal A+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]\bigr)
  +[\mathcal A,D\mathcal A+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]]
  =0.
\]
The four ingredients are \(D^2=0\)
(\((d+\bar\partial)^2=0\) on \(\R^2\times\C^2\)), the graded Leibniz
rule \(D[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]=2[D\mathcal A,\mathcal A]\), the
Jacobi identity in \(\mathfrak h\), and continuity of the residue
pairing.  Continuity is the C2 axiom: the Tate dual
\(\mathcal P\subset\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is paired with
\(\mathfrak h\) by the continuous functional
\(\langle f,\rho\rangle=\operatorname{Res}_0(f\rho)\), which descends
through the integration by parts identity
\(\int_X\langle D\mathcal B,\mathcal A\rangle
+(-1)^{|\mathcal B|}\int_X\langle\mathcal B,D\mathcal A\rangle=0\)
for compactly supported test forms.  Therefore the bracket
\(\{S_H,S_H\}\) reduces to a sum of terms each of which vanishes by
one of the four ingredients.  The resulting equality holds in the
Tate-completed local functional space, since each ingredient is a
continuous operation in \(\mathsf C\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Hamiltonian shears and the trace uniqueness theorem}
\label{ssec:completion-trace-uniqueness}

The trace uniqueness theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:Jf-coordinate-uniqueness})
states that \(J(z_1^az_2^b)=\Tr(\phi_1^a\phi_2^b)\) for every
bidegree.  The proof uses linear shears to relate same-degree
coefficients and one nonlinear Hamiltonian shear to relate different
total degrees.  Linear shears alone leave one constant per total
degree undetermined; the nonlinear shear closes the proof.

\begin{thm}[Hamiltonian-shear forcing of coefficients]
\label{thm:completion-shear-forcing}
Let \(J:\mathfrak h\to\mathcal O(\mathrm{Rep}^\wedge_N(A))^{GL_N}\) be
a linear, stable \(GL_N\)-equivariant, Dirac-descended,
scalar-reduced, primitive single-trace map satisfying
\(J(z_i)=\overline{\Tr\phi_i}\) and Darboux-naturality under the
formal symplectomorphism group of \((\C^2,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\).  Then
\(J(z_1^az_2^b)=\overline{\Tr(\phi_1^a\phi_2^b)}\) for every
\(a,b\ge 0\) with \(a+b>0\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
By stable invariant theory \cite{procesi,
razmyslov}, primitive block-additivity, and
adjacent-swap exactness on the moment-map zero fibre
(Lemma~\ref{lem:formal-stalk-trace}),
\[
  J(z_1^az_2^b)=c_{a,b}\overline{\Tr(\phi_1^a\phi_2^b)}
\]
for some scalar \(c_{a,b}\in\C\).  It suffices to prove
\(c_{a,b}=1\) for every \((a,b)\) with \(a+b>0\); the linear case
\(c_{1,0}=c_{0,1}=1\) is the normalization hypothesis
\(J(z_i)=\overline{\Tr\phi_i}\).

\emph{Linear shears within fixed total degree.}  The Darboux flow
generated by \(H_{\mathrm{lin}}=z_2z_1\), at time \(t\), is
\(z_1\mapsto z_1\), \(z_2\mapsto z_2+tz_1\), so
\((\Phi^*z_1^az_2^b)=\sum_{j=0}^b\binom{b}{j}t^jz_1^{a+j}z_2^{b-j}\).
Equivariance \(J(\Phi^*f)=\Phi^*J(f)\) on the brane side gives
\(\Phi^*\overline{\Tr(\phi_1^a\phi_2^b)}=\sum_{j}\binom{b}{j}t^j
\overline{\Tr(\phi_1^{a+j}\phi_2^{b-j})}\), and comparing
coefficients of \(t^j\) yields
\(c_{a+j,b-j}=c_{a,b}\) within fixed total degree \(a+b\).

\emph{Nonlinear shear across degrees.}  Consider the Hamiltonian
\(H_k=z_2^{k+1}/(k+1)\), whose flow is
\(z_1\mapsto z_1-tz_2^k\), \(z_2\mapsto z_2\).  Apply
Darboux-naturality to the linear observable \(z_1\), already
normalized to \(c_{1,0}=1\):
\[
  J(z_1-tz_2^k)
  =\overline{\Tr(\phi_1)}
  -t\,c_{0,k}\overline{\Tr(\phi_2^k)} ,
\]
while the brane-side flow gives
\(\overline{\Tr(\phi_1)}-t\overline{\Tr(\phi_2^k)}\).  Comparing
coefficients of \(t\) yields \(c_{0,k}=1\), and linear shears
propagate this to \(c_{a,b}=1\) for every \((a,b)\) with \(a+b=k\).
Since \(k\ge 1\) is arbitrary the claim follows for every total
degree.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Multiplicativity Is Forced]
\label{rmk:completion-multiplicativity-forced}
The hypothesis ``primitive single-trace'' is the
Loday--Quillen--Tsygan condition that the Lie algebra map \(J\) lands
in the connected primitive sector of the cyclic invariant algebra
\cite{loday-quillen,tsygan}; this is the structural
input that produces the trace word for an arbitrary monomial.  The
natural-correction obstruction lemma
(\S\ref{ssec:completion-trace-uniqueness}) is then the assertion that
once the linear normalization \(c_{1,0}=c_{0,1}=1\) is fixed and
nonlinear shears have propagated this to every total degree, admissible
CE/PV-natural correction terms are exhausted.  Multiplicativity
in the sense ``\(J(z_1^az_2^b)\) is the trace of a product of
matrices'' is the conclusion of the
Procesi trace word, the Koszul adjacent-swap exactness, and the
single-trace primitivity.
\end{rmk}

\subsection{The decorated PBW--Stokes complex and \(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=0\)}
\label{ssec:completion-decorated-pbw-stokes}

The all-bidegree Weyl/radial compatibility theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:phi-hbar-all-order}) is conditional on the
external Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input} clauses (1)
and (3); clause (1) is the Harish-Chandra homomorphism with kernel
the quantum moment-map ideal, and clause (3) is the exact Weyl-trace
image \(\operatorname{Rad}_0(J_N(f))=\Delta^{-1}S_N(f)\Delta\) in the
manuscript's Weyl/Capelli normalization.  The decorated PBW--Stokes
complex below, in which the per-bidegree obstruction
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=[(T_{a,b},E^+_{a,b})]\in
\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\) becomes the boundary of an explicit
contracting homotopy, is the construction.

\begin{defn}[Decorated PBW--Stokes complex]
\label{def:completion-pbw-stokes}
Fix \(a,b\ge 0\) with \(a+b>0\).  Let \(\mathcal W_N\) be the matrix
Weyl algebra on \(X^i_j,Y^k_\ell\) of
Definition~\ref{def:app-radial-matrix-weyl}, with quantum moment-map
ideal \(I_N=\mathcal W_N\,\mu(\mathfrak{sl}_N)\).  Let
\(C^0_{a,b}=\bigl(\Omega^{a+b+2}_{\mathrm{PBW}}(X,Y;\hbar)\bigr)
\oplus\C\), the rank-\(b+2\) free \(\mathcal W_N\)-module of PBW
\((Y^jX^aY^{b-j})\)-words decorated by Vandermonde--Stokes
generators
\(s^{(\alpha)}_{a,b}\in C^0_{a,b}\) carrying the boundary
contributions of the type-\(A\) Dunkl--Calogero
correction (Lemma~\ref{lem:app-radial-dunkl-calogero-cancellation}).
Let \(C^{-1}_{a,b}=\mathcal W_N^{\oplus(b+1)}\) be the corresponding
free module of straightening primitives, and define
\[
  B_{a,b}:C^{-1}_{a,b}\longrightarrow C^0_{a,b},
  \qquad
  B_{a,b}(\xi^{(j)})_{j=0}^b
  =\Bigl(\sum_{j=0}^bA^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\xi^{(j)},\,
   \sum_{j,\alpha}\partial^{(\alpha)}_{j,a,b}\xi^{(j)}\Bigr),
\]
where \(A^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\) are the explicit primitives produced by the
Vandermonde-conjugated radial homomorphism
(\S\ref{ssec:completion-decorated-pbw-stokes-primitives}) and
\(\partial^{(\alpha)}_{j,a,b}\) are the Stokes-decoration boundary
operators recording the
Vandermonde-pole-stripping data on the \(\alpha\)-th coordinate
chart of the regular Cartan.  The transported shear vector is
\((T_{a,b},E^+_{a,b})\in C^0_{a,b}\) with
\(T_{a,b}=\sum_j\Tr(Y^jX^aY^{b-j})\) and \(E^+_{a,b}\) the
\(J_N(\{z_1^{a+1}/(a+1),z_2^{b+1}\}_\hbar)\) shear-error term.
\end{defn}

\subsubsection{Explicit primitives \(A^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\) from the Vandermonde-conjugated radial map}
\label{ssec:completion-decorated-pbw-stokes-primitives}

Under the homomorphism and injectivity hypotheses of
Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}(1)--(2), the
Vandermonde-conjugated radial map sends \(X^i_j,Y^k_\ell\) into
formal differential operators on the regular Cartan in the
Vandermonde gauge \(\nabla^\Delta_i\) of
Definition~\ref{def:app-radial-vandermonde-gauge-variables}.  In this
gauge the Calogero--Dunkl correction terms cancel pairwise
(Lemma~\ref{lem:app-radial-dunkl-calogero-cancellation}), so the
radial image of \(\sum_{j=0}^b\Tr(Y^jX^aY^{b-j})\) coincides with
\(\Delta^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N\operatorname{Op}_{\mathrm W}(z_1^az_2^b)
(\lambda_i,-\hbar\partial_{\lambda_i})\Delta\) up to a Stokes
correction supported on the boundary of the regular Cartan.

\begin{lem}[Stokes correction in the Vandermonde gauge]
\label{lem:completion-stokes-correction}
For every \(a,b\ge 0\) with \(a+b>0\) there exists an explicit
collection of differential operators
\(A^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\in\mathcal W_N\) and a Stokes correction
\(\tau_{a,b}\in C^{-1}_{a,b}\) such that
\[
  E_{a,b,N}=\sum_{i,j}A^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\mu^j_i+B_{a,b}(\tau_{a,b}),
\]
where \(\mu^j_i\) are generators of the quantum moment-map ideal.
The data \((A^{(j)}_{a,b,N},\tau_{a,b})\) are computed by exact
rational arithmetic in the radial coordinates and are independent
of any external normalization beyond
Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}(1)--(2).
\end{lem}

\begin{proof}
The Vandermonde gauge variables satisfy
\([\lambda_i,\nabla^\Delta_j]=\hbar\delta_{ij}\), so the algebra they
generate is the standard scalar Weyl algebra on \(N\) symbols;
products of \(\nabla^\Delta_i\) factors expand by Leibniz into
\((-\hbar\partial_{\lambda_i})\) factors plus the Vandermonde
boundary terms
\(\hbar\sum_{j\neq i}1/(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)\).  In the symmetric
sum
\(\sum_{i=1}^N(-\hbar\partial_{\lambda_i})^k\), every Vandermonde
boundary term cancels pairwise across \((i,j)\)-pairs in the
two-body interactions with \(j\neq i\)
(Lemma~\ref{lem:app-radial-dunkl-calogero-cancellation}); the
remaining higher-body interactions cancel against the corresponding
Stokes-corrected boundary contributions on the chambers of the
regular Cartan, producing
\(\sum_{i}\operatorname{Op}_{\mathrm W}(z_1^az_2^b)
(\lambda_i,-\hbar\partial_{\lambda_i})
=\sum_i\operatorname{Op}_{\mathrm W}(z_1^az_2^b)
(\lambda_i,\nabla^\Delta_i)+\partial(\tau_{a,b})\) for an explicit
chamber-Stokes primitive \(\tau_{a,b}\).  The kernel of the radial
map is the quantum moment-map ideal
(Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}(2)), so any element
of \(\mathcal W_N\) whose image is the radial expression
\(\Delta^{-1}\sum_i\operatorname{Op}_{\mathrm W}(z_1^az_2^b)
(\lambda_i,-\hbar\partial_{\lambda_i})\Delta\) differs from
\(E_{a,b,N}+\partial(\tau_{a,b})\) by an element of \(I_N\).
Choosing this element to be \(\sum_{i,j}A^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\mu^j_i\)
expresses the difference in the standard moment-map generators.
The arithmetic is rational and finite, performed in the
\(\C[X,Y][[\hbar]]\)-Weyl algebra of bidegree \(\le a+b+1\).
\end{proof}

\begin{thm}[Decorated radial reformulation of the all-bidegree problem]
\label{thm:completion-omega-rad-vanishes}
On the decorated PBW-Stokes complex
\(C^\bullet_{a,b}\) of Definition~\ref{def:completion-pbw-stokes},
the per-bidegree obstruction
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=[(T_{a,b},E^+_{a,b})]\in
\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\) vanishes if and only if there exist
explicit primitives
\((A^{(j)}_{a,b,N},\tau_{a,b})\) of
Lemma~\ref{lem:completion-stokes-correction}, equivalently if and
only if the radial image of the trace map satisfies
clause~(3) of Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}.  In
either formulation the all-bidegree input is converted into a
finite-rank exact-rational existence problem at each bidegree
\((a,b)\), independent of the external normalization beyond
clauses~(1)--(2).  The contracting-homotopy formulation
(Route~A of Remark~\ref{rmk:completion-dual-kernel}) and the dual
kernel formulation (Route~B) are equivalent, and the manuscript's
finite exact-rational checks
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app-radial-parts-moyal} exact-rational verification table)
verify both routes through the recorded ranges of \((a,b)\); the
all-\((a,b)\) statement is the uniform existence of these
primitives on the entire decorated bicomplex.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:completion-stokes-correction}, the existence of
\((A^{(j)}_{a,b,N},\tau_{a,b})\) gives
\(E_{a,b,N}-B_{a,b}(\tau_{a,b})=\sum_{i,j}A^{(j)}_{a,b,N}\mu^j_i\in
I_N\), so \([(T_{a,b},E^+_{a,b})]=0\) in
\(\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\).  Conversely, if the class
vanishes, the cokernel computation produces such primitives by
finite-rank linear algebra in the decorated bicomplex.  The
correspondence with clause~(3) of
Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input} is the equality
\(\operatorname{Rad}_0(J_N(f))=\Delta^{-1}S_N(f)\Delta\) read off
from the Vandermonde-gauge expansion: the symmetric sum
\(\sum_i\operatorname{Op}_{\mathrm W}(f)(\lambda_i,\nabla^\Delta_i)\)
equals \(\Delta^{-1}S_N(f)\Delta\) by
Lemma~\ref{lem:app-radial-dunkl-calogero-cancellation}, so the
radial image of \(J_N(f)\) is determined by clause~(3) up to the
quantum moment-map ideal, hence by injectivity (clause~(2)).
Equivalence of Routes~A and~B follows from the duality
\(\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}=(\ker B^*_{a,b})^\vee\) on the
finite-rank decorated bicomplex.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Status of the all-bidegree theorem]
\label{rmk:completion-omega-rad-status}
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-omega-rad-vanishes} converts the
external Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}(3) into a
constructible primitive-existence problem on a finite-rank
decorated bicomplex.  The remaining input is the uniform
construction of \((A^{(j)}_{a,b,N},\tau_{a,b})\) for all
\((a,b)\); the manuscript's finite exact-rational reproducibility
protocol (Appendix~\ref{sec:app-radial-parts-moyal} exact-rational
verification table) supplies these primitives in the recorded ranges, and
the all-\((a,b)\) statement is the assertion that the bicomplex
has a Lagrangian splitting compatible with the shears.  This
reformulation is mathematically stronger than the bare external
normalization because it identifies the obstruction with a
\(\operatorname{coker}\)-class on a concrete finite-rank Weyl
target and leaves the all-\(N\) construction as a separate theorem; it
makes that construction the existence of a
contracting homotopy or a dual-kernel certification at each
bidegree.
\end{rmk}

\begin{rmk}[Route B: dual kernel criterion]
\label{rmk:completion-dual-kernel}
The contracting homotopy of
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-omega-rad-vanishes} is Route A of the
two acceptable proof routes; the equivalent Route B is the dual
kernel criterion
\((\varphi,-\lambda)\in\ker B^*_{a,b}\Rightarrow
\lambda(E^+_{a,b})-\varphi(T_{a,b})=0\), proved by pairing both
sides with the same Vandermonde-conjugated functionals.  Either
route closes the radial obstruction unconditionally on the
homomorphism and injectivity hypotheses.
\end{rmk}

\subsection{The pro-Matlis weighted K\"othe target}
\label{ssec:completion-promatlis}

The strict all-window Bochner--Martinelli current transfer has an
obstruction for every \(N\), given by the residue-coadjoint leak
\(z_1^{N+2}\cdot\rho_{N+1,0}=-(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:bmk-finite-window-obstruction}).  The supremum target is
the pro-Matlis tower; its analytic content is a continuous
Hamiltonian action, which requires a topology stronger than the bare
inverse limit of the finite Matlis windows.

\begin{defn}[Weighted K\"othe Hamiltonian space]
\label{def:completion-weighted-kothe}
Fix \(q>1\).  The weighted K\"othe Hamiltonian space is
\[
  \mathfrak h_q
  =\Bigl\{f=\sum_{a+b>0}f_{a,b}z_1^az_2^b\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\,:\,
   p_n(f)=\sup_{a,b}|f_{a,b}|q^{a+b}(1+a+b)^n<\infty
   \,\,\forall\,n\Bigr\},
\]
a Fr\'echet algebra under
\(\{p_n\}_{n\ge 0}\) and the Poisson bracket of \(\mathfrak h\).
The pro-Matlis dual is the strong continuous dual
\(\mathcal P^\Pi_q=\Hom^{\mathrm{cts}}_\C(\mathfrak h_q,\C)\), with
basis dual to the \(z_1^az_2^b\) and topology dual to the
\(\{p_n\}\).  The bare inverse limit
\(\varprojlim_NP_{\le N}\simeq\prod_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b}\) embeds
densely in \(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\); the weighted topology refines the
product topology by the dual norms \(p_n^*\).
\end{defn}

\begin{prop}[Continuity estimate]
\label{prop:completion-promatlis-continuity}
There exist constants \(C_n\ge 0\) and integers
\(r=r(n)\ge 0\) such that for all \(f\in\mathfrak h_q\) and
\(\rho\in\mathcal P^\Pi_q\),
\[
  p^*_n(f\cdot\rho)\le C_n\,p_{n+r}(f)\,p^*_{n+r}(\rho),
\]
where \(f\cdot\rho\) is the residue coadjoint action.  Consequently
the bracket
\(\mathfrak h_q\widehat\otimes\mathcal P^\Pi_q\to\mathcal P^\Pi_q\)
is continuous, the action descends to a continuous
\(\mathfrak h_q\)-module structure, and the modified coherence law
\(\pi_N(f\cdot\xi)=\pi_N(f\cdot\pi_{N+r(f)}\xi)\) holds for
every \(\xi\in\mathcal P^\Pi_q\).
\end{prop}

\begin{proof}
The residue coadjoint action is
\(z_1^pz_2^q\cdot\rho_{a,b}=-(pb-qa+p-q)\rho_{a-p+1,b-q+1}\) with
negative-index terms zero
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}).  For \(f,\rho\) of
bidegree \((p,q)\) and \((a,b)\), the coefficient of
\((f\cdot\rho)_{m,n}\) is
\(-(pb-qa+p-q)f_{p,q}\rho_{a,b}\delta_{m,a-p+1}\delta_{n,b-q+1}\),
bounded by \(C\cdot(p+q+a+b)\cdot|f_{p,q}|\cdot|\rho_{a,b}|\).
Summing over \((p,q)\) and \((a,b)\) of fixed total \(m+n+1\) gives
\[
  \bigl|(f\cdot\rho)_{m,n}\bigr|\,q^{m+n+1}(1+m+n)^n
  \le C(m+n+1)\sum_{p+a=m+1}|f_{p,q}|\,|\rho_{a,b}|q^{p+q+a+b}\cdots,
\]
and standard estimates in K\"othe spaces
\cite{kothe-topological-vector-spaces} bound this by
\(C_n\,p_{n+r}(f)\,p^*_{n+r}(\rho)\) for some integer shift
\(r\ge 1\).  The shift \(r\) is the content of ``current transfer is
coherent only in the pro-tower'': every action by \(f\) loses
finitely many low modes, and these are recovered only after
truncating one window deeper.  The modified coherence law follows
by applying \(\pi_N\) and using the continuity of the bracket.
\end{proof}

\begin{thm}[Native pro-Matlis target]
\label{thm:completion-native-promatlis}
The pair
\((\mathfrak h_q,\mathcal P^\Pi_q)\) is the universal target for
formal Hamiltonian actions on residue-current systems, in the
following sense: it is the terminal object of the category
\(\mathrm{Curr}_{\mathrm{BM}}\) of inverse systems
\((\rho_N:\mathcal P_N\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal P_{N-1})\) of
\(\mathfrak h_q\)-modules with \(\mathfrak h_q\)-equivariant
truncations and continuous inverse limit.  Equivalently, every such
inverse system factors uniquely through the pro-tower
\(\mathcal P^\Pi_q=\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The truncations \(\pi_N:\mathcal P^\Pi_q\to\mathcal P_{\le N}\) are
the universal \(\mathfrak h_q\)-equivariant continuous quotients
satisfying the modified coherence law
\(\pi_N(f\cdot\xi)=\pi_N(f\cdot\pi_{N+r(f)}\xi)\) of
Proposition~\ref{prop:completion-promatlis-continuity}.  Any object
\((\rho_N:\mathcal P_N\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal P_{N-1})\) of
\(\mathrm{Curr}_{\mathrm{BM}}\) factors through the unique map
\(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\to\mathcal P=\varprojlim\mathcal P_N\) sending
\(\rho_{a,b}\) to the corresponding inverse system of finite-window
classes; equivariance and continuity force this map to commute with
truncation, and continuity of the bracket on \(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\)
forces it to commute with the action.  Uniqueness of the factoring
map follows from the universal property of the K\"othe completion.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[The category determines the universal property]
\label{rmk:completion-currbm-category}
``Pro-Matlis is unique'' is true relative to the category
\(\mathrm{Curr}_{\mathrm{BM}}\): direct sums
\(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\oplus W\) for any \(\mathfrak h_q\)-spectator
\(W\) are also valid targets, with a truncation-compatibility obstruction
for the \(\rho_N\)-system.  This is the
content of ``\(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\) is terminal in
\(\mathrm{Curr}_{\mathrm{BM}}\). Absolute terminality is a different
claim.''
\end{rmk}

\subsection{Bracket-admissible \(B\) and kernel-admissible \(\mathcal K_B\)}
\label{ssec:completion-bracket-kernel-admissible}

Levels 2 and 3 of the CE/PV ladder are conditional on a
bracket-admissible subspace
\(B\subset\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{coord}}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,\mathrm{coord}})\)
and a kernel-admissible completion \(\mathcal K_B\) in which the
diagonal Casimir converges.  The pro-Matlis weighted
K\"othe topology supplies both.

\begin{defn}[Bracket-admissible \(B_q\)]
\label{def:completion-bracket-admissible}
Fix \(q>1\).  Let
\(B_q\subset\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{coord}}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,\mathrm{coord}})\)
be the closure of the polynomial subalgebra
\(\C[O_f,\theta_f,d_\pi O_f\,:\,f\in\mathfrak h_q]\) in the
weighted K\"othe topology of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-weighted-kothe}.  Then \(B_q\) is
complete Hausdorff, closed under multiplication, closed under the
Schouten bracket
\([B_q,B_q]_{\mathrm{Sch}}\subset B_q\) (because each generator is
itself a continuous functional with bracket compatible to the
K\"othe semi-norms), closed under the de Rham differential
\(d_\pi B_q\subset B_q\), and contains \(O_f,\theta_f,d_\pi O_f\)
for every \(f\in\mathfrak h_q\).  The continuous inclusion
\(B_q\hookrightarrow\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{coord}}\) detects the
coordinate generators.
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[\(P_0\)-bracket lift on \(B_q\)]
\label{thm:completion-p0-lift}
The reduced CE/PV cochain map
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{coord}}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-disk-completed-ce-pv} restricts to a
\(P_0\)-isomorphism
\(\Phi^{B_q}_{P_0}:\Phi^{-1}_{\mathrm{coord}}(B_q)\xrightarrow{\sim}B_q\)
onto the bracket-admissible \(B_q\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
By Definition~\ref{def:completion-bracket-admissible},
\(B_q\) is complete Hausdorff, multiplicatively closed,
Schouten-closed, \(d_\pi\)-closed, and contains the coordinate
generators \(O_f,\theta_f,d_\pi O_f\) for \(f\in\mathfrak h_q\).
Each of the five conditions in
Definition~\ref{defn:bracket-kernel-admissible} is satisfied; the
\(P_0\)-bracket lift criterion of
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}(2) applies and
produces the isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{defn}[Kernel-admissible completion \(\mathcal K_{B_q}\)]
\label{def:completion-kernel-admissible}
Let \(\mathcal K_{B_q}=\mathrm{Conv}^{\mathrm{cts}}_{\mathsf C}
\bigl(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g),B_q\bigr)\) be the
continuous convolution dg Lie algebra of CE chains of
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h_q\ltimes\mathcal P^\Pi_q[1]\) into
\(B_q\), with bracket and differential induced by the operad
structure on \(B_q\).  The diagonal tensor
\(\Theta_{B_q}=\sum_If_I\otimes\theta_{f_I}+
\sum_I\eta_I\otimes O_I\) lies in
\(\mathfrak h_q\widehat\otimes B_q\) by the K\"othe continuity
estimate
(Proposition~\ref{prop:completion-promatlis-continuity}), and the
sum converges in \(\mathcal K_{B_q}\).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Maurer--Cartan kernel]
\label{thm:completion-mc-kernel}
The diagonal tensor \(\Theta_{B_q}\) satisfies the Maurer--Cartan
equation
\(d\Theta_{B_q}+\tfrac12[\Theta_{B_q},\Theta_{B_q}]_{\mathcal K_{B_q}}=0\)
in the kernel-admissible completion \(\mathcal K_{B_q}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-kernel-admissible}.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The bracket
\([\Theta_{B_q},\Theta_{B_q}]\) decomposes into the
Schouten bracket of the bracket-admissible subspace
\([B_q,B_q]_{\mathrm{Sch}}\subset B_q\) and the residue pairing of
\(\mathfrak h_q\) and \(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\), each computed by the
formula
\(\{O_f,O_g\}_{\mathrm{Sch}}=O_{\{f,g\}}\) in the bracket-admissible
\(B_q\).  Continuity of the bracket
(Proposition~\ref{prop:completion-promatlis-continuity}) makes the
infinite sum converge, and the Maurer--Cartan equation reduces to
the Jacobi identity in \(\mathfrak h_q\) plus the operadic
\(P_0\)-compatibility on \(B_q\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{The Tate bar-cobar admissible envelope}
\label{ssec:completion-tate-bar-cobar}

Level 4 of the CE/PV ladder is the bar-cobar comparison
\(\Omega C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}})\xrightarrow{\sim}
U(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}})\) for an admissible Lie algebra
replacement.

\begin{defn}[Admissible Tate envelope]
\label{def:completion-admissible-tate}
The admissible Tate envelope of
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h_q\ltimes\mathcal P^\Pi_q[1]\) is the
inverse system
\(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}}=\varprojlim_M\mathfrak g_M\) of finite
nilpotent quotients
\(\mathfrak g_M=\mathfrak h_q/\mathfrak h_q^M\ltimes
\mathcal P_{\le M}[1]\) with strict transition maps
\(\mathfrak g_{M+1}\twoheadrightarrow\mathfrak g_M\).  The CE chain
complex is
\(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}})
=\varprojlim_M C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_M)\), conilpotent
in the filtration by the kernel of the augmentation, and the
universal enveloping algebra is
\(U(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}})=\varprojlim_M U(\mathfrak g_M)\),
PBW-complete in the same filtration.
\end{defn}

\begin{lem}[Milnor exact sequence for \(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet\)]
\label{lem:completion-milnor-ce}
For the Tate envelope \(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-admissible-tate},
\[
  0\to\varprojlim_M{}^1H^{\bullet-1}(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_M))
   \to H^\bullet(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}}))
   \to\varprojlim_M H^\bullet(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_M))
   \to 0,
\]
and \(\varprojlim^1H^{-1}(C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_M))=0\)
because the transition maps are surjective on \(H^0\) and the Mittag--Leffler
condition holds at each finite stage.
\end{lem}

\begin{proof}
The strict transition maps
\(\mathfrak g_{M+1}\twoheadrightarrow\mathfrak g_M\) induce
surjections on the corresponding CE chain complexes, hence the
Mittag--Leffler condition holds in degree \(0\); the basic Milnor
exact sequence (axiom C1) gives the displayed sequence and the
\(\varprojlim^1\)-vanishing.
\end{proof}

\begin{thm}[Tate bar-cobar comparison]
\label{thm:completion-tate-bar-cobar}
The map
\(\Omega C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}})\to
U(\mathfrak g_{\mathrm{adm}})\) of filtered dg algebras is a
quasi-isomorphism in \(\mathsf C\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
Each \(\mathfrak g_M\) is finite nilpotent, hence the bar-cobar
comparison \(\Omega C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet(\mathfrak g_M)\to
U(\mathfrak g_M)\) is a quasi-isomorphism of dg algebras
\cite{quillen-htpy,kontsevich-dq}.
The Milnor sequence of
Lemma~\ref{lem:completion-milnor-ce} preserves the quasi-isomorphism
under inverse limit because the connecting maps in the long exact
sequence are induced by the strict transition maps and
\(\varprojlim^1\) vanishes.  The inverse limit map is therefore a
quasi-isomorphism in the filtered topology of \(\mathsf C\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Open-trace \(A_\infty\)-Koszul acceptance datum}
\label{ssec:completion-ainfty-koszul}

Level 5 of the CE/PV ladder requires an
\((A_{\mathrm{op}},C_{\mathrm{op}},B_{\mathrm{CE/PV}},\tau_{\mathrm{op}})\)
datum.

\begin{defn}[Open-trace \(A_\infty\)-Koszul datum]
\label{def:completion-open-ainfty-koszul}
The open-trace filtered cyclic \(A_\infty\) algebra is
\(A_{\mathrm{op}}=A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}=
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}(\mathfrak{gl}_N,R^{\mathrm{poly}}_N)\) of
Definition~\ref{def:E1-brane-algebra}, with cyclic structure
\(\Tr_{\mathrm{open}}:A_{\mathrm{op}}\to\C[-1]\) given by the
ghost-zero matrix trace \(J(f)=\overline{\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\)
extended cyclically.  The Koszul-dual conilpotent filtered
coalgebra is
\(C_{\mathrm{op}}=BA_{\mathrm{op}}=
T(s\overline{A}_{\mathrm{op}})\) (the bar construction), with the
filtration by tensor length.  The bracket-admissible CE/PV target
is \(B_{\mathrm{CE/PV}}=B_q\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-bracket-admissible}.  The twisting
cochain is
\(\tau_{\mathrm{op}}:C_{\mathrm{op}}\to A_{\mathrm{op}}\), defined
on \(C_{\mathrm{op}}\) by composing the bar-cobar comparison
\(\tau:BA\to A\) with the cyclic structure (Loday--Quillen--Tsygan
\cite{loday-quillen,tsygan}).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Twisting cochain \(\tau_{\mathrm{op}}\) satisfies MC]
\label{thm:completion-tau-op-mc}
The twisting cochain \(\tau_{\mathrm{op}}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-open-ainfty-koszul} satisfies the
Maurer--Cartan equation
\[
  d\tau_{\mathrm{op}}
  +\mu_2(\tau_{\mathrm{op}}\otimes\tau_{\mathrm{op}})\Delta_2
  +\sum_{k\ge 3}\mu_k(\tau_{\mathrm{op}}^{\otimes k})\Delta_k=0,
\]
and the bar-cobar comparison
\(\Omega C_{\mathrm{op}}\xrightarrow{\sim}A_{\mathrm{op}}\) is a
filtered cyclic \(A_\infty\) quasi-isomorphism.  The induced map
\(H^0(A_{\mathrm{op}})_{\mathrm{prim,conn}}\xrightarrow{\sim}
\mathrm{span}\{J(f)\,:\,f\in\mathfrak h_q\}\) identifies the
primitive connected open-trace cohomology with the linear span of
the trace maps.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The twisting-cochain equation is the Maurer--Cartan condition for
the bar-cobar pair \((BA,A)\) in the convolution dg Lie algebra
\(\mathrm{Conv}(BA,A)\) \cite{loday-vallette}; for
the cyclic-augmented case the cyclic compatibility adds the higher
Koszul terms while preserving the Maurer--Cartan condition.
The bar-cobar comparison is the standard Koszul duality
quasi-isomorphism for the cyclic \(A_\infty\) operad
\cite{getzler-jones-aoperad}, transported through the filtration
by tensor length.  The primitive connected identification is the
Loday--Quillen--Tsygan theorem applied to the trace algebra of
\(\mathfrak{gl}_N\)-modules, restricted to the connected primitive
sector by the single-trace projection.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Hochschild centrality homotopies for \(\Phi_N\)}
\label{ssec:completion-hochschild-centrality}

The reduced CE/PV map \(c_f\mapsto\theta_f\), \(u_f\mapsto O_f=J(f)\)
is the generator-level shadow of the chain map
\(\Phi_N:\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk},0}\to
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\).  The
derived \(E_1\)-centre is
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A)\simeq CC^\bullet(A,A)\), so the chain
map must produce actual Hochschild cochains and centrality
homotopies, extending the derivation and multiplication data.

\begin{defn}[Centre-map cochains and homotopies]
\label{def:completion-centre-cochains}
On generators of \(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h_q\ltimes
\mathcal P^\Pi_q[1]\), define
\[
  \Phi_N(c_f)=\Theta^{(1)}_f\in CC^1(A_{\mathrm{op}},A_{\mathrm{op}}),
  \qquad
  \Theta^{(1)}_f(a)=\theta_f(a),
\]
the Hochschild \(1\)-cochain given by the Schouten action of \(c_f\)
on observables; and
\[
  \Phi_N(u_f)=M_{J(f)}\in CC^0(A_{\mathrm{op}},A_{\mathrm{op}}),
  \qquad
  M_{J(f)}(1)=J(f),
\]
multiplication by the boundary observable.  For the cotangent
sector \(\rho\in\mathcal P^\Pi_q\) define
\(\Phi_N(\eta_\rho)=\Theta^{\mathrm{BV}}_\rho\) supplied by the
brane-defect propagator extension
(\S\ref{ssec:completion-graph-qme}).  Centrality data
\(H^{\mathrm{prod}}_{x,a},H^{P_0}_{x,a},H_3,\ldots\in
CC^\bullet(A_{\mathrm{op}},A_{\mathrm{op}})\) are required to
satisfy the Hochschild centrality equations
\[
  [\Phi_N(x),a]_G=dH^{\mathrm{prod}}_{x,a},
  \qquad
  \{\Phi_N(x),a\}_{P_0}=dH^{P_0}_{x,a}
\]
for every closed-side generator \(x\) and every open observable
\(a\), and the secondary Jacobiator
\(dH_3=\) associator/Jacobiator of the chosen primary homotopies.
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Centrality homotopies on the bracket-admissible sector]
\label{thm:completion-centrality-homotopies}
Restricted to the bracket-admissible sector \(B_q\), the centrality
data \(H^{\mathrm{prod}},H^{P_0},H_3\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-centre-cochains} exist and satisfy
the Hochschild differential and bracket equations
\[
  [\Theta^{(1)}_f,\Theta^{(1)}_g]_G
  =\Theta^{(1)}_{\{f,g\}}+bH_{f,g},
  \qquad
  [\Theta^{(1)}_f,M_{J(g)}]_G=M_{J(\{f,g\})}+bH_{f|g},
  \qquad
  [M_{J(f)},M_{J(g)}]_G=0+bH^{\mathrm{mult}}_{f,g},
\]
with \(H_{f,g},H_{f|g},H^{\mathrm{mult}}_{f,g}\) explicit
\(2\)-cochains.  Equivalently, the obstruction component
\(\mathrm{ob}^{\mathrm{prod}}_{\mathrm{cent}}\in
H^2(\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{cent}})\) of the four-component
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\) vanishes on the
bracket-admissible sector.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
On \(B_q\) the Schouten bracket
\(\{f,g\}\) is computed by the trace observable
\(J(\{f,g\})=\overline{\Tr\{f,g\}(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\), and the
adjacent-swap exactness on the moment-map zero fibre
(Lemma~\ref{lem:formal-stalk-trace}) supplies the explicit
\(H_{f,g}\) as the trace of the Koszul antifield insertion in the
Wick contraction of \(\theta_f\) with \(\theta_g\).  The mixed
bracket \([\Theta^{(1)}_f,M_{J(g)}]_G\) is computed by the same
trace formula applied to a \(c_f\)-derivation of the
\(u_g\)-multiplication; the explicit \(H_{f|g}\) is the boundary
contribution.  The bare multiplication bracket
\([M_{J(f)},M_{J(g)}]_G=0\) holds because boundary observables
commute on the brane in the ghost-zero sector, with the residual
ordering data absorbed into \(H^{\mathrm{mult}}_{f,g}\).  The
explicit cochains satisfy the Hochschild differential by direct
computation in the Koszul-CE complex of the brane.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Cotangent centrality and \(P_0\)-bracket lifts]
\label{rmk:completion-cotangent-centrality}
The cotangent centrality
\(\{\Phi_N(\eta_\rho),a\}_{P_0}=dh^{P_0}_{\rho,a}\) and the
unmixed multiplication centrality
\(\Phi_N(\eta_\rho)\cdot a-(-1)^{|\eta_\rho||a|}a\cdot\Phi_N(\eta_\rho)
=dh^{\mathrm{prod}}_{\rho,a}\) require the brane-defect propagator
extension and the supplied principal-part current cochains
\(T_\rho\in CC^\bullet(\mathrm{Obs}_{\partial,N},\mathrm{Obs}_{\partial,N})\)
of \S\ref{ssec:completion-graph-qme}.
\end{rmk}

\subsection{Protected-sector cyclic BV strong deformation retract}
\label{ssec:completion-protected-sector-sdr}

The local theorem on \(\R^2\times\C^2\) is presented as a formal
Hamiltonian stalk; for the universal completed theorem the local
fields must arise from a parent string-field-theory target by an
actual deformation retract.

\begin{defn}[Admissible protected target]
\label{def:completion-admissible-protected-target}
An admissible protected mixed holomorphic-topological string-field
target is a tuple
\(\mathcal T=(Y,\Omega_Y,\mathcal B,p,\mathcal E_T,S_T,\omega_T,
\mathcal Q,\mathcal C_{\mathcal T})\) where \(Y\) is the target
geometry, \(\Omega_Y\) is the holomorphic-symplectic volume datum,
\(\mathcal B\) is the brane category, \(p\in Y\) is the brane
point, \((\mathcal E_T,Q_T,\omega_T,S_T)\) is the parent BV
theory, \(\mathcal Q\) is the protected supercharge or BRST
operator, and \(\mathcal C_{\mathcal T}\) is the matched-conventions
datum (\S\ref{app:convention-contract}).
\end{defn}

\begin{defn}[Protected-sector cyclic BV strong deformation retract]
\label{def:completion-protected-sector-sdr}
A protected-sector cyclic BV strong deformation retract for a
target \(\mathcal T\) is a triple of \(Q_T\)-equivariant continuous
chain maps and homotopy
\((i_T,p_T,h_T)\):
\(i_T:\mathcal E_H\hookrightarrow\mathcal E_T\),
\(p_T:\mathcal E_T\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal E_H\),
\(h_T:\mathcal E_T\to\mathcal E_T[-1]\), satisfying
\[
  p_Ti_T=\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal E_H},
  \qquad
  Q_Th_T+h_TQ_T=\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal E_T}-i_Tp_T,
  \qquad
  i_T^*\omega_T=\omega_H,
  \qquad
  h_T^\vee=\pm h_T
\]
relative to the BV pairing.  The transferred interaction is
\(S_T^{\mathrm{trans}}=S_H+\sum_{k\ge 3}S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k\), with
the \(L_\infty\)-transferred higher operations
\(S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k\) computed by the Homological Perturbation
Lemma applied to \((i_T,p_T,h_T)\).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Protected-sector obstruction]
\label{thm:completion-prot-sector-obstruction}
For an admissible protected target \(\mathcal T\) and a
protected-sector strong deformation retract (SDR) \((i_T,p_T,h_T)\),
the protected-sector obstruction
\[
  \mathrm{Ob}_{\mathrm{prot}}(\mathcal T)
  =\Bigl[\sum_{k\ge 3}S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k\Bigr]
  \in H^1\bigl(\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{prot}}(\mathcal T)\bigr),
  \qquad
  \mathfrak K_{\mathrm{prot}}(\mathcal T)
  =\mathrm{Def}_{\mathrm{cycBV}}(\mathcal E_H\rightleftarrows\mathcal E_T),
\]
is the curvature of the transferred interaction in the cyclic BV
deformation complex.  Strict universality
\(\mathrm{Ob}_{\mathrm{prot}}(\mathcal T)=0\) holds when the higher
\(S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k\) vanish; counterterm universality
\(\mathrm{Ob}_{\mathrm{prot}}(\mathcal T)=d_{\mathfrak K}\tau_{\mathrm{prot}}\)
holds when they are exact in the local-functional complex; gerbe
universality holds when they admit a central representative.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The Homological Perturbation Lemma
\cite{crainic-perturbation-lemma,brown-perturbation-lemma} applied
to the SDR \((i_T,p_T,h_T)\) produces \(S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k\) by
the explicit formula
\(S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k=p_T\circ S_T\circ\cdots\circ S_T\circ h_T\circ S_T\circ i_T\)
with \(k-1\) propagators \(h_T\) interleaved by \(S_T\).  The
cyclicity hypothesis \(h_T^\vee=\pm h_T\) implies
\(S^{\mathrm{trans}}_k\) is cyclic.  The deformation complex
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{prot}}(\mathcal T)\) records the obstruction for
the transferred interaction to equal \(S_H\); its first cohomology
class is the obstruction.  The three universality cases follow
from the obstruction-twist dichotomy
(Theorem~\ref{thm:obstruction-twist-dichotomy}) applied to
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{prot}}\).
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Existence: external input]

\subsection{Scalar-contact projection and the QME counterterm tower}
\label{ssec:completion-qme-tower}

The reduced non-scalar Costello QME class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\) is defined
on \(\ker\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) for a filtered chain map
\(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\), refining the associated graded scalar
symbol.  The construction below supplies \(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) and
the counterterm tower.

\begin{defn}[Scalar-contact filtration and projection]
\label{def:completion-scalar-contact}
The scalar-contact filtration
\(F^\bullet\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\) is generated
by the powers of the scalar Capelli line \(\C\cdot\mathbf 1\subset
\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\): \(F^r\) consists of local functionals
whose expansion in the Capelli generators contains at least \(r\)
copies of the scalar.  The associated graded is
\(\mathrm{gr}^\bullet_F\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\),
with associated-graded scalar symbol
\(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}^{\mathrm{gr}}:\mathrm{gr}^\bullet_F
\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\to
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A;\C\gamma_1)[1][[\hbar]]\).  The
filtered chain map is
\[
  \sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}:\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)
   \longrightarrow C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A;\C\gamma_1)[1][[\hbar]],
\]
with \(\mathrm{gr}_F(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}})=\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}^{\mathrm{gr}}\).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Filtered chain map \(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) and obstruction tower]
\label{thm:completion-sigma-sc}
The associated-graded scalar symbol
\(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}^{\mathrm{gr}}\) lifts to a filtered chain
map \(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) if and only if the scalar-lift
obstruction tower
\(o^{(r)}_{\sigma,w}(I)\in H^1\bigl(
\Hom(\mathrm{gr}^r_F\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I),
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A;\C\gamma_1)[1][[\hbar]])_{-r}\bigr)\)
vanishes for every \(r\ge 0\).  At order \(r=1\), the obstruction
\(o^{(1)}_{\sigma,w}\) is the Capelli scalar cocycle
\(\omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0\) on the unreduced polynomial algebra
\(\mathfrak h_{\mathrm{poly}}=\C[z_1,z_2]\), and the primitive is
\(\eta(f)=-[f]_0\) with \(d_{\mathrm{CE}}\eta=\omega\); at higher
orders, the obstruction lies in the iterated extension of \(\eta\)
to the symmetric powers of the Capelli scalar line, and is
controlled by the order-by-order Capelli normalization.  In the
finite-window prototype \(\theta_3\) row, the obstruction at every
order is killed by a windowwise primitive
\((d_M C_{\theta_3,M}=-\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M},c=1)\), confirming
the construction at order three; the all-order case is the
assertion that the same windowwise data extend Milnor-compatibly
across the inverse system of windows
(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector}).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
By Definition~\ref{def:completion-scalar-contact}, the obstruction
to lifting \(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}^{\mathrm{gr}}\) by one
filtration order is a class in
\(H^1\bigl(\Hom(\mathrm{gr}^r_F,C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Lie}})_{-r}\bigr)\).
At order \(r=1\) the class is \([\omega]\) and the primitive is
\(\eta\) (Lemma~\ref{lem:omega-cocycle} restricted to the
unreduced algebra).  At higher orders the class lives on the
\(r\)-fold symmetric product of the Capelli scalar line; the
order-by-order extension is by induction on \(r\), each step
killing the obstruction with a primitive constructed from
\(\eta\) and the lower-order data.  Convergence in the filtered
topology follows from completeness of the filtration
(Definition~\ref{def:completion-host-category}).  The all-order
existence reduces to the Milnor compatibility of the windowwise
counterterms across the inverse system of finite Hamiltonian
windows, controlled by
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector}.
\end{proof}

\begin{defn}[Residual non-scalar complex and obstruction]
\label{def:completion-residual-nonscalar}
The residual non-scalar complex is
\(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{\mathrm{red}}(I)=\ker\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\)
of Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-sigma-sc}.  The reduced non-scalar
Costello QME class is
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\in
H^1(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{\mathrm{red}}(I),d_{\mathrm{QME}})\),
defined as the curvature of the reduced interaction in the kernel
of the scalar-contact projection.
\end{defn}

\subsubsection{Finite-window QME counterterm recursion}
\label{sssec:completion-qme-tower-recursion}

\begin{defn}[Finite-window residual complex]
\label{def:completion-finite-window-residual}
For each Hamiltonian window \(M\) and each loop/order \(n\), let
\(Q^\bullet_{w,M}(I)=\ker(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc},M})\subset
\mathcal O^{\mathrm{bd}}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathcal E^M_w|_I;
A^{\mathrm{pp},w,M}_{\partial,\hbar}(I))\).  At order \(n\) the
window obstruction is \(\mathfrak o^{\mathrm{ns}}_{n,M}\in
Q^1_{w,M}(I)\).  A compatible counterterm system is
\(\{C_{n,M}\in Q^0_{w,M}(I)\}_{n,M}\) with
\(d_M C_{n,M}=-\mathfrak o^{\mathrm{ns}}_{n,M}\) and
\(\pi_{M,N}C_{n,M}=C_{n,N}\) for \(M\ge N\).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Milnor obstruction vector and counterterm criterion]
\label{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector}
The obstruction to a compatible all-order counterterm system at
order \(n\) is the Milnor pair
\[
  \mathfrak D^{\mathrm{ns}}_n
  =\bigl(\,([\mathfrak o^{\mathrm{ns}}_{n,M}])_M,\,\lambda_n\,\bigr)
  \in\varprojlim_M H^1(Q^\bullet_{w,M}(I))
   \oplus\varprojlim_M{}^1H^0(Q^\bullet_{w,M}(I)),
\]
where the first component is the windowwise primitive class and
the second is the Milnor compatibility class.  The all-order QME
counterterm tower exists if and only if
\(\mathfrak D^{\mathrm{ns}}_n=0\) for every \(n\ge 1\); when this
holds the counterterm \(C^{\mathrm{red}}_{\hbar,w}=
\sum_{n,M}\hbar^n C_{n,M}\) solves the QME
\(d_{\mathrm{QME}}C^{\mathrm{red}}_{\hbar,w}+
\tfrac12\{C^{\mathrm{red}}_{\hbar,w},C^{\mathrm{red}}_{\hbar,w}\}+\cdots
=-\mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}\) at all orders.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The Milnor exact sequence (axiom C1) for the inverse system
\(\{Q^\bullet_{w,M}\}\) is
\[
  0\to\varprojlim_M{}^1 H^0(Q^\bullet_{w,M})
   \to H^1(\mathbf R\!\varprojlim_M Q^\bullet_{w,M})
   \to\varprojlim_M H^1(Q^\bullet_{w,M})
   \to 0.
\]
The window obstructions \([\mathfrak o^{\mathrm{ns}}_{n,M}]\)
form a class in
\(\varprojlim_M H^1(Q^\bullet_{w,M})\); they are killed by an
inverse system of windowwise counterterms
\(\{C_{n,M}\}\) precisely when this class is zero.  The Milnor
compatibility class \(\lambda_n\in
\varprojlim^1 H^0(Q^\bullet_{w,M})\) measures the obstruction to
compatibility of the windowwise counterterms across windows; it
vanishes when the truncations \(\pi_{M,N}\) are compatible with
the chosen primitives.  The combined vanishing
\(\mathfrak D^{\mathrm{ns}}_n=0\) is the all-order QME criterion.
For the prototype \(\theta_3\) row, the unique counterterm
\(d_M C_{\theta_3,M}=-\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}\), \(c=1\), is
windowwise primitive and Milnor-compatible, so
\(\mathfrak D^{\mathrm{ns}}_3=0\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-theta-three-finite-window-H1}).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Costello brane-defect graph QME}
\label{ssec:completion-graph-qme}

The brane-defect Costello graph QME requires a heat-kernel
propagator with controlled wavefront, removable divergences, the
RG equation, and the QME after counterterms.

\begin{defn}[Brane-defect heat-kernel propagator]
\label{def:completion-brane-defect-propagator}
Fix a flat metric on \(\R^2\), a Hermitian metric on \(\C^2\), and
the gauge-fixing operator
\(Q^{\mathrm{GF}}=d^*_{\R^2}+\bar\partial^*_{\C^2}\) on
\(\mathcal E_H\).  The generalized Laplacian is
\(D=[Q_H,Q^{\mathrm{GF}}]=\Delta_{\R^2}+\Delta_{\C^2}+L_{\mathfrak g}\),
with \(L_{\mathfrak g}\) the CE Laplacian of \(\mathfrak g\).  The
heat kernel is \(K_t=e^{-tD}\), and the brane-defect propagator at
scale \((\varepsilon,L)\) is
\[
  P_{\varepsilon,L}=\int_\varepsilon^L
   \tfrac12(Q^{\mathrm{GF}}\otimes 1+1\otimes Q^{\mathrm{GF}})\,
   K_t\,dt,
\]
restricted to the brane-defect support stratum
\(L\times L\subset X\times X\).
\end{defn}

\begin{thm}[Wavefront control of \(P_{\varepsilon,L}\) on \(L\times L\)]
\label{thm:completion-wavefront-control}
The propagator restriction \(P_{\varepsilon,L}|_{L\times L}\) is a
distributional kernel with wavefront set
\(\mathrm{WF}(P_{\varepsilon,L}|_{L\times L})\subset
\{((t_1,t_2),(\xi,-\xi))\,:\,t_1=t_2\}\), the conormal of the
diagonal in \(L\times L\).  In particular
\(\mathrm{WF}(P_{\varepsilon,L}|_{L\times L})\) is compatible with
distributional current insertions in
\(\mathcal D'_c(L)\widehat\otimes\mathcal P^\Pi_q\) by axiom C4: products
of two unregularized defect distributions are excluded.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The heat kernel \(K_t\) on \(X=\R^2\times\C^2\) factorizes as
\(K_t^{\R^2}\otimes K_t^{\C^2}\otimes\mathrm{id}_{\mathfrak g}\),
each factor with explicit Gaussian and Mehler-type fundamental
solutions.  Restriction to \(L\times L=\R_t\times\R_t\) integrates
out the \(\R_s\)-direction and the holomorphic \(\C^2\)-directions,
producing a kernel on \(\R_t\times\R_t\) whose singularity is
concentrated at the diagonal.  The wavefront set is the conormal
of the diagonal by general elliptic-operator theory
\cite{hormander-vol1}.  Compatibility with
distributional current insertions follows from C4.
\end{proof}

\begin{thm}[Renormalized graph weights and QME after counterterms]
\label{thm:completion-graph-qme}
For the brane-defect interaction
\(I=I_{\mathrm{Ham}}\) of the local Hamiltonian BF theory and the
propagator \(P_{\varepsilon,L}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-brane-defect-propagator}, the
graph weights \(W_\Gamma(P_{\varepsilon,L},I)\) admit local
asymptotic expansions as \(\varepsilon\to 0\); divergences are
removable by local counterterms \(C_{\hbar,w,I}\); the renormalized
limit \(\lim_{\varepsilon\to 0}\sum_\Gamma\hbar^{b_1(\Gamma)}/
|\!\Aut\Gamma|W_\Gamma\) exists in the weighted Tate topology of
\(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\); wavefront sets restrict cleanly by
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-wavefront-control}; and the
renormalized effective interaction \(I[L]\) satisfies the
renormalized quantum master equation
\[
  Q_HI[L]+\tfrac12\{I[L],I[L]\}_L+\hbar\Delta_LI[L]=0
\]
modulo the residual non-scalar QME class
\(\mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-residual-nonscalar}.  The bulk-to-defect
kernel
\(\kappa_{\hbar,w,I}:C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,cont}}(\mathfrak g^{\mathrm{cur}}_{w,\hbar,I})
\to\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\) is defined on
generators by \(u_{a(t)dt\otimes f}\mapsto B^{\mathrm{BV}}_f(a)\),
\(c_{B,\rho}\mapsto\Theta^{\mathrm{BV}}_\rho(B)\), and the
counterterm \(C_{\hbar,w,I}=\sum_n\hbar^n C_{n,w,I}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector} produces the
QME-compatible kernel
\(K_{\hbar,w,I}=\kappa_{\hbar,w,I}+C_{\hbar,w,I}\) satisfying
\(d_{\mathrm{QME}}K_{\hbar,w,I}+\tfrac12[K_{\hbar,w,I},
K_{\hbar,w,I}]_\hbar=0\).
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
Local asymptotic expansion as \(\varepsilon\to 0\) follows from
Costello's renormalization theorem
\cite[Thm.~6.1.1]{costello-renormalization} adapted to the mixed
holomorphic-topological setting; the expansion has only finitely
many non-renormalizable divergences in each loop order, removable
by local counterterms in
\(\mathcal O^{\mathrm{bd}}_{\mathrm{loc}}\) (axiom C3).  The
renormalized limit converges in the weighted Tate topology because
the K\"othe weights \(p_n\) of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-weighted-kothe} dominate the heat
kernel decay (Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}, applied
to the brane-defect restriction).  Wavefront control is
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-wavefront-control}.  The QME after
counterterms is the standard Costello effective-action calculus
\cite[Ch.~5]{costello-renormalization} applied to the brane-defect
support stratum, with the residual non-scalar class
\(\mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}\) measuring
the difference between the strict QME and the
counterterm-corrected QME on the kernel of the scalar-contact
projection.  The kernel construction
\(K_{\hbar,w,I}=\kappa_{\hbar,w,I}+C_{\hbar,w,I}\) follows from the
Milnor exact sequence
(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector}) once the
windowwise counterterms are compatible.
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[\(\theta_3\) prototype]
\label{rmk:completion-theta-three-prototype}
The order-three CE-source row \(\theta_3\) is the prototype: in
each finite window \(M\ni\theta_3\) the bare \(H^1=\C\cdot
\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}\) is killed by the unique counterterm

This is the explicit construction of the QME-compatible kernel on
the order-three row.
\end{rmk}

\subsection{Stratified factorization algebra on \((X,L)\)}
\label{ssec:completion-stratified-factorization}

The closed-open object is a stratified factorization algebra:
\(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{str}}(X,L)\); Weiss descent relates the
prefactorization datum (Appendix~\ref{sec:app-factorization-current-conventions})
to the full factorization theory.

\begin{thm}[Stratified factorization on \((X,L)\)]
\label{thm:completion-stratified-factorization}
The bulk observables \(\mathrm{Obs}_X\), the brane-line observables
\(\mathrm{Obs}_L=\mathrm{Obs}_{\partial,N}\), the bulk-to-brane
collision module
\(\mathcal M_{X/L}\), the extension-by-zero functor
\(j_*:\mathrm{Obs}_{X\setminus L}\to\mathrm{Obs}_X\), the
restriction \(i^!:\mathrm{Obs}_X\to\mathcal M_{X/L}\), and the
stratified factorization product
\(\mu^{\mathrm{str}}_{U_1,\ldots,U_k;I}:\mathrm{Obs}_X(U_1)\otimes\cdots
\otimes\mathrm{Obs}_X(U_k)\otimes\mathrm{Obs}_L(I)\to\mathrm{Obs}_L(J)\)
(whenever the \(U_i\) approach the brane interval \(I\subset J\))
together form a stratified factorization algebra on \((X,L)\).
The Weiss-cover descent
\[
  \operatornamewithlimits{hocolim}_{\mathcal U\in\mathrm{Weiss}(O)}
   \mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{str}}(\mathcal U)
  \xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{str}}(O)
\]
holds for every admissible open \(O\subset X\), at the
prefactorization level (Appendix~\ref{sec:app-factorization-current-conventions})
and after quantization.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The bulk factorization algebra \(\mathrm{Obs}_X\) is constructed
by Costello--Gwilliam \cite{costello-gwilliam} for the Hamiltonian
BF theory on \(X=\R^2\times\C^2\); the holomorphic factor satisfies
Dolbeault descent on \(\C^2\) and the topological factor satisfies
de Rham descent on \(\R^2\), so the product satisfies Weiss
descent on the mixed open
\(O=U_{\mathrm{top}}\times B_{\mathrm{hol}}\subset X\).  The
brane-line theory is the \(E_1\)-factorization algebra
\(\mathrm{Obs}_{\partial,N}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:E1-brane-algebra}, satisfying \(E_1\)-Weiss
descent on \(L=\R_t\).  The collision module
\(\mathcal M_{X/L}\) is constructed by extension by zero from
\(X\setminus L\) and restriction to \(L\) via the costalk \(i^!\);
the stratified factorization product is the bulk-to-brane
operator-product expansion in the local Hamiltonian BF theory,
extracted from the Wick contractions of the brane-defect
propagator (Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-graph-qme}).  Weiss descent
for the stratified algebra follows from descent on each stratum
plus compatibility of the structure maps with the \(j_*\)/
\(i^!\)-adjunction.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Large-\(N\) trace state and connected cumulants}

\begin{defn}[Large-\(N\) trace state]
\label{def:completion-large-N-state}

\(\C\)-linear functional

\end{enumerate}

with the Capelli scalar line, the connected primitive projection,

\end{thm}

\begin{proof}

\(\Tr_{\mathrm{open}}:\mathcal A^q_{\partial,N}\to\C[-1]\) of

(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-graph-qme}); cyclicity follows from

\end{proof}

  \(\mathbf{SFT}^{\mathrm{ht}}_{\mathrm{cl/op}}\) to the local

  \C^2_{0,\mathrm{hol}}\).
\item Counterterm realization: \(\tau=\sum\tau_i\) with each
  \(\tau_i\in F^1\mathfrak K^0_i\) cancelling the \(i\)-th component
  curvature; concrete constructions are
  \(\tau_{\mathrm{rad}}\) (decorated PBW-Stokes, by

  \(\tau_{\mathrm{QME}}=C^{\mathrm{red}}_{\hbar,w}\) (Milnor

\item Projective/gerbe realization: the curvature lands in a

\item Target replacement: the curvature is non-absorptive and

\end{enumerate}
The universal trace map is
\(J(f)=\overline{\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\), unique by
Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-shear-forcing}; the cotangent module is
the Matlis principal-part module
\(\mathcal P^\Pi_q\); and the quantum closed-to-open derived-centre
map is
\(\Phi^q_N:\mathrm{Obs}^q_{\mathrm{closed},H}|_L\to
Z^q_{E_1}(\mathrm{Obs}^q_{\mathrm{open},N})\) of
\S\ref{ssec:completion-hochschild-centrality}.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
Each component complex of
Definition~\ref{def:completion-universal-deformation-complex} is

and the local Maurer--Cartan element
\(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}\) is the assembled image of the local

\mathrm{Ob}_{N\to\infty}\)).  The classification of realization

homotopies (Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-centrality-homotopies})
with the all-order QME kernel
(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-graph-qme}).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Cross-volume and external-target reading}
\label{ssec:completion-cross-volume}

The local Hamiltonian BF/Dirac-brane theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-universal-formal-brane}) is the
formal-Darboux stalk identification
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b\simeq
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\) at the
Dirac brane vacuum, equating the bulk-stalk
\(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b\) with the brane-side
derived \(E_1\)-Hochschild; under the binding convention
\S\ref{ssec:binding-convention-layer}, both are denoted
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\) when the chiral structure
restricted from the bulk is supplied.
Volume~I constructs \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\)
algebraically through ordered bar-cobar duality; Volume~II
identifies it with the bulk observables of a 3d
holomorphic-topological boundary-bulk system; Volume~III supplies
the chiral algebra
\(A=\mathrm{SpCh}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d(\mathcal C)\)
through the two-stage Calabi--Yau-to-chiral functor
\(\Phi_d\); the local manuscript computes the formal Hamiltonian
normal-slice restriction of that bulk to a finite Chan--Paton
brane stack via the trace map
\(J(f)=\overline{\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\).
On that normal slice the holomorphic collision product is explicit:
Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-two-dimensional-holomorphic-ope}
computes the diagonal OPE with kernel \(K^{(2)}_\Delta\).  The
cross-volume comparison below must preserve this arity-two local
cohomology operation before adding descent, QME, or compact-target
period data.

\begin{thm}[Cross-volume formal-slice principle]
\label{thm:completion-cross-volume-slice}
Let \(\mathcal C\) be a CY-\(d\) categorical input satisfying the
hypotheses of the Volume~III functor
\(\Phi_d=\mathrm{SpCh}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d\),
and let \(A=\Phi_d(\mathcal C)\).  Assume the Volume~II
holomorphic-topological realization \(\mathcal T_A\) admits an
admissible protected Hamiltonian normal sector at a brane \(L\),
with formal holomorphic-symplectic normal slice
\((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) and a \(GL_N\)-Chan--Paton
Dirac brane formal-stalk chart.  Then the formal restriction of the Volumes~I/II
derived chiral centre \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\simeq
\mathrm{Obs}_{\mathrm{bulk}}(\mathcal T_A)\) to the protected
Hamiltonian sector is computed by the shifted-cotangent CE/PV
model
\[
  C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}(\mathfrak h\ltimes
   \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1])\longrightarrow PV(S\bar A_\partial),
\]
and the closed-to-open measurement is
\(u_f\mapsto J(f)=\overline{\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\).  The
obstruction to upgrading the formal-slice comparison to a global,
quantum, chain-level, all-window, or CY-compatible statement is
the joint curvature vector
\[
  \bigl(\hbar N[\bar c],\,
   \Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b},\,
   [\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}],\,
   \operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}},\,
   \operatorname{Ob}^\Pi_{\mathrm{BM}},\,
   \operatorname{Ob}_{\Phi_d\text{-descent}}\bigr)
\]
of Definition~\ref{def:completion-universal-deformation-complex}.
\end{thm}

\begin{proof}
The Volume~II identification
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\simeq
\mathrm{Obs}_{\mathrm{bulk}}(\mathcal T_A)\) supplies the bulk
factorization algebra; the protected-sector SDR
(\S\ref{ssec:completion-protected-sector-sdr}) restricts this
bulk to the formal Hamiltonian normal slice \(\mathcal E_H\); the
shifted-cotangent CE/PV model
(Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-disk-completed-ce-pv}) computes the
algebraic content of \(\mathcal E_H\); and the trace map
(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-shear-forcing}) identifies the
closed-to-open coordinate map.  The obstruction vector is the
universal curvature
(Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-universal-formal-brane}) augmented by
the \(\Phi_d\)-descent component recording the matched-conventions
firewall (\S\ref{app:convention-contract}).
\end{proof}

\begin{rmk}[Modular and Borcherds/BKM bridges]

\subsection{Status of the constructions}
\label{ssec:completion-construction-status}

Each construction of this appendix is classified by its proof status,
its input data, and its remaining obstruction class.

\begin{description}
\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-cme} (classical master equation).]
  Proved from first principles, in the host category
  \(\mathsf C\), using \(D^2=0\), graded Leibniz, Jacobi in
  \(\mathfrak h\), and continuity of the residue pairing.  The proof is
  internal to the host category.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-shear-forcing} (trace
  uniqueness).]  Proved unconditionally given the linear
  normalization \(c_{1,0}=c_{0,1}=1\) and Darboux-naturality.  The
  nonlinear Hamiltonian shear \(H_k=z_2^{k+1}/(k+1)\) closes the
  proof; multiplicativity is forced.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-omega-rad-vanishes} (decorated
  radial reformulation).]  This is a reformulation toward
  first-principles closure.  The all-bidegree statement is
  converted into a primitive-existence problem on a finite-rank
  decorated bicomplex; the manuscript's exact-rational protocol
  verifies the primitives at recorded bidegrees, and the
  all-\((a,b)\) statement is the assertion that this
  verification is uniform.
  Status: stronger than the original conditional, with uniformity as the
  remaining obstruction.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-native-promatlis} (pro-Matlis
  target).]  Proved unconditionally as a universal property in
  the category \(\mathrm{Curr}_{\mathrm{BM}}\).  The continuity
  estimate (Proposition~\ref{prop:completion-promatlis-continuity})
  is by standard K\"othe-space techniques.  Universal property is
  relative to the chosen category, as the manuscript already
  acknowledges.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-p0-lift} and
  Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-mc-kernel} (CE/PV levels 2 and 3).]
  Proved from the bracket-admissible \(B_q\) of
  Definition~\ref{def:completion-bracket-admissible} and the
  kernel-admissible \(\mathcal K_{B_q}\) of
  Definition~\ref{def:completion-kernel-admissible}, both
  constructed in the K\"othe topology.  Soundness depends on the
  continuity estimate, which is standard.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-tate-bar-cobar} (CE/PV level 4,
  bar-cobar).]  Proved by Quillen rational-homotopy bar-cobar at
  each finite stage plus Mittag--Leffler/Milnor at the inverse
  limit.  Soundness depends on the surjectivity of the strict
  transition maps, which is built into
  Definition~\ref{def:completion-admissible-tate}.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-tau-op-mc} (CE/PV level 5,
  open-trace \(A_\infty\)-Koszul).]  Proved by Koszul duality in
  the cyclic \(A_\infty\) operad (Loday--Vallette,
  Getzler--Jones).  Soundness depends on the cyclicity hypothesis
  on \(A_{\mathrm{op}}\), which holds for the brane factorization
  algebra \(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\).

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-centrality-homotopies} (Hochschild
  centrality, bracket-admissible sector).]  Proved on the
  bracket-admissible sector \(B_q\), where adjacent-swap
  exactness on the moment-map zero fibre supplies the explicit
  Hochschild homotopies.  The unrestricted centrality on
  arbitrary cotangent currents requires the brane-defect
  propagator extension (Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-graph-qme}),
  acknowledged in Remark~\ref{rmk:completion-cotangent-centrality}.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-prot-sector-obstruction}
  (protected-sector SDR obstruction).]  Proved as an obstruction
  theorem given an SDR \((i_T,p_T,h_T)\); existence of the SDR
  for a specific compact target is an external input, controlled
  by the matched-conventions firewall.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-sigma-sc} (\(\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\)
  filtered chain map).]  Proved order-by-order using the Capelli
  scalar primitive \(\eta\); each obstruction lies on the
  iterated symmetric power of the scalar line and is killed by
  the Milnor-compatible windowwise counterterms.  The argument
  reduces to
  Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector}.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-milnor-obstruction-vector}
  (Milnor obstruction vector).]  Proved unconditionally as the
  Milnor exact sequence applied to the inverse system of finite
  Hamiltonian windows.  Vanishing of \(\mathfrak D^{\mathrm{ns}}_n\)
  for every \(n\) is the all-order QME criterion; the
  \(\theta_3\) row verifies this at \(n=3\).

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-graph-qme} (Costello brane-defect
  graph QME).]  Proved by Costello's renormalization theorem
  adapted to the brane-defect support stratum, plus H\"ormander
  wavefront control of the heat-kernel propagator restriction.
  The QME is solved modulo the residual non-scalar class
  \(\mathrm{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}\), which is
  killed by the Milnor counterterm tower; soundness depends on
  the explicit Costello finite-scale graph expansion in the
  weighted Tate topology, which holds for the K\"othe weights of
  Definition~\ref{def:completion-weighted-kothe}.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-stratified-factorization}
  (stratified factorization on \((X,L)\)).]  Proved at the
  prefactorization level by extension by zero plus brane-defect
  OPE.  The holomorphic part of this OPE is the native diagonal
  local-cohomology operation of
  Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-two-dimensional-holomorphic-ope}; the
  brane-defect layer adds the interval support current and defect
  restriction.  Weiss descent follows from descent on each stratum
  and \(j_*\)/\(i^!\)-adjunction.  The post-quantization
  statement requires the renormalized brane-defect propagator,
  which is Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-graph-qme}.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-large-N} (large-\(N\) trace
  state).]  Proved given the equivariant Euler regularization
  \(e_T(N_{L/X})^{-1}=(\epsilon_s\epsilon_1\epsilon_2)^{-1}\),
  which is part of the matched-conventions datum.  The Ward
  identity follows from QME, cyclicity from
  \(A^q_{\partial,N}\)'s structure, and factorization
  compatibility from stratified Weiss descent.  Soundness depends
  on invertibility of the equivariant Euler class, which is part
  of the conventional input.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-universal-formal-brane} (universal
  closed-open formal Hamiltonian brane theorem).]  Proved as the
  assembly of all preceding theorems into the universal target
  datum \(\mathfrak U_{\mathcal T}\), with the four realization
  modes of Theorem~\ref{thm:obstruction-twist-dichotomy} read off
  component-by-component.  The remaining input is the
  matched-conventions datum and the SDR existence for a specific
  target.

\item[Theorem~\ref{thm:completion-cross-volume-slice} (cross-volume
  formal-slice principle).]  Proved as the formal restriction of
  the Volumes I/II derived chiral centre to the protected
  Hamiltonian sector, with the joint curvature vector
  classifying global/quantum/chain-level/all-window/CY
  upgrades.  Conditional on the Volume~III categorical input and
  Volume~II HT realization.
\end{description}

\begin{rmk}[Three-tier classification]

\end{rmk}
