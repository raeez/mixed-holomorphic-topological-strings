\begin{abstract}

\noindent
Let
\[
  X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{0,\mathrm{hol}},
  \qquad
  \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot1,
  \qquad
  \{f,g\}
  =\partial_{z_1}f\,\partial_{z_2}g
   -\partial_{z_2}f\,\partial_{z_1}g.
\]
The native closed model is the Hamiltonian BF theory associated to the
shifted-cotangent Lie algebra
\[
  \mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes P[1],
  \qquad
  P=\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}},
\]
where \(P\) is realized by Grothendieck--Matlis principal parts and paired
with \(\mathfrak h\) by the residue.  Its classical local-observable
algebra is the standard continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg cochain algebra
\[
  \mathcal O^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}
  =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak g)
  =\widehat{\mathbf S}
    \bigl(\mathfrak g^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[-1]\bigr).
\]
For \(\rho\in P\) and \(f\in\mathfrak h\), denote the corresponding
linear cochains by \(c^\rho\) and \(u_f\).  With
\((V[1])^n=V^{n+1}\), their standard cohomological degrees are
\[
  |c^\rho|_{\mathrm{CE}}=1,
  \qquad
  |u_f|_{\mathrm{CE}}=2.
\]

\medskip\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes has the non-effective boundary chart
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
  =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}\!\left(
    \mathfrak{gl}_N,
    \mathrm{Sym}\bigl(
      \mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
      \oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]\bigr)\right),
  \qquad
  Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2].
\]
The effective scalar-reduced chart is
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl,red}}_{\partial,N}
  =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}\!\left(
    \mathfrak{pgl}_N,
    \mathrm{Sym}\bigl(
      \mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
      \oplus\mathfrak{sl}_N[1]\bigr)\right).
\]
The non-effective chart retains the
central Koszul class \(\operatorname{Tr}\psi\), the scalar trace line,
the quotient-stack stabilizer cohomology, and the finite-rank trace
relations.  For a polynomial Hamiltonian
\(f\in\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot1\), the Koszul
adjacent-transposition homotopy defines the trace class
\[
  J_N(f)
  =\bigl[\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,\widetilde f
      (\phi_1,\phi_2)}\bigr]
  \in H^0(A^{\mathrm{cl,red}}_{\partial,N}),
\]
independently of the ordinary-word lift \(\widetilde f\).  A chosen
Koszul lift of the Hamiltonian action gives a derivation \(X_{f,N}\) on
the derived commuting chart.  On scalar-reduced cohomology,
\[
  X_{f,N}\bigl(J_N(g)\bigr)=J_N(\{f,g\}_{\mathfrak h}).
\]
These are the fixed-rank trace and Hamiltonian-action data.

\medskip\noindent
Procesi--Razmyslov stability in total trace degree \(W\) begins in the
range \(N\geq W\).  Scalar reduction followed by degreewise stable rank
passage and admissible completion gives the rank-free trace algebra
\[
  A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}
  \cong\widehat{\mathbf S}_{\mathrm{Tate}}(\mathfrak h).
\]
Its formal spectrum is the completed linear Poisson space \(P\).  Write
\(O_f\) for the linear function determined by \(f\in\mathfrak h\), and
\(\partial_\rho\) for the constant vector field determined by
\(\rho\in P\):
\[
  \partial_\rho(O_f)=\langle\rho,f\rangle_{\mathrm{res}}.
\]
For a basis \(H_I\) of \(\mathfrak h\), residue-dual basis
\(\rho^I\), and structure constants
\([H_I,H_J]=C^K_{IJ}H_K\), the linear Kirillov Poisson bivector is
\[
  \pi_{\mathrm{lin}}
  =\frac12 C^K_{IJ}O_K\,\partial^I\partial^J,
  \qquad
  d_{\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}}=[\pi_{\mathrm{lin}},-]_S.
\]

Give every \(u\)-coordinate cotangent weight one and every
\(c\)-coordinate cotangent weight zero.  The coordinate grading is the
explicit regrading
\[
  |a|_{\mathrm{coord}}
  =|a|_{\mathrm{CE}}-2\operatorname{wt}_u(a),
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Regr}_u
  C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{std,coord}}(\mathfrak g),
\]
where \(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{std,coord}}(\mathfrak g)\)
is the row-finite coordinate envelope of the standard continuous
Chevalley--Eilenberg algebra.
Thus \(|u_f|_{\mathrm{coord}}=0\).  The row-finite monomial structure
constants define the differential on the full coordinate product; finite
Taylor windows serve as coefficient projections and trace-evaluation
windows.  We prove the coordinate Lichnerowicz isomorphism
\[
  \Phi^{\mathrm{st}}_{\mathrm{coord}}\colon
  \operatorname{Regr}_u
  C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{std,coord}}(\mathfrak g)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \bigl(
    \mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{coord}}(A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}),
    d_{\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}}\bigr),
\]
determined by
\[
  c^\rho\longmapsto\partial_\rho,
  \qquad
  u_f\longmapsto O_f=J^{\mathrm{st}}(f).
\]
The Hamiltonian vector field is
\[
  X_f=d_{\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}}O_f
      =\Phi^{\mathrm{st}}_{\mathrm{coord}}
        (d_{\mathrm{CE}}u_f),
  \qquad
  X_f(O_g)=O_{\{f,g\}}.
\]
The constant fields \(\partial_\rho\) and the Hamiltonian fields
\(X_f\) are thereby distinguished intrinsically.  The canonical
contraction bracket has degree \(-3\) in the standard physical grading
and degree \(-1\) after \(\operatorname{Regr}_u\).  A bracket-admissible
weighted-Tate completion realizes this contraction continuously; a
declared Batalin--Vilkovisky (BV) receiver supplies the diagonal kernel
and heat-kernel propagator.

\medskip\noindent
The ordinary Hochschild--Kostant--Rosenberg (HKR) theorem occupies the
zero-Poisson special fibre.  On every finite polynomial coordinate
algebra \(A_V=\mathbf S(V)\),
\[
  \operatorname{HKR}_V\colon
  \bigl(\mathrm{PV}(A_V),0\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \bigl(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Hoch}}(A_V,A_V),b\bigr)
\]
is a quasi-isomorphism.  A compatible system of transition homotopies
gives its Roos-completed admissible form at \(\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}=0\).
For the nonzero linear Poisson tensor, a supplied continuous filtered
\(L_\infty\) formality quasi-isomorphism
\[
  \mathcal U=(\mathcal U_n)_{n\geq1}\colon
  \bigl(
    \mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{adm}}
      (A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H})[1],0\bigr)
  \longrightarrow
  \bigl(
    C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Hoch},\mathrm{adm}}
      (A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H},
       A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H})[1],b\bigr),
\]
with
\(\mathcal U_1=\operatorname{HKR}\), produces the Maurer--Cartan image
\[
  \mu_\hbar
  =\sum_{n\geq1}
    \frac{\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}^n}{n!}
    \mathcal U_n(\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}^{\otimes n}),
  \qquad
  m_\hbar=m+\mu_\hbar,
  \qquad
  A_\hbar=
  \bigl(A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}
    [[\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}]],m_\hbar\bigr).
\]
Together with a Poincar\'e--Birkhoff--Witt (PBW) equivalence
\[
  \beta_{\mathrm{PBW}}\colon
  (A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H}[[\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}]],m_\hbar)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \widehat U_{\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}}(\mathfrak h)
\]
and Roos-compatible transition homotopies, the twisted Taylor components
give
\[
  \mathcal U^{\hbar\pi}\colon
  \bigl(
    \mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{adm}}(A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H})
      [[\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}]],
    \hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}d_{\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}}\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\ \simeq\ }
  \bigl(
    C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Hoch},\mathrm{adm}}(A_\hbar,A_\hbar),
    b_\hbar=[m_\hbar,-]_G\bigr).
\]
Put
\[
  \mathfrak t
  =\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{adm}}
    (A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H})[1],
  \qquad
  \mathfrak d
  =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{Hoch},\mathrm{adm}}
    (A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H},
     A^{\mathrm{st}}_{\partial,H})[1],
\]
and let
\[
  \mathcal C^\bullet_{\mathcal U}
  =\prod_{k\geq1}
    \underline{\operatorname{Hom}}^\bullet_{\mathrm{filt}}
    \left(
      \widehat{\operatorname{Sym}}^k(\mathfrak t[1]),
      \mathfrak d[1]\right)
\]
be the comparison-map deformation complex.  The successive formality
curvatures define classes
\[
  \operatorname{Ob}_{\mathcal U,m+1}
  \in
  H^1\!\left(
    \operatorname{gr}^{m+1}\mathcal C^\bullet_{\mathcal U},
    D^{(m)}_{\mathcal U}\right),
\]
where \(D^{(m)}_{\mathcal U}\) is the linearized formality differential.
PBW multiplicativity is governed by the deformation complex of
\(\beta_{\mathrm{PBW}}\).  Their vanishing with compatible primitives is
the precise nonzero-Poisson Hochschild comparison criterion.  A
finite-\(N\) Hochschild-centre morphism further consists of a
Maurer--Cartan solution in the completed Hochschild-centrality
convolution \(L_\infty\)-algebra.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian line defines the cocycle
\[
  \omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0,
  \qquad
  \bar c=\omega|_{\bar A\wedge\bar A}.
\]
Writing \(\Phi_{\hbar_{\mathrm W}}\) for the Weyl-ordered trace
representation, the rank-\(N\) trace character sends this cocycle to the
Capelli projective class
\[
  [\Phi_{\hbar_{\mathrm W}}(z_1),
    \Phi_{\hbar_{\mathrm W}}(z_2)]_{\mathrm{proj}}
  =\hbar_{\mathrm W}N\,\mathbf 1,
  \qquad
  \hbar_{\mathrm W}N[\bar c]
  \in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar_{\mathrm W}]].
\]
The parameter \(\hbar_{\mathrm{Rees}}\) grades the linear-Poisson/PBW
deformation, while \(\hbar_{\mathrm W}\) grades the Weyl/Moyal trace
representation.
A determinant-line or modular-line realization consists of a line,
connection, and Atiyah-class pullback representing this cocycle.

\medskip\noindent
The Hamiltonian BF theory is the native formal-local field theory of the
theorem.  A realization inside
Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa (BCOV)/Kodaira--Spencer B-model field
theory is a matched reduction datum: a retained BCOV BV subquotient, a
quasi-isomorphism to the Hamiltonian BF local Lie algebra, preservation of
the BV pairing and brackets, identification of the Dirac-brane image, and
matching of the anomaly classes.  The curvature of this comparison lies
in the corresponding filtered mapping-cone deformation complex.

Let \(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}/\hbar}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\) be the bulk
mixed holomorphic--topological (HT) factorization algebra, and let
\(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial\) be the primitive boundary open
factorization category.  The global mixed-HT Deligne problem asks for an
open--closed morphism
\[
  \mathrm{OCA}_N\colon
  \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}/\hbar}_{\mathrm{bulk}}
  \longrightarrow
  Z^{\mathrm{der},\mathrm{Mor}}_{E_2^{\mathrm{HT}}}
  (\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial).
\]
Its construction consists of the mixed holomorphic--topological
Swiss-cheese action, the all-arity Morita centre lift, the admissible
Hochschild-centre lift, flat formal-Darboux transport, the unreduced
cotangent-current realization, Weiss/Ran descent, the all-order quantum
master equation (QME) counterterm tower, anomaly and locality primitives,
and a contracting homotopy of the comparison cone.  The scalar-reduced
stable trace coordinate and the Capelli projective class form the
formal-Darboux row of this obstruction problem.

\end{abstract}
