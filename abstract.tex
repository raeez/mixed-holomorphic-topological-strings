\begin{abstract}

\noindent
The local target is
\[
  X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}} .
\]
In this formal neighbourhood the Costello--Li renormalized
Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa B-model is the
holomorphic-topological BF theory
\[
  S=\int_X\bigl\langle\mc B,\,(d+\bar\partial)\mc A
    +\tfrac12[\mc A,\mc A]_{\mathfrak h}\bigr\rangle .
\]
Here
\[
  \mc A\in
  \Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
  \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h,
  \qquad
  \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot1,
\]
and \(\mc B\) takes values in the continuous Grothendieck--Matlis dual
\[
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}
   =
  H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2 .
\]
The pairing is the residue pairing.

\medskip\noindent
Fix a brane vacuum \(b\in\C^2\).  Its formal Darboux chart is
\[
  A_b=\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\C^2,b}\cong\C[[z_1,z_2]],
  \qquad \omega_b=dz_1\wedge dz_2 .
\]
For \(N\) Dirac branes at \(b\), the Chan--Paton fields are
\[
  \phi_1,\phi_2\in\mathfrak{gl}_N,\qquad
  z_i\longmapsto\phi_i .
\]
The derived moment-map equation is imposed by an antifield
\(\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]\) with
\[
  Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2].
\]
Thus the classical open-string algebra on the worldline is
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
   =
   C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}\bigl(\mathfrak{gl}_N,\,
       \mathrm{Sym}(\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
                    \oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N[1])\bigr),
\]
with Koszul differential \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\).

\medskip\noindent
The brane trace is the map
\[
  J\colon\mathfrak h\longrightarrow
  \mathcal O(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0))^{GL_N},
  \qquad
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)} .
\]
It is the unique \(GL_N\)-equivariant, single-trace, moment-map
compatible formal-coordinate expression of the closed generator at the
brane vacuum.

\medskip\noindent
The closed classical observables form the mixed
holomorphic-topological factorization algebra
\[
  \mc F^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}(U\times B)
   =
  C^{\mathrm{CE}}_\bullet\bigl(
     \Omega^\bullet_c(U)\widehat\otimes
     \Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g\bigr),
  \qquad
  \mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1].
\]
Pushing forward along
\(\pi\colon\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\to\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\)
gives a holomorphic factorization algebra on \(\C^2\) valued in
\(E_2\)-algebras:
\[
  \mc A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}(B)
   =
  \mc F^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}(D^2\times B).
\]
Before any curve restriction, its holomorphic OPE is a
two-variable diagonal expansion.  For
\(\Delta\subset\C^2_z\times\C^2_w\) and
\(\zeta_i=z_i-w_i\), the singular coefficient module is
\[
  H^2_\Delta(\C[[z_1,z_2,w_1,w_2]])\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2,
\]
with Cauchy kernel
\[
  K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)=\frac{d\zeta_1d\zeta_2}{\zeta_1\zeta_2}.
\]
For \(x\in\mathfrak g\), write \(\mathsf J_x(z)\) for the linear
holomorphic current determined by \(x\) in the
Chevalley--Eilenberg factorization algebra
\(B\mapsto C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)
\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g)\); then
\[
  \mathsf J_x(z)\mathsf J_y(w)
  \sim
  K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)\mathsf J_{[x,y]_{\mathfrak g}}(w),
  \qquad x,y\in\mathfrak g.
\]
Restricting along a holomorphic curve \(C\hookrightarrow\C^2\)
produces a Beilinson--Drinfeld curve chiral algebra (a vertex
algebra) with generators and relations the one-variable
specialisation of this two-dimensional OPE through the curve Cauchy
kernel \(K^{(1)}_C(z,w)=dz/(z-w)\).

\medskip\noindent
The main theorem is the formal-Darboux stalk of the mixed
holomorphic-topological Deligne conjecture:
\[
  \mc A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b
   \simeq
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
   \simeq
  HH^\bullet(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
\]
as \(E_2\)-algebras.  Under this identification
\[
  c_f\longmapsto\theta_f,\qquad
  u_f\longmapsto J(f).
\]
Here \(\theta_f\) is the Hamiltonian Schouten derivation determined by
\(f\), and \(J(f)\) acts by multiplication.  Thus the trace map
\(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) is the formal
Darboux coordinate of the brane-side derived Hochschild centre.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian produces the finite-\(N\) central term.  Its
three equivalent expressions are
\[
  \omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0,\qquad
  \{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N,\qquad
  [\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\,I .
\]
This is the Capelli scalar
\[
  \hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)} .
\]

\medskip\noindent
The theorem is local.  It does not prove the global chiral
\(E_d\)-Deligne theorem at \(d\ge2\), a compact Calabi--Yau theorem, or
an operator-level modular lift.  Those require descent, quantum master
equation data, anomaly cancellation, and matched-conventions
comparison.  The paper records the corresponding obstruction classes.

\end{abstract}
