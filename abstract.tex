\begin{abstract}

\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line
\(L=\R_t\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\}\subset
X=\R_t\times\R_s\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}\) probes the formal symplectic
polydisk \((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) through a commuting
pair \(\phi_1,\phi_2\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\). A closed Hamiltonian
\(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\) is measured on the brane by literal substitution
\(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\), the unique
\(GL_N\)-invariant Darboux-natural scalar-reduced measurement
(Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-local-universal-property}).

\medskip
\noindent
The conjugation moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\) imposes
\([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\). With \(A\in\Omega^1(\R)\otimes\mathfrak{gl}_N\)
the Lagrange multiplier in the Dirac action
\(S_\partial=\int_\R\operatorname{Tr}(\phi_1\,d\phi_2+
A[\phi_1,\phi_2])\), the BV antifield
\(\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]\) of \(A\) resolves the constraint
homologically: the BV differential \(Q\) acts by
\(Q\phi_1=Q\phi_2=0\), \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\), and the trace
algebra \(\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) for
\(f\in\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot1\) is the ghost-zero
\(GL_N\)-invariant \(Q\)-cohomology.

\medskip
\noindent
Constants give the zero Hamiltonian vector field, so the closed
symmetry algebra is
\(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1\) with Poisson bracket
\(\{f,g\}=
\partial_{z_1}f\,\partial_{z_2}g-
\partial_{z_2}f\,\partial_{z_1}g\pmod\C\). The
canonical-transformation BV theory takes its fields in the shifted
cotangent extension
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\),
with closed observables
\(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,cont}}(\mathfrak g)\). A Hamiltonian \(f\)
has two avatars: the Hamiltonian-dual CE coordinate \(c_f\) acting
through the Schouten vector field \(\theta_f\), and the
shifted-cotangent-dual coordinate \(u_f\) measured on the brane as
\(O_f=J(f)\). The cochain map
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{coord}}\colon
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,coord}}(\mathfrak g)\xrightarrow{\sim}
\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\) is an isomorphism with the Schouten
polyvectors of \(\bar A\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}); the
\(P_0\)-bracket and Maurer--Cartan kernel refinements hold on the
topologised subalgebras for which they converge, with weighted-Tate
completion \(B_w\) the minimal RG-stable completion in which the
heat-kernel propagator extends and the Casimir converges
(Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}).

\medskip
\noindent
The continuous dual
\(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong\mathcal P=
\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b}\),
\(\rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1dz_2\), is the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module \cite{matlis-injective}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}); the
polynomial PBW classes \(\Psi_{a,b}\) carry the same labels as the
residues \(\rho_{a,b}\) with a different
\(\mathfrak h\)-module structure. Compactly supported Dolbeault forms
on a polydisk \(B\subset\C^2\), tensored with \(\mathfrak g\), realise
the holomorphic factorization algebra
\(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{hol}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}(B)=
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,cont}}
(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g)\), locally
constant in \(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\) and holomorphic in
\(\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\). Three locality differentials --- contact
\(\delta(t-t')\) between two brane-time points (with \(s\) reserved
for the transverse \(\R_s\) coordinate of \(X\)), topological
\(L_v=[Q,\iota_v]\) for \(v\in T\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\), and
formal-holomorphic
\(L_{\partial/\partial\bar z_i}=
[Q,\iota_{\partial/\partial\bar z_i}]\) --- structure the
factorization-current model on the brane line
(Theorem~\ref{thm:dict-kinematic-locality}).

\medskip
\noindent
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) on the
unreduced phase space. A four-face transgression --- algebraic
\(0\to\C\!\cdot\!1\to\C[z_1,z_2]\to\bar A\to 0\), Lie by cocycle
\(\bar c\), trace-algebraic boundary, and Weyl commutator
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\) with
\(\Phi_\hbar\colon\C[[z_1,z_2]]\to
\mathrm{Weyl}_\hbar(\mathfrak{gl}_N^{\oplus 2})\) the Weyl
quantization --- realises
\(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-scalar-anomaly-transgression}). Under Moyal
quantisation \cite{kontsevich-dq} this is a Capelli term
\cite{capelli,howe-capelli} and the first quantum master equation
obstruction \(W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N[\bar c]\) in the
scalar-reduced \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch; the unreduced,
opposite-extension, and balanced-supertrace branches kill it.

\medskip
\noindent
Compatibility of the Moyal star \(\star\), the Weyl quantization
\(\Phi_\hbar\), and the brane trace \(J\) holds componentwise:
weighted BV kernel convergence, balanced-supertrace scalar QME
cancellation, weighted Moyal principal-part current brackets, and a
finite-certificate degree-zero Hamiltonian trace comparison through
total degree~\(14\) in enumerated bidegrees
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison};
Proposition~\ref{prop:app-edge-pbw-telescoping}).

\medskip
\noindent
The radial/Weyl normal-form obstruction
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\), the
reduced non-scalar Costello QME class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\), the
unreduced \(P_0\)-factorization-centre obstruction vector
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\), and the strict
all-window Bochner--Martinelli current-transfer obstruction
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\) are obstruction theorems;
the \(\theta_3\) window has
\(H^1=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\ne 0\) bare and \(H^1=0\) under
supplied counterterm.

\end{abstract}
