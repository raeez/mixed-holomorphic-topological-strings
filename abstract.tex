\begin{abstract}

\noindent
In a formal Darboux neighborhood of a holomorphic-symplectic brane
point---the local sector the B-model topological string of
Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa~\cite{bcov} produces under
Costello--Li renormalization
\cite{costello-renormalization,costello-li-quantum-bcov,costello-li-open-closed-bcov}---the
local closed-open theory is the holomorphic-topological BF theory on
$X=\R^2_{t,s}\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}$ with field $\mathcal A$ and
antifield $\mathcal B$,
\[
  \mathcal A\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
    \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h,
  \quad
  \mathcal B\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
    \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes
    \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
   \quad
   \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1,
\]
and BF action
\(S=\int_X\langle\mathcal B,(d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\rangle\).  The residue pairing
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\operatorname{Res}_0\,f\omega$ realizes
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}$ as the Grothendieck--Matlis
principal-part module
$\mathcal P=H^2_{\mathfrak m}(A)\otimes\omega_A$,
$\omega_A=dz_1\wedge dz_2$ --- the holomorphic-surface analog of the
Beilinson--Drinfeld Heisenberg current on the formal disk.

\medskip\noindent
A stack of $N$ Dirac branes is the boundary condition along
$\R_t\subset X$ pinning a Chan--Paton matrix probe pair
$(\phi_1,\phi_2)\in(\mathfrak m_R\otimes\mathfrak{gl}_N)^2$ over an
Artin-local base $R$ (so that formal power series in $\phi_i$
terminate), with substitution $z_i\mapsto\phi_i$.  The moment-map
antifield $\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$, $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$,
realizes the relations $[\phi_1,\phi_2]=0$ as a derived zero fibre;
the substitution
$\rho_\phi\colon A=\C[[z_1,z_2]]\to\End(\C^N)$ is therefore a
continuous algebra homomorphism modulo the Koszul ideal, and the
open ghost-zero classical brane moduli is the derived commuting-pair
stack $[\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)/GL_N]$.  Adjoining a cyclic framing
vector $v\in\C^N$ with $\C[\phi_1,\phi_2]\cdot v=\C^N$ cuts out the
Hilbert scheme $\Hilb^N(\C^2)$; on this branch the Moyal/Weyl
deformation restricts to the Nekrasov--Schwarz noncommutative Hilbert
scheme.  A
Hamiltonian $f\in\mathfrak h=A/\C\cdot 1$ is measured on the brane as
the gauge-invariant trace character of its representation,
\[
  J(f)(\rho_\phi)
   =\overline{\operatorname{Tr}_V\,\rho_\phi(f)}
   =\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)} ,
\]
a regular function on the derived stack
$[\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)/GL_N]$, with values at a fixed
representation in $\C$ via the cyclic trace.  We prove $J$ is the
unique linear $GL_N$-equivariant Darboux-natural primitive
single-trace map
$\mathfrak h\to\mathcal O\bigl(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)\bigr)^{GL_N}$
satisfying scalar reduction, the Dirac descent, and linear
normalization $J(z_i)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_i}$.  On CE
generators of
$\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$
the reduced closed-to-open dictionary is
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f$ (Hamiltonian Schouten field) and
$u_f\mapsto O_f=J(f)$ (boundary observable).  Lurie--Francis
additivity identifies the target as the derived $E_1$-centre
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})$ of the brane
$E_1$-algebra; the chain-level lift to a closed-to-open derived-centre
map
\(\Phi_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk},0}\to
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\)
is conditional on vanishing of the obstruction vector
$\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}$ with chosen
null-homotopies.

\medskip\noindent
Closed Hamiltonian reduction quotients the constant Hamiltonian line
$\C\cdot 1$, on which $\operatorname{Tr}\,1=N$; the brane retains it
as the diagonal-$U(1)$ centre-of-mass cocycle of $N$ coincident
D-branes on noncommutative $\C^2_\hbar$.  The class
$\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$ has four
equivalent realizations: the Lie 2-cocycle
$\omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0$ on $\bar A$, the unreduced Poisson bracket
$\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N$, the Moyal
commutator
$[\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\cdot I$ in
$M_N(\C)$, and the diagonal-$U(1)$ central charge of the
noncommutative D-brane stack on $\C^2_\hbar$.  The strict scalar-reduced Hamiltonian
action upgrades to a projective action on the Capelli central
extension $0\to\C\to\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\to\bar A\to 0$.

\medskip\noindent
All-bidegree Weyl/trace compatibility is controlled by the radial
Weyl cokernel
$\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}$ on the
decorated PBW Stokes operator $B_{a,b}$, with the all-bidegree
vanishing $\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=0$ conditional on a
Harish-Chandra/Levasseur--Stafford radial-image input in the exact
Weyl/Capelli normalization
(Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}); the chain-level
$P_0$-factorization-centre lift of $\Phi_N$ by the
factorization-centre vector $\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}$;
the reduced non-scalar Costello QME by
$[\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\in
H^1\bigl(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I),\,d_{\mathrm{QME}}\bigr)$
in the filtered weighted brane-defect local-functional complex,
whose order-three row $\theta_3$ exhibits
$H^1_{\mathrm{bare}}=\C\!\cdot\!\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}$ killed by the
unique counterterm $d_M C_{\theta_3,M}=-\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}$,
$c=1$, in any finite window $M\ni\theta_3$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-theta-three-finite-window-H1}); and the strict
all-window Bochner--Martinelli current transfer by
$\operatorname{Ob}^\Pi_{\mathrm{BM}}$, nonzero on every finite Matlis
window $\mathcal P_{\le N}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:bmk-finite-window-no-go}), with coherent action on
the pro-Matlis substituted target
$\widehat{\mathcal P}_\Pi=\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:promatlis-universal-property}).  The local model
is the two-complex-dimensional analog of the Heisenberg chiral
algebra of Beilinson--Drinfeld, with $\mathcal P$ playing the role
of the Kac--Moody central pairing module $\Omega_K$ for a curve $K$
and $\mathfrak h$ that of regular functions; it is the formal local
sector of the Costello twisted-M-theory family
\cite{costello-twistedM}, and on its cyclic framed stable branch the
universal local probe of $\Hilb^N(\C^2)$ in its noncommutative Moyal
deformation.  Globalization to compact Calabi-Yau targets---BCOV
holomorphic anomaly, Gopakumar--Vafa, Donaldson--Thomas---transports
$\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}$ into the relevant compact-target deformation
complex; the classes above are the formal-local input.

\end{abstract}
