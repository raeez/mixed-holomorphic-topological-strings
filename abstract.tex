\begin{abstract}

\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line
\(L=\R_t\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\}\) is a finite-matrix microscope for the
formal symplectic disk \((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\): the
Chan--Paton substitution
\(z_i\rightsquigarrow\phi_i\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\) sends a closed
Hamiltonian \(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\) to the gauge-invariant brane
measurement
\[
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)} .
\]
This evaluation is literal, not representation-theoretic, and it forces
every other piece of open-side structure --- the Liouville form
\(\theta_N=\operatorname{Tr}(\phi_1d\phi_2)\), the moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\), the
first-class constraint \([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\), the Dirac action
\(S_\partial=\int_\R\operatorname{Tr}(\phi_1d\phi_2+A[\phi_1,\phi_2])\),
and the Koszul differential \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\).  The probe sees
the formal-local interface between Witten's Chern--Simons and BCOV
Kodaira--Spencer source data \cite{witten-cs,bcov} on
\(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\); no compact Calabi--Yau
hypothesis is invoked.

\medskip
\noindent
Constants generate the zero Hamiltonian vector field, so the closed
symmetry algebra is the Hamiltonian Lie algebra modulo constants
\[
  \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot1,
  \qquad
  \{f,g\}=\partial_{z_1}f\,\partial_{z_2}g
   -\partial_{z_2}f\,\partial_{z_1}g\pmod\C ,
\]
and a canonical-transformation BV theory promotes it to the shifted
cotangent extension
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\)
with closed observables
\(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak g)\).  The continuous
dual \(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module
\cite{matlis-injective}
\[
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong\mathcal P
    =\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\,\rho_{a,b},
  \qquad
  \rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1dz_2,
\]
paired with Taylor Hamiltonians by residue and carrying the
residue-coadjoint action
\(z_1^pz_2^q\cdot\rho_{a,b}=-(pb-qa+p-q)\rho_{a-p+1,b-q+1}\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}).  Cotangent
fields on a formal brane are residues, not polynomials: the polynomial
PBW classes \(\Psi_{a,b}\) carry the same labels and a different
\(\mathfrak h\)-module structure
(Corollary~\ref{cor:descendant-coadjoint-difference}).

\medskip
\noindent
The cochain CE/PV identity sends Hamiltonian-dual coordinates to
Schouten vector fields and shifted-cotangent-dual coordinates to
measured boundary Hamiltonians,
\[
  c^I\longmapsto\theta^I,
  \qquad
  u_I\longmapsto O_I ,
\]
proving that the completed CE cochains of \(\mathfrak g\), inside their
coordinate window, are isomorphic as a dg-commutative algebra to the
Schouten polyvectors of the boundary Hamiltonian polynomial algebra
\(\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\),
\(\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot1\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}); the brane
substitution is the image of \(u_f\), not of \(c_f\), and the
\(P_0\)-bracket and Maurer--Cartan kernel refinements hold on
exactly the topologised subalgebras \(B\subset\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{coord}}\)
for which the Schouten bracket and the diagonal kernel converge.
Hamiltonians smeared on a brane interval lift to factorisation
currents whose arity-two collisions reproduce the Hamiltonian Poisson
bracket and the residue-coadjoint action on \(\mathcal P\)
(Theorems~\ref{thm:hamiltonian-current-factorization},
\ref{thm:reduced-principal-part-boundary-current}).  Compactly
supported Dolbeault forms tensored with \(\mathfrak g\) realise the
native holomorphic factorization algebra
\[
  \mathcal F_{\mathrm{Ham}}^{\mathrm{hol}}(B)
   =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}
   \bigl(\Omega_c^{0,\bullet}(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g\bigr),
\]
locally constant in \(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\) and holomorphic in
\(\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\); a one-pair analytic pro-Matlis
Bochner--Martinelli--Koppelman retract \cite{laurent-thiebaut-bmk}
realises the arity-two output projection
(Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-window-bm-native-e2-transfer}).

\medskip
\noindent
Constants vanish on the closed side as Hamiltonian vector fields and
survive on the open side as the brane center of mass:
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) on the
unreduced phase space.  The mismatch is the distinguished class
\([\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)\), and Moyal quantisation
of the constant Poisson tensor \cite{kontsevich-dq} returns the same
scalar at order \(\hbar\) as the Capelli term
\cites{capelli,howe-capelli}: the first quantum master equation
obstruction in the ordinary scalar-reduced \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch
is
\[
  W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N\,[\bar c],
\]
cancelled by the unreduced central-character, opposite
central-extension, and balanced-supertrace source-replacement branches
(Theorems~\ref{thm:u1-center-anomaly},
\ref{thm:u1-center-anomaly-open},
\ref{thm:quantum-classical-anomaly}).
The Moyal star product preserves the reduced current comparison
componentwise --- weighted BV kernel convergence, balanced-supertrace
scalar QME cancellation on its habitat, weighted Moyal principal-part
current brackets, and a finite-certificate degree-zero Hamiltonian
trace comparison (Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison}).
The finite-certificate range covers the balanced bidegree diagonal
through \((6,6)\), every non-edge bidegree of total degree at most
\(13\), the total-degree-\(14\) bidegrees
\((3,11),(11,3),(4,10),(10,4),(5,9),(9,5)\), and the complete
one-moving-letter edge strips
(Proposition~\ref{prop:app-edge-pbw-telescoping}).

\medskip
\noindent
Four stronger statements remain exact obstruction-theory problems in
the sense of Costello renormalised BV \cite{costello-renormalization}
and Costello--Gwilliam factorization
\cite{costello-gwilliam}.

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{All-bidegree radial/Weyl normal-form vanishing.}  At each
\((a,b)\), failure of the Moyal/Weyl trace comparison to a
degree-zero Hamiltonian normal form is a single class
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\);
positive form is the decorated PBW Stokes identity
\(D^\square_{a,b}=C^+_{a,b}\partial_2\), failure exactly a signed row
\((\phi,-\lambda)\in\ker B_{a,b}^*\) with
\(\lambda(E^+_{a,b})-\phi(T_{a,b})\ne0\)
(Proposition~\ref{prop:app-radial-dual-potential-obstruction}).  The
first undecided cases are \((6,8),(8,6),(7,7)\).

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{Reduced non-scalar Costello quantum master equation.}  In the
filtered local-functional complex
\(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\), once a complete
\(d_{\mathrm{QME}}\)-stable scalar-contact filtration is supplied for
which the successive scalar-symbol extension classes vanish, the
filtered scalar-symbol projection
\(\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) is constructed and the reduced
non-scalar QME obstruction is the cohomology class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]
\in H^1(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},d_{\mathrm{QME}})\)
together with its finite-window
\(\varprojlim^1H^0\)-primitive-compatibility class
(Problem~\ref{prob:quantum-p0-operation-realization}).

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{Unreduced Tate-coefficient cotangent lift.}  The reduced cochain
CE/PV map promotes to the chain-level factorization-centre morphism
\[
  \Phi_{\mathrm{cl}}\colon
  \mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{closed},H}\big|_L
  \longrightarrow
  \mathrm Z^{P_0}_{\mathrm{fact}}\!
  \bigl(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{open},N}\bigr)
\]
exactly when the obstruction vector for product centrality,
\(P_0\)-centrality, brane--defect quantum master equation, and
\v{C}ech--Roos descent vanishes
(Problem~\ref{prob:formal-factorization-center}).

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{Strict native all-window Bochner--Martinelli current transfer.}
The native \(\C^2\) holomorphic factorization object admits a strict
support-local finite-current transfer to the brane line exactly when
the obstruction vector
\[
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}
   =(\operatorname{ob}_{\oplus},\operatorname{ob}_{I_\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{\mathfrak h,\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{collar},\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{3,\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{unif},\Pi})
\]
--- uniform moment restriction onto the direct-sum Matlis module, a
compact-current section of the pro-product tower, the full
Hamiltonian action on the chosen target, collar removal under
extension-by-zero factorization, the first transferred ternary
obstruction, and the \(\mathfrak m\)-adic uniform estimate ---
vanishes or is replaced by an admissible compact-current substitute.
The pro-Matlis retract gives the positive analytic data; strict
all-window transfer is exactly its named cokernel.

\end{abstract}
