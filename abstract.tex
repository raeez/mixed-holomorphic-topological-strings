\begin{abstract}

\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line of
\(X=\R_t\times\R_s\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}\) is a finite Chan--Paton
representation
\(\rho_\phi\colon A=\C[[z_1,z_2]]\to\End(\C^N)\),
\(z_i\mapsto\phi_i\), and the conjugation moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2]\) forces
\(\rho_\phi\) onto the commuting variety \([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\).  A
Hamiltonian on the formal symplectic normal polydisk
\((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) is measured on the brane as
the gauge-invariant trace character of its representation,
\[
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}.
\]
This measurement is unique under stable polynomial
\(GL\)-invariance, Dirac descent, scalar reduction, Darboux
equivariance under formal symplectomorphisms, primitive block-sum
additivity under brane stacking, and linear normalization on
\(z_1,z_2\).  The forcing is monomial-by-monomial: Procesi--Razmyslov
reduces stable invariants to cyclic trace words, adjacent-swap
exactness on the moment-map zero fibre collapses cyclic words to
commutative monomial traces, and Loday--Quillen--Tsygan single-trace
primitivity rules out every multi-trace correction.

\medskip\noindent
The closed BV fields take values in the shifted-cotangent Lie
algebra
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\),
\(\mathfrak h=A/\C\).  Continuous duality on the \(\mathfrak m\)-adic
Taylor topology identifies the cotangent module with the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module
\[
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong\mathcal P
  =\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\,\rho_{a,b},\qquad
  \rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1dz_2,
\]
paired with Taylor Hamiltonians by residue; principal parts are
defect momenta conjugate to sampling, not polynomial PBW shadows.
A Hamiltonian carries two avatars on \(\mathfrak g\).  The
generator-dual coordinate \(c_f\mapsto\theta_f\) acts as the
Hamiltonian Schouten vector field on representation moduli; the
cotangent-dual coordinate \(u_f\mapsto O_f=J(f)\) is the brane
measurement; the two are paired by the CE/PV ladder.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian is killed by reduction on the closed side
and survives as the brane centre of mass on the open side.  The
unreduced linear bracket
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) and the
Weyl/Moyal commutator
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\) detect the same
Lie 2-cocycle
\(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\); the
strict scalar-reduced Hamiltonian action is replaced by a projective
Hamiltonian action on the central extension
\(0\to\C\to\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\to\bar A\to0\) with cocycle
\(\hbar N\bar c\).

\medskip\noindent
Globalization, quantization, and chain-level upgrades are filtered
Maurer--Cartan transports of the local datum \(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}\)
into a target deformation complex \(\mathfrak K_{\mathcal T}\), with
master equation
\(F_{\mathcal T}(\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}+\tau)=0\).  Vanishing
curvature gives strict coherence; null-homotopy of the curvature
gives counterterm coherence; a central closed representative gives a
projective Hamiltonian action; a non-absorptive class forces target
replacement.  The named curvature classes are the radial cokernel
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\)
obstructing all-bidegree Weyl/trace compatibility; the unreduced
factorization-centre vector
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\) obstructing the
chain-level lift of the closed-to-open derived-centre map to a
strict \(P_0\)-centre map; the reduced Costello QME class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\), whose
cubic finite window \(\theta_3\) carries
\(H^1_{\mathrm{bare}}=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\) and is killed by the
unique counterterm \(dC=-E,\,c=1\); and the Bochner--Martinelli
class \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\), non-absorptive in
every finite Matlis window and selecting the pro-Matlis tower
\(\widehat{\mathcal P}_\Pi=\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}\) as the
coherent target replacement.

\end{abstract}
