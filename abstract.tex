\begin{abstract}

\noindent
Mixed holomorphic--topological string theory on
$X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$ --- the Costello
holomorphic-topological twist
\cite{costello-twistedM} of the Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa
B-twist
\cite{bcov} of the $\mathcal N=(2,2)$ worldsheet sigma model in the
Costello--Li Batalin--Vilkovisky (BV) / factorization renormalisation
\cite{costello-renormalization}\cite{costello-li-quantum-bcov}\cite{costello-li-open-closed-bcov}
--- has, at each formal holomorphic-symplectic Dirac brane vacuum,
a closed sector that coincides with the chiral Hochschild cochain
complex of the boundary brane algebra at that vacuum.  Its closed sector is the
holomorphic-topological Hamiltonian BF theory with fields
$\mathcal A\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h$ valued in the
reduced Hamiltonian Lie algebra
$\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1$ of the formal symplectic
polydisk, antifields $\mathcal B$ valued in the Grothendieck--Matlis
residue dual
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}
   =\operatorname{Ann}_{\Res}(\C\cdot1)\subset
    H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2$
paired by $\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0 f\omega$, and master action
\[
  S=\int_X\bigl\langle\mathcal B,\,
        (d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
        +\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\bigr\rangle .
\]
The open sector is a stack of $N$ Dirac branes on the worldline
$\R_t\subset\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}$ at the brane vacuum, with matrix transverse position
$(\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N$, Chan--Paton
substitution $z_i\mapsto\phi_i$ subject to the moment-map constraint
$[\phi_1,\phi_2]=0$, and antifield $\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$ with
$Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$, giving the Koszul resolution of the derived
commuting variety.

\medskip\noindent
At a brane vacuum $b\in\C^2$ with formal Darboux chart
$A_b=\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\C^2,b}\cong\C[[z_1,z_2]]$ and symplectic
form $\omega_b=dz_1\wedge dz_2$, the classical open algebra on $\R_t$
is the Chevalley--Eilenberg cochain complex
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
   =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}\bigl(\mathfrak{gl}_N,\,
       \mathrm{Sym}(\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
                    \oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N[1])\bigr),
\]
and a closed Hamiltonian $f\in\mathfrak h$ acts on this brane sector
through the trace
\[
  J\colon\mathfrak h\longrightarrow
      \mathcal O\bigl(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)\bigr)^{GL_N},
  \qquad
  J(f)=\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2),
\]
the unique $GL_N$-equivariant, primitive, single-trace,
Darboux-natural expression of $f$ on the derived commuting variety.
The closed observables form the mixed-HT factorization algebra of
Hamiltonian currents valued in CE chains of the shifted-cotangent BF
Lie algebra $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$,
with two-variable singular product on the diagonal $\Delta\subset\C^2_z\times\C^2_w$ of the Hamiltonian currents $\mathsf J_x(z)$, $x\in\mathfrak g$,
\[
  \mathsf J_x(z)\mathsf J_y(w)
   \sim K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)\,\mathsf J_{[x,y]_{\mathfrak g}}(w),
  \qquad
  K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)
    =\frac{d\zeta_1\,d\zeta_2}{\zeta_1\zeta_2},\quad
    \zeta_i=z_i-w_i,
\]
the two-complex-dimensional refinement of the Cauchy kernel
$K^{(1)}_C(z,w)=dz/(z-w)$ recovered from any chosen curve restriction
$C\hookrightarrow\C^2$ with its pushed-forward bracket and anomaly
matching.

\medskip\noindent
Inside the admissible filtered nuclear Tate / pro-Matlis /
Capelli-projective derived-centre category $\mathcal B^{\mathrm{adm}}$,
the formal-Darboux stalk identification at $N$ Dirac branes is
unconditional:
\[
  \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b
   \;\simeq\;
  C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}\!\bigl(
    \widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}\bigr)
   \;\simeq\;
  Z^{\mathrm{der,adm}}_{E_1}\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)
   \;\simeq\;
  HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{adm}}\!\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
                                   A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)
\]
as $E_2$-algebras, where
$\widehat{\mathfrak g}_{\mathrm{cent}}$ is the Capelli central
extension of $\mathfrak g$ absorbing the finite-$N$ scalar anomaly
as projective central charge $\hbar N[\bar c]$.  The bulk
restricted to the formal neighbourhood of $b$ is equated with the
brane-side derived $E_1$-centre by Costello--Gwilliam
bulk--boundary correspondence and the $E_1$-Hochschild
theorem; the trace
$J(f)$ is the formal Darboux coordinate expression of the
identification; the closed-to-open dictionary is the Koszul resolution
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f$, $u_f\mapsto J(f)$ with $\theta_f$ the Hamiltonian
Schouten derivation.  This is the formal-Darboux stalk of the chiral
$E_d$-Deligne conjecture at $d=2$
\cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian line $\C\cdot 1\subset\C[z_1,z_2]$, killed
on the closed side, acts on the brane as $c\mapsto c\cdot I$,
producing the finite-$N$ Capelli scalar in three equivalent forms
--- the Lie $2$-cocycle on $\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1$, the Poisson
bracket of trace coordinates, and the Moyal commutator with the
Weyl-symmetric quantisation $\Phi_\hbar\colon\C[z_1,z_2]\to M_N(\C)$:
\[
  \omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0,\qquad
  \{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N,\qquad
  [\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\cdot I.
\]
The three realise one class $\hbar N[\bar c]\in
H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$, equal to the projective
curvature $\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}$ of the modular line
bundle on the moduli stack at the trace generator,
\[
  \hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}.
\]

\medskip\noindent
The local model is the two-complex-dimensional residue-pairing
extension of the Heisenberg vertex algebra: the Kac--Moody central
pairing module $\Omega_K$ of a curve $K$ is the curve restriction of
the Matlis principal-part module
$\mathcal P=\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}$, regular functions on
$K$ are the curve restriction of $\mathfrak h$, and the curve residue $\Res_p$ is the curve restriction of the
two-variable Grothendieck residue $\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0
f\omega$, with central cocycle the Capelli scalar.  On the cyclic
framed stable branch $\C[\phi_1,\phi_2]\cdot v=\C^N$ the formal-Darboux
chart recovers the Nakajima ADHM presentation of $\mathrm{Hilb}^N(\C^2)$;
the Moyal/Weyl deformation of the brane Poisson algebra restricts to
the Nekrasov--Schwarz noncommutative Hilbert scheme
\cite{nekrasov-schwarz-noncommutative} with FI parameter $\hbar\cdot
I$ and $U(1)$ centre-of-mass charge $\hbar N$.  In the Costello
twisted-supergravity / M-theory family \cite{costello-twistedM},
the formal-Darboux stalk computes the local protected closed-string
algebra at a Dirac brane vacuum after admissible Hamiltonian
restriction.

\medskip\noindent
The formal-Darboux stalk theorem is the local fibre of a conjectured
global identification on the Kato--Nakayama log boundary
$\partial X^{\mathrm{KN}}$; the latter holds when the three-component obstruction
\[
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{global}}=
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{ch}\,E_2}_{\mathrm{Deligne}}\oplus
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{desc}}_{\partial}\oplus
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{QME}}_{\mathrm{all\mbox{-}loop}}
\]
vanishes: the chiral $E_2$-Deligne extension of the arity-two diagonal
kernel $K^{(2)}_\Delta$ to a chiral $E_2$-operad on all arities, the
$d_2$-differential of the Costello--Gwilliam descent spectral sequence
on an \'etale cover of $\partial X^{\mathrm{KN}}$, and the all-loop quantum
master-equation class of the closed-to-open coupling.  Each
component vanishes locally; the global gluing is conjectural.
Operator-level lifts of the level-$2$ modular section $\Phi_{10}$ on
$K3\times E$ (Borcherds square root $\Delta_5=\Phi_{10}^{1/2}$) and
the Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds comparison spectral sequence on
compact non-toric Calabi--Yau threefolds remain open.

\end{abstract}
