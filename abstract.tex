\begin{abstract}

\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line
\(L=\R_t\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\}\subset
X=\R_t\times\R_s\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}\) probes the formal symplectic
normal polydisk \((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) through a
matrix-valued transverse Chan--Paton coordinate
\((\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N^{\oplus 2}\).  A formal Hamiltonian
\(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\) is read by substitution and trace,
\[
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}
    \in\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A),\qquad
  \bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1,
\]
where \(\widehat{\mathbf S}\) is the completed symmetric algebra,
\(\bar A\) is the polynomial Hamiltonian subalgebra modulo constants,
and the bar removes the scalar trace \(\operatorname{Tr}I=N\) of any
constant Hamiltonian.  Constants generate the zero Hamiltonian vector
field, so \(J\) factors through the reduced Hamiltonian Lie algebra
\(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1\).  Among linear maps
\(\mathfrak h\to\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\) satisfying stable
\(GL_N\)-invariance, the Dirac constraint \([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\),
scalar reduction, Darboux equivariance under the formal
symplectomorphism group of \((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\), and
linear normalization on \(z_1,z_2\), trace substitution is the unique
survivor, fixed monomial-by-monomial by stable invariant theory and
the no-natural-correction lemma rather than by an algebra-homomorphism
extension (Theorem~\ref{thm:Jf-coordinate-uniqueness}).  The same
datum is terminal in the category of admissible protected Hamiltonian
sectors at a holomorphic-symplectic brane point; the obstruction to
membership of an external ambient datum is the four-component
universal-property class \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{univ}}_{p,X}\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-local-universal-property}).

\medskip
\noindent
The conjugation moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\) imposes
\([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\), and an odd matrix antifield
\(\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]\) with BV differential
\(Q\phi_i=0,\ Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\) realizes the moment-map ideal
as the Koszul derived zero fibre.  The closed BV sector takes its
fields in the shifted-cotangent extension
\[
  \mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
\]
on which a Hamiltonian \(f\in\mathfrak h\) carries two
Chevalley--Eilenberg coordinates: the generator-dual
\(c_f\in\mathfrak h^\vee[-1]\), producing the Hamiltonian Schouten
vector field \(\theta_f\) on \(\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\); and the
cotangent-dual \(u_f\in(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1])^\vee\),
producing the boundary observable \(O_f=J(f)\).  The CE/PV cochain
dictionary on generators is
\(c_f\mapsto\theta_f,\ u_f\mapsto O_f\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reduced-ce-pv-central-operation}).  Continuous
duality on the formal-disk topology identifies the cotangent module
with the Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module
\[
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong\mathcal P
    =\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\,\rho_{a,b},\qquad
  \rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1\,dz_2,
\]
paired with Taylor Hamiltonians by residue
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}).  The
weighted-Tate completion \(B_w\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat} is the smallest \(w\)-weighted
bracket-admissible RG-stable Tate completion in which the heat-kernel
propagator extends and the Casimir converges.

\medskip
\noindent
The unreduced linear-Hamiltonian Poisson bracket
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) records a
single Lie 2-cocycle: the closed--open mismatch class
\([\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)\) attached to the constant
Hamiltonian line, generating the zero vector field on the closed side
and the brane centre of mass on the open side.  The class \(\hbar N[\bar c]
\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\) is detected on four
faces --- algebraic central extension on the closed Taylor ring, Lie
cocycle on \(\bar A\), trace-algebraic boundary central extension on
the unreduced phase space, and the order-\(\hbar\) Weyl/Moyal
commutator
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\), with
\(\Phi_\hbar\) the Weyl-symmetric quantization map and \(\star\) the
Kontsevich--Moyal star product
\cite{capelli,howe-capelli,kontsevich-dq,matlis-injective}.  It
appears as the first scalar quantum-master-equation obstruction
\(W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N[\bar c]\) of the ordinary
scalar-reduced \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-scalar-anomaly-transgression}).

\medskip
\noindent
The Moyal star, the matrix Weyl algebra, and the brane trace agree
componentwise on a weighted target
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison}).
The decorated PBW Stokes operator \(B_{a,b}\) carries a per-bidegree
radial/Weyl normal-form cokernel
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\); the
unreduced \(P_0\)-factorization-centre lift is obstructed by the
four-component vector \(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\);
strict all-window support-local current transfer to the brane is
obstructed by \(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\); and the
reduced non-scalar Costello quantum-master-equation lane carries the
class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\in
 H^1(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},d_{\mathrm{QME}})\), with
\(\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) the filtered scalar projection and
\(d_{\mathrm{QME}}\) the QME differential on the
admissible-counterterm complex.  The class acquires its ambient
complex only once counterterm data is supplied; its first non-scalar
finite window is the cubic row \(\theta_3\), with bare verdict
\(H^1=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\) on the obstruction generator
\(E_{\theta_3}\), killed by the unique counterterm \(dC=-E,\ c=1\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-theta-three-finite-window-H1}).

\end{abstract}
