\begin{abstract}

\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line
\(L=\R_t\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\}\subset
X=\R_t\times\R_s\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}\) measures formal Hamiltonians
\(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\) on the symplectic normal polydisk
\((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) by substituting them into the
brane's matrix-valued transverse coordinate
\((\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N^{\oplus 2}\):
\[
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}
    \in\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A),\qquad
  \bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot1.
\]
This measurement is the unique \(GL_N\)-invariant, Dirac-constrained,
scalar-reduced, Darboux-natural, linearly-normalized map
\(\mathfrak h\to\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:Jf-coordinate-uniqueness}; categorical refinement
Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-local-universal-property}).

\medskip
\noindent
The conjugation moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\) imposes
\([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\); the Dirac antifield
\(\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]\) with \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\) is the
Koszul derived zero-fibre model for the moment-map equation.  The
closed sector takes its fields in the shifted-cotangent extension
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\)
with \(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot1\); a Hamiltonian \(f\) acts
through \(c_f\mapsto\theta_f\) and is measured through
\(u_f\mapsto O_f=J(f)\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reduced-ce-pv-central-operation}); continuous
duality identifies
\(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong\mathcal P=
 \bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b}\) with the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}); and the
weighted-Tate completion \(B_w\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat} is the minimal RG-stable
completion in which the heat-kernel propagator extends and the
Casimir converges.

\medskip
\noindent
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) on the
unreduced phase space is one Lie cocycle
\(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\) detected
on four faces (algebraic central extension, Lie cocycle, trace-algebraic
boundary, Weyl commutator
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\))
\cite{capelli,howe-capelli,kontsevich-dq,matlis-injective}; under
Moyal quantization it is the first scalar QME obstruction
\(W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N[\bar c]\) in the
\(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-scalar-anomaly-transgression}).  The Moyal star,
the matrix Weyl algebra, and the brane trace agree componentwise on a
weighted target
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison});
mismatches outside this product are exact obstruction theorems --- the
radial/Weyl normal form
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\), the
reduced non-scalar Costello QME class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\) with first
exposed row \(\theta_3\) (\(H^1=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\) bare,
vanishing under supplied counterterm), the unreduced
\(P_0\)-factorization-centre vector
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\), and the strict
all-window Bochner--Martinelli current-transfer obstruction
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\) --- each named cohomology
class blocking a stronger global, chain-level, or all-bidegree
quantum claim.

\end{abstract}
