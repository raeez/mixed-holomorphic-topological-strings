\begin{abstract}

\noindent
Inside the formal local model
\[
  X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}},
  \qquad
  L=\R_{\mathrm{brane}}\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\},
\]
a stack of \(N\) Dirac branes carries a finite slice of mixed
holomorphic-topological string field theory.  The holomorphic factor
enters only through its formal symplectic disk
\((\operatorname{Spf}\C[[z_1,z_2]],\,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) at the origin,
and the brane line \(L\) carries the topological factor.

The infinitesimal symmetries of the holomorphic-symplectic disk are
Hamiltonian vector fields, and constants have zero Hamiltonian vector
field; the closed Lie algebra controlling the slice is therefore the
Hamiltonian Lie algebra of the disk modulo constants,
\[
  \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1,
  \qquad
  \{f,g\}=
  \partial_{z_1}f\,\partial_{z_2}g
   -\partial_{z_2}f\,\partial_{z_1}g
   \pmod\C .
\]
A canonical-transformation BV theory has fields valued in the shifted
cotangent extension
\[
  \mathfrak g
    =\mathfrak h\ltimes
     \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
\]
the continuous dual \(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\) carrying the
cotangent-direction antifields, and the closed observables of this
slice are \emph{by definition} the continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg
cochains \(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak g)\); the
proved CE/PV identity below lives on the coordinate envelope
\(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{coord}}(\mathfrak g)\), the
two-complex-dimensional holomorphic-symplectic analogue of the
Kodaira--Spencer / BCOV target-space theory \cite{bcov}.

A stack of \(N\) branes on \(L\) records its position in the normal
disk through a probe pair of \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\)-valued matrices
\(\phi_1,\phi_2\); the moment map for \(GL_N\)-conjugation is
\(-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\), its first-class zero
locus the commuting-pair constraint \([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\).  The Dirac
open string-field-theoretic action is
\[
  S_\partial=\int_\R\operatorname{Tr}
    \bigl(\phi_1\,d\phi_2+A[\phi_1,\phi_2]\bigr),
  \qquad Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2],
\]
its ghost-zero BV algebra the Koszul zero-fibre of the constraint, its
gauge-invariant content the trace algebra
\(\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) for
\(f\in\mathfrak h\).

A bulk Hamiltonian \(f\in\mathfrak h\) is measured at the brane by
substitution into the probe pair,
\(f\mapsto\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\).  For a
finite-dimensional Lie algebra \(\mathfrak l\) the Schouten / coadjoint
identity
\[
  C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}(T^*[1]\mathfrak l)
    \cong
  \mathrm{PV}(\mathbf S(\mathfrak l))
\]
turns CE cochains into polyvectors of the symmetric algebra.  The
question of the manuscript is whether this identity, and its
descendant \(P_0\)-bracket, kernel, and Koszul-duality refinements,
survive the passage from finite-dimensional \(\mathfrak l\) to the
infinite-dimensional Hamiltonian disk algebra \(\mathfrak h\); and
where its survival is exactly an obstruction-theory problem in the
sense of Costello renormalised BV \cite{costello-renormalization} and
Costello--Gwilliam factorization \cite{costello-gwilliam}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Proved.}  The completed CE cochains of
\(\mathfrak g\), inside their coordinate window, are isomorphic as a
dg-commutative algebra to the Schouten polyvectors of the boundary
Hamiltonian polynomial algebra
\(\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\),
\(\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}); the dictionary
on generators sends each Hamiltonian-dual CE coordinate to a Schouten
vector-field coordinate, and each shifted-cotangent-dual CE coordinate
to a boundary Hamiltonian function on the probe pair.  The
\(P_0\)-bracket and Maurer--Cartan kernel refinements of this
dictionary are theorems on exactly the topologised subalgebras for
which the Schouten bracket and the diagonal kernel converge; the
strict ambient product completion supports neither.

The continuous dual of the disk Hamiltonian algebra is the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module \cite{matlis-injective},
the residue annihilator of the constants quotiented out of
\(\mathfrak h\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}):
\[
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong
  \mathcal P
   =\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b},
  \qquad
  \rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1dz_2,
\]
with residue-coadjoint action
\(z_1^pz_2^q\cdot\rho_{a,b}=-(pb-qa+p-q)\rho_{a-p+1,b-q+1}\).  The
polynomial trace classes that the brane probe sees realise instead
the PBW special-fibre shadow of this module; the separation persists
at all orders in \(\hbar\) once flatness, PBW linear-action, and
Moyal-coadjoint-target hypotheses are stated.

Hamiltonians smeared on a brane interval lift to factorisation
currents whose binary collisions reproduce the Hamiltonian Poisson
bracket and the residue-coadjoint action on \(\mathcal P\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:hamiltonian-current-factorization},
Theorem~\ref{thm:reduced-principal-part-boundary-current}); reduction
along the brane line and de Rham-current contraction returns the open
trace algebra.

The all-order algebraic deformation quantisation of the disk is the
Moyal star product \cite{kontsevich-dq}.  Its scalar-reduced trace
comparison to the closed CE/PV side splits into four independent
components -- weighted BV kernel convergence, the balanced
scalar-contact zero projection on its habitat, weighted Moyal
principal-part current brackets, and a finite-certificate degree-zero
trace comparison
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison}).
The finite-certificate range now reaches the balanced bidegree
diagonal through \((6,6)\), every non-edge bidegree of total degree
at most \(13\), and the total-degree-\(14\) bidegrees
\((3,11),(11,3),(4,10),(10,4),(5,9),(9,5)\); the one-moving-letter
edge strips close in closed form
(Proposition~\ref{prop:app-edge-pbw-telescoping}).

Compactly supported Dolbeault forms on a holomorphic polydisk
\(B\subset\C^2\), tensored with \(\mathfrak g\), form the dg-Lie input
of a holomorphic factorization algebra
\(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{hol}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}(B)
=C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}
(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g)\); its enhancement
\(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{mix}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}\) over
\(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\) is locally constant in the topological
direction and holomorphic in the transverse direction.  The
Bochner--Martinelli--Koppelman kernel \cite{laurent-thiebaut-bmk,
ruppenthal-bmk}, the finite Matlis moments, and the arity-two output
projection identifying binary collisions with the Hamiltonian bracket
and the residue coadjoint action on \(\mathcal P\) are constructed,
and assembled into a one-pair analytic pro-Matlis retract
(Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-window-bm-native-e2-transfer}).  Strict
native all-window finite-current transfer is exactly obstructed: the
residue-coadjoint formula applied to \(z_1^{N+2}\) on
\(\rho_{N+1,0}\) lands in \(-(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\), and the projected
finite-window Jacobiator is \((N+1)(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\).

The center-of-mass Poisson pair on the unreduced brane phase space
satisfies \(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\).
Killing it produces a one-dimensional central extension of
\(\mathfrak h\); its generator \([\bar c]\) is the scalar class
through which the \(U(1)\) center couples back to the closed Lie
algebra.  After Moyal quantisation the same scalar appears as a
Capelli term \cite{capelli, howe-capelli}: the first quantum master
equation obstruction in the ordinary scalar-reduced
\(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch is
\[
  W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N\,[\bar c],
\]
and its exact cancellation by the unreduced central-character,
opposite central-extension, and balanced-supertrace replacement
branches is proved.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Conjectural, with precise statement.}

\smallskip
\noindent\emph{(C1) All-bidegree radial/Weyl normal-form vanishing.}
At each bidegree \((a,b)\), failure of the Moyal/Weyl trace comparison
to a degree-zero Hamiltonian normal form is detected by a single
class \(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\) in the cokernel of the radial
PBW boundary operator at that bidegree
(Proposition~\ref{prop:app-radial-dual-potential-obstruction}).  The
conjecture is that
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}=0\) for every \((a,b)\), equivalently
that the decorated PBW Stokes identity holds at every bidegree.  The
first undecided total-degree-\(14\) cases are
\((6,8),(8,6),(7,7)\).

\smallskip
\noindent\emph{(C2) Reduced non-scalar Costello quantum master
equation.}  The non-scalar Costello QME on a brane interval lives in
a filtered local-functional complex
\(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\) with
quantum-master-equation differential \(d_{\mathrm{QME}}\).  Once a
complete \(d_{\mathrm{QME}}\)-stable scalar-contact filtration is
supplied for which the successive scalar-symbol extension classes
vanish, the filtered scalar-symbol projection
\(\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) is constructed, and the reduced
non-scalar QME obstruction is a single cohomology class
\[
  [\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]
  \in
  H^1\!\bigl(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},
    d_{\mathrm{QME}}\bigr).
\]
The conjecture is that this class vanishes by a compatible
counterterm recursion, together with its finite-window
\(\varprojlim^1H^0\)-primitive-compatibility class
(Problem~\ref{prob:quantum-p0-operation-realization}).

\smallskip
\noindent\emph{(C3) Unreduced Tate-coefficient cotangent lift.}  The
reduced cochain CE/PV map promotes to a chain-level morphism of
factorization algebras
\[
  \Phi_{\mathrm{cl}}\colon
  \mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{closed}}\big|_L
  \longrightarrow
  \mathrm Z^{P_0}_{\mathrm{fact}}\!
  \bigl(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{open},N}\bigr)
\]
on the unreduced (cotangent-included) sector, sending closed
observables on the brane line to the Poisson factorization centre of
the open brane observables; equivalently, the obstruction vector for
product centrality, \(P_0\)-centrality, brane--defect quantum master
equation, and \v{C}ech--Roos descent vanishes
(Problem~\ref{prob:formal-factorization-center}).

\smallskip
\noindent\emph{(C4) Strict native all-window Bochner--Martinelli
current transfer.}  The native \(\C^2\) holomorphic factorization
object admits a strict support-local finite-current transfer to the
brane line; equivalently the obstruction vector
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\) -- assembling uniform moment
restriction onto the direct-sum Matlis module, a compact-current
section of the pro-product tower, the full Hamiltonian action on the
chosen target, collar removal under extension-by-zero factorization,
the first transferred ternary obstruction, and the \(m\)-adic
uniform estimate -- vanishes or is replaced by an admissible
compact-current substitute.

\end{abstract}
