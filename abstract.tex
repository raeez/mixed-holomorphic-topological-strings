\begin{abstract}

\noindent
Around any holomorphic-symplectic brane point of any Calabi--Yau
threefold, the B-model topological string of
Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa \cite{bcov}, in its renormalized BV
form
\cite{costello-renormalization,costello-li-quantum-bcov,costello-li-open-closed-bcov},
reduces to a single universal local model: the
holomorphic-topological BF theory on $X=\R^2_{t,s}\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}$
with BV fields
\[
  (\mathcal A,\mathcal B)\in
  \Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)
   \widehat\otimes
   \bigl(\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\bigr),
   \qquad
   \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C,
\]
and BF action
\(S=\int_X\langle\mathcal B,(d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]\rangle\).  We work in the category of
Tate $\C$-vector spaces; the residue pairing
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0\,f\omega$ realizes
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}$ as the Grothendieck--Matlis
principal-part module
$\mathcal P=H^2_{\mathfrak m}(A)\otimes\omega_A$ --- the
holomorphic-surface analog of the Beilinson--Drinfeld Heisenberg
current.

\medskip\noindent
A stack of $N$ Dirac branes is the boundary condition along
$\R_t\subset X$ pinning $z_i\mapsto\phi_i\in\mathfrak{gl}_N$; the
conjugation moment-map antifield $\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$ realizes
$[\phi_1,\phi_2]=0$ as a derived zero fibre, the framed Hilbert
scheme $\Hilb^N(\C^2)$ in its noncommutative Moyal deformation.  The
universal local probe of bulk Hamiltonians is the gauge-invariant
trace character
\[
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}.
\]
We prove $J$ is the unique $GL$-equivariant Tate-cyclic-trace
functional $\mathfrak h\to\operatorname{HC}_0(\End\C^N)^{GL_N}$ that is
Darboux-natural, block-additive on stacking, and linear-normalized
$J(z_i)=\operatorname{Tr}\phi_i$.  The closed-to-open derived-centre
map
\(\Phi_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}\to
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\)
is Koszul duality between the bulk holomorphic $E_2$-algebra and the
brane $E_1$-algebra in the Costello sense, with reduced-coordinate
shadow $c_f\mapsto\theta_f$, $u_f\mapsto O_f=J(f)$.

\medskip\noindent
Closed Hamiltonian reduction kills $\operatorname{Tr}\,1=N$; the brane
records it as the trace-$U(1)$ centre-of-mass cocycle of $N$
coincident D-branes on noncommutative $\C^2_\hbar$.  The same class
$\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$ is detected
as a Lie 2-cocycle, the unreduced bracket
$\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N$, and the
Moyal commutator
$[\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N$.  The strict
scalar-reduced Hamiltonian action upgrades to a projective action on
the Capelli central extension
$0\to\C\to\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\to\bar A\to 0$.

\medskip\noindent
The local model is formal: the filtered $L_\infty$ algebra
$\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{loc}}$ controlling deformations of $\Phi_N$ has
a minimal model whose only nonzero higher brackets are four named
curvature classes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}$,
    the radial Weyl cokernel governing all-bidegree Weyl/trace
    compatibility;
  \item $\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}$, the
    factorization-centre vector governing the chain-level
    $P_0$-centre lift of $\Phi_N$;
  \item
    $[\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\in
     H^1\bigl(\mathcal O^{\mathrm{loc}}[[\hbar]],\,
              Q+\hbar\Delta_L+\{S_\infty,-\}\bigr)$,
    the reduced Costello QME class in local-functional cohomology,
    with cubic finite window $\theta_3$ exhibiting the prototype
    $H^1_{\mathrm{bare}}=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}$ killed by the unique
    counterterm $dC=-E$, $c=1$;
  \item $\operatorname{Ob}^\Pi_{\mathrm{BM}}\in
        \varprojlim^1_N\operatorname{HH}^\bullet(\mathcal P_{\le N})$,
    the Bochner--Martinelli class, nonzero in every finite Matlis
    window; the Hamiltonian factorization current acts coherently on
    the derived pro-Matlis tower
    $\widehat{\mathcal P}_\Pi=\mathbf{R}\!\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}$.
\end{itemize}

\medskip\noindent
The local matrix microscope is the holomorphic-2-dimensional analog
of the Heisenberg chiral algebra of Beilinson--Drinfeld, the
formal-symplectic local fixture of the Costello--Yagi
twisted-M-theory family, and the universal local probe of
$\Hilb^N(\C^2)$ in its noncommutative Moyal deformation.  It is the
local model in which the BCOV holomorphic anomaly equations and the
Gopakumar--Vafa / Donaldson--Thomas correspondences become tractable
in a future global theorem.

\end{abstract}
