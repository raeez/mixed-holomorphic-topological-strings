\begin{abstract}

\noindent
The B-model topological string of
Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa~\cite{bcov} produces, under
Costello--Li renormalization
\cite{costello-renormalization,costello-li-quantum-bcov,costello-li-open-closed-bcov},
a holomorphic-topological BF theory on a formal Darboux neighborhood
of a brane point.  On
$X=\R^2_{t,s}\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}$ the field and antifield are
\[
  \mathcal A\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
    \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h,
  \quad
  \mathcal B\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
    \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes
    \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
   \quad
   \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1,
\]
with action
$S=\int_X\langle\mathcal B,(d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\rangle$ and Grothendieck--Matlis
principal-part dual
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=\mathcal P=H^2_{\mathfrak m}(A)\otimes
dz_1\wedge dz_2$, $A=\C[[z_1,z_2]]$, $\mathfrak m=(z_1,z_2)$, paired by
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\operatorname{Res}_0 f\omega$.  This is the
holomorphic-surface analog of the Beilinson--Drinfeld Heisenberg
current on the formal disk.

\medskip\noindent
A stack of $N$ Dirac branes along $\R_t\subset X$ pins a
Chan--Paton substitution
$(\phi_1,\phi_2)\in(\mathfrak m_R\otimes\mathfrak{gl}_N)^2$ over an
Artin-local base $R$, $z_i\mapsto\phi_i$, with moment-map antifield
$\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$, $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$.
The open ghost-zero classical brane moduli is the derived
commuting-pair stack $[\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)/GL_N]$; adjoining a
cyclic framing vector $v$ with $\C[\phi_1,\phi_2]\cdot v=\C^N$ cuts out
$\Hilb^N(\C^2)$, on which the Moyal/Weyl deformation specialises to the
Nekrasov--Schwarz noncommutative Hilbert scheme.  A Hamiltonian
$f\in\mathfrak h$ is read on the brane as the cyclic trace
\[
  J(f)(\rho_\phi)
   =\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)},
\]
the unique linear $GL_N$-equivariant Darboux-natural primitive
single-trace map
$\mathfrak h\to\mathcal O\bigl(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)\bigr)^{GL_N}$
satisfying scalar reduction, the Dirac descent, and
$J(z_i)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_i}$.  On
Chevalley--Eilenberg (CE) generators of
$\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$
the reduced closed-to-open dictionary is
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f$, $u_f\mapsto O_f=J(f)$; Lurie--Francis additivity
identifies the target as the derived $E_1$-centre
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})$.  The chain-level
lift
$\Phi_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk},0}\to
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})$
is conditional on vanishing of
$\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}$ with chosen null-homotopies.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian line $\C\cdot 1$, on which
$\operatorname{Tr}1=N$, is removed from the closed Lie algebra and
retained on the brane as the diagonal-$U(1)$ centre-of-mass cocycle
of $N$ coincident D-branes on $\C^2_\hbar$.  The class
$\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$,
$\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1$, is at once
the Lie cocycle $\omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0$ on $\bar A$, the unreduced
bracket $\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N$, and
the Moyal commutator
$[\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\cdot I$ in $M_N(\C)$.
The strict scalar-reduced Hamiltonian action upgrades to a projective
action on the Capelli central extension
$0\to\C\to\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\to\bar A\to 0$.

\medskip\noindent
All-bidegree Weyl/trace compatibility is the radial Weyl cokernel
$\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}$ on the
decorated Poincar\'e--Birkhoff--Witt (PBW) Stokes operator, vanishing
on every bidegree under a Harish-Chandra / Levasseur--Stafford
radial-image input
(Statement~\ref{stmt:app-radial-external-input}).  The reduced
non-scalar Costello quantum-master-equation (QME) class
$[\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\in
H^1\bigl(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I),d_{\mathrm{QME}}\bigr)$
has order-three row $\theta_3$ with $H^1_{\mathrm{bare}}=
\C\!\cdot\!\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}$, killed in every finite window
$M\ni\theta_3$ by the unique counterterm
$d_M C_{\theta_3,M}=-\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-theta-three-finite-window-H1}).  The strict
all-window Bochner--Martinelli current transfer
$\operatorname{Ob}^\Pi_{\mathrm{BM}}$ is nonzero on every finite Matlis
window $\mathcal P_{\le N}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:bmk-finite-window-no-go}); coherence is restored on
the pro-Matlis substituted target
$\widehat{\mathcal P}_\Pi=\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:promatlis-universal-property}).

\medskip\noindent
The local model is the two-complex-dimensional analog of the
Beilinson--Drinfeld Heisenberg chiral algebra, with
$\mathcal P$ playing the role of the Kac--Moody central pairing module
$\Omega_K$ and $\mathfrak h$ the regular functions; it is the formal
local sector of the Costello twisted-M-theory family
\cite{costello-twistedM} and, on its cyclic framed stable branch, the
formal-Darboux stalk chart of $\Hilb^N(\C^2)$ in its noncommutative Moyal
deformation.

\end{abstract}
