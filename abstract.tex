\begin{abstract}

\noindent
The Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa B-model topological string
\cite{bcov} on $X=\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$,
renormalised in the sense of Costello--Li
\cite{costello-renormalization,costello-li-quantum-bcov,costello-li-open-closed-bcov},
is the holomorphic-topological Hamiltonian BF theory whose closed
sector consists of fields
$\mathcal A\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
\Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h$ valued in the
reduced Hamiltonian Lie algebra
$\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1$ of the formal symplectic
polydisk, antifields $\mathcal B$ valued in the
Grothendieck--Matlis residue dual
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}=
H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2$ paired by
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\Res_0 f\omega$, and master action
\[
  S=\int_X\bigl\langle\mathcal B,\,
        (d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
        +\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\bigr\rangle.
\]
The open sector is a stack of $N$ Dirac branes on the worldline
$\R_t\subset X$ with matrix transverse position
$(\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N$,
Chan--Paton substitution $z_i\mapsto\phi_i$ subject to the
moment-map constraint $[\phi_1,\phi_2]=0$, and antifield
$\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$ with $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$ resolving
the constraint derivatively.

\medskip\noindent
At a brane vacuum $b\in\C^2$ the formal Darboux chart is
$A_b=\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\C^2,b}\cong\C[[z_1,z_2]]$ with symplectic
form $\omega_b=dz_1\wedge dz_2$; the classical open-string algebra of
observables on $\R_t$ is the Chevalley--Eilenberg cochain complex
of the Chan--Paton gauge algebra with values in the symmetric
algebra of the matrix coordinates, the antifield, and the
moment-map antifield,
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
   =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}\bigl(\mathfrak{gl}_N,\,
       \mathrm{Sym}(\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
                    \oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N[1])\bigr),
\]
with Koszul differential $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$.  A closed
Hamiltonian $f\in\mathfrak h$ acts on this brane sector through
the trace map
\[
  J\colon\mathfrak h\longrightarrow
       \mathcal O\bigl(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)\bigr)^{GL_N},
  \qquad
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)},
\]
the unique $GL_N$-equivariant, primitive, single-trace,
Darboux-natural expression of $f$ on the derived commuting variety,
pinned by $J(z_i)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,\phi_i}$.

\medskip\noindent
The closed observables form the mixed-HT factorization algebra
$\mathcal F^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}$ valued in CE chains of
the shifted-cotangent BF Lie algebra
$\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$;
its pushforward along $\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}
\to\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$ is a holomorphic factorization algebra
$\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ on $\C^2$ valued in
$E_2$-algebras.  This is a two-complex-dimensional chiral algebra of
Hamiltonian currents: it factorises over the holomorphic surface, as a
chiral algebra factorises over a curve.  Its holomorphic OPE is a
two-variable expansion on the diagonal
$\Delta\subset\C^2_z\times\C^2_w$: for $x\in\mathfrak g$ the
linear holomorphic current $\mathsf J_x(z)$ in the CE
factorization algebra
$B\mapsto C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}
(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g)$ satisfies
\[
  \mathsf J_x(z)\mathsf J_y(w)
  \sim K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)\,\mathsf J_{[x,y]_{\mathfrak g}}(w),
  \qquad
  K^{(2)}_\Delta(z,w)
   =\frac{d\zeta_1\,d\zeta_2}{\zeta_1\zeta_2},
  \quad \zeta_i=z_i-w_i,
\]
with the diagonal local-cohomology principal-part module
$H^2_\Delta(\C[[z_1,z_2,w_1,w_2]])\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2$ as its
singular coefficient module.  Restricting along a holomorphic curve
$C\hookrightarrow\C^2$ specialises this two-variable kernel to the
one-variable curve Cauchy kernel
$K^{(1)}_C(z,w)=dz/(z-w)$ and produces a chiral algebra on \(C\),
equivalently a vertex algebra after choosing a formal coordinate.  Its
generators and relations are the one-variable specialisation of the
two-dimensional OPE.

\medskip\noindent
The Mixed Holomorphic--Topological Deligne conjecture identifies
$\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}$ with the mixed-HT chiral
derived $E_2$-centre of the boundary open factorisation category
$\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}_\partial$ on the Kato--Nakayama log
boundary $\partial X^{\mathrm{KN}}$ --- the two-complex-dimensional
extension of the \(d=1\) chiral Deligne theorem for chiral algebras
on algebraic curves \cite{beilinson-drinfeld-chiral}*{\S 4}.  This
manuscript proves the
formal-Darboux stalk identification of that conjecture at $N$ Dirac
branes over the Heisenberg-core vacuum $b$:
\[
  \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b
   \;\simeq\;
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
   \;\simeq\;
  HH^\bullet\bigl(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
                 A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}\bigr)
\]
as $E_2$-algebras --- the bulk restricted to the formal
neighbourhood of $b$, equated with the brane-side derived
$E_1$-centre (equivalently the $E_1$-Hochschild cochain complex) by
Costello--Gwilliam factorization-algebra bulk--boundary
correspondence and Deligne's $E_1$-Hochschild theorem.  The trace
map $J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}$ is the
formal Darboux coordinate expression of this identification; the
closed-to-open dictionary is the Koszul resolution
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f\in HH^1$, $u_f\mapsto J(f)\cdot(-)\in HH^0$, with
$\theta_f$ the Hamiltonian Schouten derivation determined by $f$.

\medskip\noindent
The constant Hamiltonian line $\C\cdot 1\subset\C[z_1,z_2]$ lies
in the kernel of the closed Hamiltonian Lie algebra reduction but
acts on the brane as $c\mapsto c\cdot I$, producing the finite-$N$
central term.  With $\Phi_\hbar\colon\C[z_1,z_2]\to M_N(\C)$ the
Weyl-symmetric quantisation map and $\star$ the Moyal star-product,
this term has three equivalent expressions:
\[
  \omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0,\qquad
  \{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N,\qquad
  [\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\cdot I,
\]
the Lie 2-cocycle on $\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1$, the Poisson
bracket of trace coordinates, and the Moyal commutator in
$M_N(\C)$.  They realise one class
$\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$ --- the
Capelli scalar of the trace centre-of-mass and the projective
curvature $\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}$ of the modular line
bundle on the period domain at the trace generator,
\[
  \hbar N[\bar c]=\Omega_{\mathrm{central}}|_{J(f)}.
\]

\medskip\noindent
The local model is the two-complex-dimensional analogue of the
Heisenberg vertex algebra, with $\mathcal P$ playing the role of
the Kac--Moody central pairing module $\Omega_K$ for a curve $K$
and $\mathfrak h$ that of regular functions; it is the formal
local sector of the Costello twisted-M-theory family
\cite{costello-twistedM}; and on its cyclic framed stable branch
$\C[\phi_1,\phi_2]\cdot v=\C^N$ it is the formal-Darboux stalk
chart of $\mathrm{Hilb}^N(\C^2)$ in its Nekrasov--Schwarz
noncommutative Moyal deformation
\cite{nekrasov-schwarz-noncommutative}.

\medskip\noindent
The formal-Darboux stalk theorem is the tangent vector to a global
identification.  The chiral $E_d$-Deligne conjecture at $d\geq 2$,
the operator-level lift of the modular line on $K3\times E$, and
the Hall--Drinfeld--Borcherds comparison on compact non-toric
Calabi--Yau threefolds are three open problems forced by the local
theorem and recorded as named obstruction classes in the body.

\end{abstract}
