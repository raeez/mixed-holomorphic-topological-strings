\begin{abstract}

\noindent
We study the formal local mixed holomorphic-topological theory on
\(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\) whose holomorphic
fields are Hamiltonians on the formal symplectic disk.  The reduced
Hamiltonian Lie algebra is
\[
  \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1,
  \qquad
  \omega=dz_1\wedge dz_2,
\]
and the shifted-cotangent BF Lie algebra is
\[
  \mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
  \qquad
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}
  =H^2_{(z_1,z_2)}(\C[[z_1,z_2]])\,dz_1dz_2 .
\]
The pairing is the Grothendieck residue pairing.  The classical
action is
\[
  S=\int
  \Bigl\langle\mathcal B,\,
  (d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
  +\frac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\Bigr\rangle .
\]

\medskip\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the worldline \(\R_t\) has
Chan--Paton coordinates
\[
  (\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\oplus\mathfrak{gl}_N
\]
and the derived moment-map equation
\[
  Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2],\qquad \psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1].
\]
Thus the classical boundary algebra is
\[
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N}
  =
  C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE}}
  \bigl(\mathfrak{gl}_N,\mathrm{Kosz}([\phi_1,\phi_2])\bigr),
\]
the Chevalley--Eilenberg complex for the quotient stack
\([\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)/\mathrm{GL}_N]\).  At a brane vacuum
\(b\) the formal Darboux chart is
\[
  A_b=\widehat{\mathcal O}_{\C^2,b}\cong\C[[z_1,z_2]] .
\]

\medskip\noindent
The main local theorem identifies the formal-Darboux stalk of the
bulk factorization algebra with the brane-side derived \(E_1\)-centre:
\[
  \mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk}}|_b
  \simeq
  Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
  \simeq
  HH^\bullet(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N},
  A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})
\]
as \(E_2\)-algebras.  In formal Darboux coordinates the closed-to-open
map is
\[
  c_f\longmapsto\theta_f,\qquad
  u_f\longmapsto J(f),\qquad
  J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)} ,
\]
where \(\theta_f\) is the Hamiltonian Schouten derivation.  The trace
map \(J\) is the normalised \(GL_N\)-invariant single-trace function
on the derived commuting variety.

\medskip\noindent
The bulk object is a holomorphic factorization algebra on \(\C^2\),
not a chiral algebra on a curve and not a vertex operator algebra.
Its arity-two singular product is a formal diagonal local-cohomology
operation with coefficient module
\[
  H^2_\Delta
  \bigl(\C[[z_1,z_2,w_1,w_2]]\bigr)\,d\zeta_1d\zeta_2,
  \qquad
  \zeta_i=z_i-w_i .
\]
A one-dimensional chiral algebra appears only after an additional
restriction or pushforward to a curve, with a pushed-forward bracket,
brane image, BV pairing, and anomaly normalisation.  Affine
Kac--Moody, \(\beta\gamma\), Virasoro, and \(W\)-algebra comparisons
are therefore separate curve-chiral problems, not consequences of the
formal-Darboux theorem.

\medskip\noindent
Quantisation retains the constant Hamiltonian line on the brane even
though it is removed from \(\mathfrak h\).  The same order-\(\hbar\)
class is seen as
\[
  \omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0,\qquad
  \{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N,\qquad
  [\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\cdot I .
\]
It defines
\[
  \hbar N[\bar c]\in
  H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot 1,\C)[[\hbar]],
\]
the finite-\(N\) Capelli scalar of the trace algebra.  It is not a
Kac--Moody level, a Virasoro central charge, or a modular form without
a separate anomaly-matching theorem.

\medskip\noindent
The remaining parts of the manuscript formulate the obstruction
calculus for extending this formal local statement: descent,
quantisation, all-bidegree Weyl/Moyal compatibility, pro-Matlis
coherence, and global compact-target comparisons.  Compact
Calabi--Yau threefolds, Igusa modular forms, Borcherds--Kac--Moody
denominators, and large-\(N\) \(W\)-algebra limits enter only as
matched-conventions comparison problems beyond the formal-Darboux
stalk.

\end{abstract}
