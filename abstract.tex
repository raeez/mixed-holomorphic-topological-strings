\begin{abstract}

\noindent
The formal local closed-open Hamiltonian sector near a Dirac brane is
governed by a single measurement principle: a closed Hamiltonian on
the formal symplectic normal polydisk is evaluated on the brane's
matrix-valued transverse coordinate. The brane does not represent the
Hamiltonian algebra abstractly; it literally evaluates Hamiltonians on
that coordinate.

\medskip
\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line
\(L=\R_t\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\}\subset
X=\R_t\times\R_s\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}\) probes the formal symplectic
polydisk \((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\) through a commuting
pair \(\phi_1,\phi_2\in\mathfrak{gl}_N\). A closed Hamiltonian
\(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\) is measured on the brane by literal substitution
\(J(f)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\), the unique
\(GL_N\)-invariant Darboux-natural scalar-reduced measurement
(Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-local-universal-property}). The conjugation
moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\) imposes
the constraint \([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\). To resolve it homologically,
introduce a fresh odd matrix-valued generator
\(\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]\), the BV antifield of the Lagrange
multiplier \(A\) in the Dirac action
\(S_\partial=\int_\R\operatorname{Tr}(\phi_1\,d\phi_2+
A[\phi_1,\phi_2])\); the BV differential \(Q\) acts by
\(Q\phi_1=Q\phi_2=0\), \(Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]\), and the trace
algebra \(\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) for
\(f\in\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot1\) is the ghost-zero
\(GL_N\)-invariant \(Q\)-cohomology --- the Koszul resolution of the
constraint ideal.

\medskip
\noindent
\emph{Closed BV theory and the CE/PV dictionary.} Constants kill the
Hamiltonian vector field, so the closed symmetry algebra is the
Hamiltonian Lie algebra modulo constants
\(\mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1\) with Poisson bracket; the
canonical-transformation BV theory takes its fields in the shifted
cotangent extension \(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes
\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\), with closed observables the
continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains
\(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,cont}}(\mathfrak g)\). A Hamiltonian \(f\) has
two avatars: the Hamiltonian-dual CE coordinate \(c_f\) acting through
the Schouten vector field \(\theta_f\), and the
shifted-cotangent-dual coordinate \(u_f\) measured on the brane as the
boundary observable \(O_f=J(f)\). The cochain map
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{coord}}\colon
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,coord}}(\mathfrak g)\xrightarrow{\sim}
\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\) is an isomorphism with the Schouten
polyvectors of \(\bar A\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}); the
\(P_0\)-bracket and Maurer--Cartan kernel refinements hold on the
topologised subalgebras for which they converge --- the minimal
RG-stable habitat is the weighted-Tate completion \(B_w\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}).

\medskip
\noindent
\emph{Matlis residues; native two-complex-dimensional habitat.} A
Taylor Hamiltonian is what the brane samples; a principal part is the
residue functional detecting a Taylor coefficient. The continuous dual
\(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong
\mathcal P=\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b}\),
\(\rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1dz_2\), is the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module \cite{matlis-injective}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}); the
polynomial PBW classes \(\Psi_{a,b}\) share labels with the residues
\(\rho_{a,b}\) but a different \(\mathfrak h\)-module structure.
Compactly supported Dolbeault forms on a polydisk \(B\subset\C^2\),
tensored with \(\mathfrak g\), realise the native holomorphic
factorization algebra
\(\mathcal F^{\mathrm{hol}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}(B)=
C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE,cont}}
(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g)\), locally
constant in \(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\) and holomorphic in
\(\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\) --- not a one-dimensional vertex / OPE / Zhu /
curve chiral algebra (Beilinson--Drinfeld 2004); a curve-chiral
reduction requires a separately named restriction datum. Three
locality differentials --- contact \(\delta(t-s)\), topological
\(L_v=[Q,\iota_v]\) for \(v\in T\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\), and
formal-holomorphic
\(L_{\partial/\partial\bar z_i}=
[Q,\iota_{\partial/\partial\bar z_i}]\) --- force the
factorization-current model on the brane line
(Theorem~\ref{thm:dict-kinematic-locality}).

\medskip
\noindent
\emph{Center-of-mass anomaly.} Constants vanish closed-side and
survive open-side as the brane center of mass:
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) on the
unreduced phase space. A four-face transgression --- algebraic, Lie
(by cocycle \(\bar c\)), trace-algebraic boundary, and Weyl commutator
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\) (with
\(\Phi_\hbar\) the Weyl quantization of the brane coordinate) ---
realises
\(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-scalar-anomaly-transgression}). After Moyal
quantisation \cite{kontsevich-dq} this is a Capelli term
\cite{capelli,howe-capelli} and the first QME obstruction
\(W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N[\bar c]\) in the ordinary
scalar-reduced \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch, cancelled by the unreduced,
opposite-extension, and balanced-supertrace replacement branches.

\medskip
\noindent
\emph{Moyal quantization is a brane-measurement compatibility test.}
Compatibility of the Moyal star \(\star\), the Weyl quantization
\(\Phi_\hbar\), and the brane trace measurement \(J\) is proved
componentwise: weighted BV kernel convergence, balanced-supertrace
scalar QME cancellation, weighted Moyal principal-part current
brackets, and a finite-certificate degree-zero Hamiltonian trace
comparison through total degree~\(14\) in enumerated bidegrees
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison};
Proposition~\ref{prop:app-edge-pbw-telescoping}) --- not a completed
quantum theory.

\medskip
\noindent
\emph{Stronger claims are obstruction problems.} The radial/Weyl
normal-form obstruction
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\), the
reduced non-scalar Costello QME class
\([\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\), the
unreduced \(P_0\)-factorization-centre obstruction vector
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}\), and the strict native
all-window Bochner--Martinelli current-transfer obstruction
\(\operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}\) are proved obstruction
theorems; the \(\theta_3\) window has bare
\(H^1=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\ne 0\) and \(H^1=0\) under supplied
counterterm. Proved/conditional/not-claimed status appears in the
front-matter theorem table on page~5.

\end{abstract}
