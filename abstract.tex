\begin{abstract}

\noindent
At an admissible formal Darboux Hamiltonian sector around a
holomorphic-symplectic brane point---the protected local datum that
the B-model topological string of Bershadsky--Cecotti--Ooguri--Vafa
\cite{bcov} produces in its renormalized BV form
\cite{costello-renormalization,costello-li-quantum-bcov,costello-li-open-closed-bcov}---the
formal local closed-open theory is the holomorphic-topological BF
theory on $X=\R^2_{t,s}\times\C^2_{z_1,z_2}$ with BV connection
$\mathcal A$ and cotangent partner $\mathcal B$,
\[
  \mathcal A\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
    \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h,
  \quad
  \mathcal B\in\Omega^\bullet(\R^2)\widehat\otimes
    \Omega^{0,\bullet}(\C^2)\widehat\otimes
    \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1],
   \quad
   \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1,
\]
and BF action
\(S=\int_X\langle\mathcal B,(d+\bar\partial)\mathcal A
+\tfrac12[\mathcal A,\mathcal A]_{\mathfrak h}\rangle\).  The residue pairing
$\langle f,\omega\rangle=\operatorname{Res}_0\,f\omega$ realizes
$\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}$ as the Grothendieck--Matlis
principal-part module
$\mathcal P=H^2_{\mathfrak m}(A)\otimes\omega_A$,
$\omega_A=dz_1\wedge dz_2$ --- the holomorphic-surface analog of the
Beilinson--Drinfeld Heisenberg current on the formal disk.

\medskip\noindent
A stack of $N$ Dirac branes is the boundary condition along
$\R_t\subset X$ pinning a Chan--Paton matrix probe pair
$(\phi_1,\phi_2)\in\mathfrak{gl}_N^2$ with substitution
$z_i\mapsto\phi_i$.  The moment-map antifield
$\psi\in\mathfrak{gl}_N[1]$, $Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2]$, realizes the
relations $[\phi_1,\phi_2]=0$ as a derived zero fibre; the substitution
$\rho_\phi\colon A=\C[[z_1,z_2]]\to\End(\C^N)$ is therefore an algebra
homomorphism modulo the Koszul ideal, and the open ghost-zero
classical brane moduli is the derived commuting-pair stack
$[\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)/GL_N]$; its cyclic framed stable locus,
after a framing vector $v\in\C^N$ with $\C[\phi_1,\phi_2]\cdot v=\C^N$,
is the Hilbert scheme $\Hilb^N(\C^2)$, and the Moyal/Weyl deformation
restricts to its noncommutative incarnation on this branch.  A
Hamiltonian $f\in\mathfrak h=A/\C\cdot 1$ is measured on the brane as
the gauge-invariant trace character of its representation,
\[
  J(f)(\rho_\phi)
   =\overline{\operatorname{Tr}_V\,\rho_\phi(f)}
   =\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\,f(\phi_1,\phi_2)} ,
\]
a regular function on the derived stack; the value at a fixed
representation factors through
$\operatorname{HC}_0(\End\C^N)\cong\C$.  We prove $J$ is the unique
linear $GL_N$-equivariant Darboux-natural primitive single-trace map
$\mathfrak h\to\mathcal O\bigl(\mu^{-1}_{\mathrm{der}}(0)\bigr)^{GL_N}$
satisfying scalar reduction, the Dirac descent, and linear
normalization $J(z_i)=\overline{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_i}$.  The closed-to-open derived-centre
map
\(\Phi_N\colon\mathcal A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{bulk},0}\to
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{E_1}(A^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\partial,N})\)
is Koszul duality between the bulk holomorphic $E_2$-algebra and the
brane $E_1$-algebra in the Costello sense, with reduced-coordinate
shadow on the CE generators of
$\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]$:
$c_f\mapsto\theta_f$ (Hamiltonian Schouten field), $u_f\mapsto
O_f=J(f)$ (boundary observable).

\medskip\noindent
Closed Hamiltonian reduction quotients the constant Hamiltonian line
$\C\cdot 1$, on which $\operatorname{Tr}\,1=N$; the brane retains it
as the trace-$U(1)$ centre-of-mass cocycle of $N$ coincident D-branes
on noncommutative $\C^2_\hbar$.  The class
$\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]$ has four
equivalent realizations: the Lie 2-cocycle
$\omega(f,g)=[\{f,g\}]_0$ on $\bar A$, the unreduced Poisson bracket
$\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N$, the Moyal
commutator
$[\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\cdot I$ in
$M_N(\C)$, and the trace-$U(1)$ central charge of the noncommutative
D-brane stack on $\C^2_\hbar$.  The strict scalar-reduced Hamiltonian
action upgrades to a projective action on the Capelli central
extension $0\to\C\to\mathfrak h_{\hbar,N}\to\bar A\to 0$.

\medskip\noindent
The four upgrade directions investigated here---all-bidegree
Weyl/radial normal form, chain-level $P_0$-factorization-centre lift,
reduced non-scalar Costello QME, and strict all-window
Bochner--Martinelli current transfer---each carry a named curvature
component in the filtered $L_\infty$ algebra $\mathfrak K_{\mathrm{loc}}$
controlling deformations of $\Phi_N$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}$,
    the radial Weyl cokernel governing all-bidegree Weyl/trace
    compatibility;
  \item $\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}$, the
    factorization-centre vector governing the chain-level
    $P_0$-centre lift of $\Phi_N$;
  \item
    $[\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]\in
     H^1\bigl(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I),\,d_{\mathrm{QME}}\bigr)$,
    the reduced Costello QME class in the filtered weighted
    brane-defect local-functional complex
    (Problem~\ref{prob:quantum-p0-operation-realization}), with
    order-three CE-source row $\theta_3$ exhibiting the prototype
    $H^1_{\mathrm{bare}}=\C\!\cdot\!\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}$ killed by
    the unique counterterm $d_M C_{\theta_3,M}=-\mathsf E_{\theta_3,M}$,
    $c=1$, in any finite window $M\ni\theta_3$;
  \item $\operatorname{Ob}^\Pi_{\mathrm{BM}}$, the
    Bochner--Martinelli class, with non-vanishing per-window
    representative in every finite Matlis window $\mathcal P_{\le N}$
    (Theorem~\ref{thm:bmk-finite-window-no-go}); the Hamiltonian
    factorization current acts coherently on the substituted target
    $\widehat{\mathcal P}_\Pi=\varprojlim_N\mathcal P_{\le N}$
    (Theorem~\ref{thm:promatlis-universal-property}).
\end{itemize}

\medskip\noindent
The local matrix microscope is the holomorphic-2-dimensional analog
of the Heisenberg chiral algebra of Beilinson--Drinfeld, with
$\mathcal P$ in the role of the Kac--Moody central pairing module
$\Omega_K$ for a curve $K$ and $\mathfrak h$ in the role of regular
functions; the formal-symplectic local fixture of the Costello
twisted-M-theory family \cite{costello-twistedM}; and, on its cyclic framed stable branch, the
universal local probe of $\Hilb^N(\C^2)$ in its noncommutative Moyal
deformation.  Global compact-target lifts---BCOV holomorphic anomaly,
Gopakumar--Vafa, Donaldson--Thomas---proceed by transporting the local
Maurer--Cartan element $\Theta_{\mathrm{loc}}$ into the corresponding
compact-target deformation complex; the obstruction calculus given
above is the formal-Darboux input.

\end{abstract}
