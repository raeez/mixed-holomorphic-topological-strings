\begin{abstract}

\noindent
A stack of \(N\) Dirac branes on the topological line
\(L=\R_t\times\{s=z_1=z_2=0\}\) is a finite-matrix microscope for the
formal symplectic polydisk
\((\widehat{\C^2}_0,dz_1\wedge dz_2)\).  The brane carries a commuting
pair of \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\)-valued probe matrices \(\phi_1,\phi_2\),
and a closed Hamiltonian \(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]\) is measured on it by
literal substitution into the probe pair,
\[
  J(f)
   =\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)} .
\]
This is not an action through abstract representation theory; it is
the only gauge-invariant measurement a holomorphic Hamiltonian on the
formal normal polydisk admits on a brane whose transverse coordinate is
matrix-valued
(Theorem~\ref{thm:formal-local-universal-property}).  The same
evaluation forces the entire open-side architecture: the Liouville
form \(\theta_N=\operatorname{Tr}(\phi_1d\phi_2)\), the moment map
\(\mu(\epsilon)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\epsilon[\phi_1,\phi_2])\), the
first-class constraint \([\phi_1,\phi_2]=0\), the Dirac action
\[
  S_\partial=\int_\R\operatorname{Tr}
    \bigl(\phi_1\,d\phi_2+A[\phi_1,\phi_2]\bigr),
  \qquad
  Q\psi=[\phi_1,\phi_2],
\]
the Koszul zero-fibre of the constraint, and the gauge-invariant
trace algebra \(\overline{\operatorname{Tr}f(\phi_1,\phi_2)}\) for
\(f\in\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot1\).

\medskip
\noindent
Constants generate the zero Hamiltonian vector field, so the closed
symmetry algebra is the Hamiltonian Lie algebra modulo constants
\[
  \mathfrak h=\C[[z_1,z_2]]/\C\cdot 1,
  \qquad
  \{f,g\}=
  \partial_{z_1}f\,\partial_{z_2}g
   -\partial_{z_2}f\,\partial_{z_1}g
   \pmod\C ,
\]
and the canonical-transformation BV theory has fields valued in the
shifted cotangent extension
\(\mathfrak g=\mathfrak h\ltimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}[1]\),
with closed observables the continuous Chevalley--Eilenberg cochains
\(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}(\mathfrak g)\); the
coordinate envelope
\(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{coord}}(\mathfrak g)\) is the
habitat of the CE/PV identity.

\medskip
\noindent
The continuous dual \(\mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is the
Grothendieck--Matlis principal-part module
\cite{matlis-injective}, the residue annihilator of the constants
quotiented out of \(\mathfrak h\),
\[
  \mathfrak h^\vee_{\mathrm{cont}}\cong
  \mathcal P
   =\bigoplus_{a+b>0}\C\rho_{a,b},
  \qquad
  \rho_{a,b}=z_1^{-a-1}z_2^{-b-1}\,dz_1dz_2,
\]
paired with Taylor Hamiltonians by residue and carrying the
residue-coadjoint action
\(z_1^pz_2^q\cdot\rho_{a,b}=-(pb-qa+p-q)\rho_{a-p+1,b-q+1}\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:principal-part-coadjoint-uniqueness}).  Cotangent
fields on a formal brane are residues, not polynomials: the polynomial
PBW classes \(\Psi_{a,b}\) carry the same labels and a different
\(\mathfrak h\)-module structure, and the Moyal coadjoint action acts
on the residue side and does not lift to the polynomial shadow
(Corollary~\ref{cor:descendant-coadjoint-difference},
Theorem~\ref{thm:app-defect-polarization}).

\medskip
\noindent
The cochain CE/PV identity sends Hamiltonian-dual coordinates to
Schouten vector fields and shifted-cotangent-dual coordinates to
measured boundary Hamiltonians,
\[
  c^I\longmapsto\theta^I,
  \qquad
  u_I\longmapsto O_I ,
\]
realising the completed CE cochains of \(\mathfrak g\), inside their
coordinate window, as a dg-commutative algebra isomorphic to the
Schouten polyvectors of the boundary Hamiltonian polynomial algebra
\(\widehat{\mathbf S}(\bar A)\),
\(\bar A=\C[z_1,z_2]/\C\cdot1\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}).  The brane
substitution is the image of \(u_f\), not of \(c_f\): a Hamiltonian
generates motion on the closed side and is measured on the open
side.  The \(P_0\)-bracket and Maurer--Cartan kernel refinements hold
on exactly the topologised subalgebras
\(B\subset\mathrm{PV}_{\mathrm{coord}}\) for which the Schouten
bracket and the diagonal kernel converge; the strict ambient product
completion supports neither.  The minimal RG-stable analytic
completion generated by \(\{O_f,\theta_f\}_{f\in\bar A}\) under the
Schouten bracket and pointwise product is the weighted-Tate habitat
\(B_w\), in which the heat-kernel propagator extends and the Casimir
\(C_{\mathfrak h,w}\in
\mathfrak h_w\widehat\otimes\mathfrak h^\vee_{w,\mathrm{cont}}\)
converges
(Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}).

\medskip
\noindent
The factorization-current model on the brane line is the local
algebra of brane measurements: the three locality identities
\[
  \delta(t-s),
  \qquad
  L_v=[Q,\iota_v],
  \qquad
  L_{\partial/\partial\bar z_i}
    =[Q,\iota_{\partial/\partial\bar z_i}]
\]
force support, topological, and formal-holomorphic locality
respectively; the trace observables exhibit all three, the
shifted-cotangent currents only the first two
(Theorem~\ref{thm:dict-kinematic-locality}).  Hamiltonians smeared on
a brane interval lift to factorisation currents whose arity-two
collisions reproduce the Hamiltonian Poisson bracket and the
residue-coadjoint action on \(\mathcal P\)
(Theorems~\ref{thm:hamiltonian-current-factorization},
\ref{thm:reduced-principal-part-boundary-current}).  Compactly
supported Dolbeault forms on a holomorphic polydisk
\(B\subset\C^2\), tensored with \(\mathfrak g\), realise the native
holomorphic factorization algebra
\[
  \mathcal F^{\mathrm{hol}}_{\mathrm{Ham}}(B)
    =C^\bullet_{\mathrm{CE},\mathrm{cont}}
     \bigl(\Omega^{0,\bullet}_c(B)\widehat\otimes\mathfrak g\bigr),
\]
locally constant in \(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\) and holomorphic in
\(\C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}\); its
Bochner--Martinelli--Koppelman kernel
\cite{laurent-thiebaut-bmk,ruppenthal-bmk}, finite Matlis moments,
and arity-two output projection assemble into a one-pair analytic
pro-Matlis retract
(Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-window-bm-native-e2-transfer}).

\medskip
\noindent
Constants vanish on the closed side as Hamiltonian vector fields and
survive on the open side as the brane center of mass:
\(\{\operatorname{Tr}\phi_1,\operatorname{Tr}\phi_2\}=N\) on the
unreduced phase space.  Four equivalent sightings detect the same
nonzero class --- the algebraic central extension
\(0\to\C\!\cdot\!1\to\C[z_1,z_2]\to\bar A\to 0\), the Lie central
extension
\(0\to\C\bar c\to\widehat{\mathfrak h}_{\mathrm{cent}}\to\bar A\to 0\),
the trace-algebraic boundary central extension on the unreduced phase
space, and the quantum Weyl commutator
\([\Phi_\hbar(z_1),\Phi_\hbar(z_2)]_\star=\hbar N\) ---
realising
\(\hbar N[\bar c]\in H^2_{\mathrm{Lie}}(\bar A,\C)[[\hbar]]\)
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-scalar-anomaly-transgression}).  After Moyal
quantisation \cite{kontsevich-dq} this is a Capelli term
\cite{capelli,howe-capelli} and the first quantum master equation
obstruction
\[
  W_1^{\mathrm{sc,ord}}=\hbar N\,[\bar c]
\]
in the ordinary scalar-reduced \(\mathfrak{gl}_N\) branch, cancelled
by the unreduced central-character, opposite central-extension, and
balanced-supertrace replacement branches
(Theorems~\ref{thm:u1-center-anomaly},
\ref{thm:u1-center-anomaly-open},
\ref{thm:quantum-classical-anomaly}).
The Moyal star product preserves the reduced current comparison
componentwise: weighted BV kernel convergence, balanced-supertrace
scalar QME cancellation on its habitat, weighted Moyal principal-part
current brackets, and a finite-certificate degree-zero Hamiltonian
trace comparison
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison}).
The finite-certificate range covers the balanced bidegree diagonal
through \((6,6)\), every non-edge bidegree of total degree at most
\(13\), the total-degree-\(14\) bidegrees
\((3,11),(11,3),(4,10),(10,4),(5,9),(9,5)\), and the complete
one-moving-letter edge strips
(Proposition~\ref{prop:app-edge-pbw-telescoping}).

\medskip
\noindent
\emph{All-bidegree radial/Weyl normal-form vanishing.}  At each
bidegree \((a,b)\), failure of the Moyal/Weyl trace comparison to a
degree-zero Hamiltonian normal form is a single class
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\);
positive form is the decorated PBW Stokes identity
\(D^\square_{a,b}=C^+_{a,b}\partial_2\), failure exactly a signed row
\((\phi,-\lambda)\in\ker B_{a,b}^*\) with
\(\lambda(E^+_{a,b})-\phi(T_{a,b})\ne0\)
(Proposition~\ref{prop:app-radial-dual-potential-obstruction}).  The
radial bicomplex is interior-acyclic conditional on the named
radial-parts statement
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-radial-bicomplex-acyclic}); unconditionally,
each interior bidegree closes by stable Procesi--Razmyslov reduction
through total degree~15.

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{Reduced non-scalar Costello quantum master equation.}  In the
filtered local-functional complex
\(\mathfrak Q^\bullet_{w,\partial,\hbar}(I)\) with
quantum-master-equation differential \(d_{\mathrm{QME}}\), once a
complete \(d_{\mathrm{QME}}\)-stable scalar-contact filtration is
supplied for which the successive scalar-symbol extension classes
vanish, the filtered scalar-symbol projection
\(\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}}\) is constructed and the reduced
non-scalar QME obstruction is the cohomology class
\[
  [\operatorname{Ob}^{\mathrm{red}}_{w,\partial,\hbar}]
  \in
  H^1\!\bigl(\ker\widehat\sigma_{\mathrm{sc}},
    d_{\mathrm{QME}}\bigr)
\]
together with its finite-window
\(\varprojlim^1H^0\)-primitive-compatibility class
(Problem~\ref{prob:quantum-p0-operation-realization}); the smallest
non-scalar window \(\theta_3\) carries the bare verdict
\(H^1=\C\!\cdot\!E_{\theta_3}\ne 0\) with covector \(\ell=1\) and,
under the supplied counterterm \(dC=-E\) with \(c=1\) unique,
\(H^1=0\) (Theorem~\ref{thm:app-theta-three-finite-window-H1}).

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{Unreduced Tate-coefficient cotangent lift.}  The reduced cochain
CE/PV map promotes to the chain-level factorization-centre morphism
\[
  \Phi_{\mathrm{cl}}\colon
  \mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{closed},H}\big|_L
  \longrightarrow
  \mathrm Z^{P_0}_{\mathrm{fact}}\!
  \bigl(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{cl}}_{\mathrm{open},N}\bigr)
\]
exactly when the obstruction vector
\(\mathrm{Ob}_{\mathrm{cent/QME/desc}}=
(\mathrm{ob}^{\mathrm{prod}}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\mathrm{ob}^{P_0}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\mathrm{ob}^{\mathrm{bd}}_{\mathrm{QME}},
\delta^{\mathrm{Weiss}}_{\check C/R})\)
vanishes
(Theorem~\ref{thm:app-unreduced-cotangent-lift-obstruction},
Problem~\ref{prob:formal-factorization-center}); the chain-level
primitive shadow
(Theorem~\ref{thm:chain-level-primitive-projection}) computes the
\(E_1\)-page of the spectral sequence converging to
\(\mathrm{ob}^{P_0}_{\mathrm{cent}}\).

\smallskip
\noindent
\emph{Strict native all-window Bochner--Martinelli current transfer.}
The native \(\C^2\) holomorphic factorization object admits a strict
support-local finite-current transfer to the brane line iff the
obstruction vector
\[
  \operatorname{Ob}^{\Pi}_{\mathrm{BM}}
   =(\operatorname{ob}_{\oplus},\operatorname{ob}_{I_\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{\mathfrak h,\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{collar},\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{3,\Pi},
     \operatorname{ob}_{\mathrm{unif},\Pi})
\]
--- uniform moment restriction onto the direct-sum Matlis module, a
compact-current section of the pro-product tower, the full Hamiltonian
action on the chosen target, collar removal under extension-by-zero
factorization, the first transferred ternary obstruction, and the
\(\mathfrak m\)-adic uniform estimate --- vanishes or is replaced by an
admissible compact-current substitute.  The strict form fails for
every \(N\): the residue-coadjoint formula returns
\(z_1^{N+2}\cdot\rho_{N+1,0}=-(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\) and the projected
finite-window Jacobiator is \((N+1)(N+2)\rho_{0,1}\ne 0\), so high
transverse holomorphic modes leak back into low brane-momentum modes
(Theorem~\ref{thm:bmk-finite-window-no-go}); the pro-Matlis retract
is the supremum form selected by this leak
(Corollary~\ref{cor:promatlis-retract-as-supremum}).

\end{abstract}
