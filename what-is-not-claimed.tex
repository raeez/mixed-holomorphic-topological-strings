\section*{What is not claimed}\label{sec:what-is-not-claimed}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{What is not claimed}

The local theorem is sharp on a deliberately narrow surface.  The
following statements are \emph{not} asserted by anything in this
manuscript; each is a target lying outside the formal-local mixed
holomorphic-topological model on
\(\R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\widehat{\C^2}_{0,\mathrm{hol}}\).

\paragraph{Not a global worldsheet string theory.}  ``String'' is used
in the local-string-field-theoretic sense of Bershadsky--Cecotti--
Ooguri--Vafa~\cite{bcov}, Witten~\cite{witten-cs-as-string}, and
Costello~\cite{costello-bv} for the BV/factorization observables of a
local field theory --- not in the sense of a worldsheet
amplitude prescription on a target Calabi--Yau threefold.  No
Polyakov-style worldsheet path integral, no Riemann-surface
construction, and no physical-state cohomology of a closed bosonic or
type II string is constructed or implied.

\paragraph{Not a compact CY\(_3\) BCOV theorem.}  The Calabi--Yau
input here is the trivial holomorphic volume / holomorphic-symplectic
datum on \(\widehat{\C^2}_0\), used solely for the BV pairing,
divergence-free Hamiltonian fields, and cyclic trace densities of the
local model.  No compact CY\(_3\) BCOV partition function, no
holomorphic anomaly equation, no all-genus topological-string
generating function, and no CY\(_3\) Kodaira--Spencer construction is
proved or implied.  Cross-volume use of compact CY\(_3\) BCOV passes
through the matched-conventions acceptance class
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{UKD}}\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:matched-conventions-transfer}.

\paragraph{Not a derivation of MNOP, GV, OSV, DT, or PT.}  Generating
functions on compact CY\(_3\) targets, Gromov--Witten / Donaldson--
Thomas / Pandharipande--Thomas / MNOP virtual-cycle counts, OSV
attractor identifications, and any quintic, K3, or other compact
target-specific output are external programmes.  None is derived from
the local theorem; the cross-volume firewall theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-volume-firewall}) records this exclusion as
seven divergence rows, each naming the local convention layer, the
target convention layer, the obstruction class, and the
matched-conventions claim that the local theorem could in principle
contribute, never as a free implication.

\paragraph{Not a BKM / Igusa derivation of \(\Delta_5\).}  The
modular-form frontier of the manuscript records the Borcherds /
Borcherds--Kac--Moody bridge to the Igusa cusp form \(\Delta_5\) as a
heuristic and convention-checking comparison surface
(Theorem~\ref{thm:divergence-igusa-bkm}); it is \emph{not} a BKM
consequence of the local Hamiltonian BF/Moyal calculation.  In
particular, no equality \(K(A)=\kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}\) of the local
Hamiltonian scalar cocycle \(\hbar N[\bar c]\) with a Borcherds--Kac--
Moody \(\Delta_5\) scalar is asserted, and the Vol III material itself
records the failure of naive scalar collapse at canonical witnesses.

\paragraph{Not a derivation of Volumes I, II, III.}  The companion
programme has three volumes.  Volume I (\(\sim\)~\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar})
constructs the algebraic bar/cobar duality calculus, the modular
Maurer--Cartan element \(\Theta_A\), and the derived chiral centre
\(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)=C^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(A,A)\) of
an \(A_\infty\)-chiral algebra.  Volume II
(\(\sim\)~\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2}) interprets the pair
\((Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A),A)\) as the bulk-boundary datum of
a 3d holomorphic-topological QFT.  Volume III
(\(\sim\)~\texttt{calabi-yau-quantum-groups}) supplies the Calabi--Yau
categorical source via the two-stage functor
\(\Phi_d=\mathrm{Sp}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d\).
None of Volumes I--III is proved here; this manuscript is the formal
Hamiltonian Darboux brane-probe chart of the last arrow only -- the
restriction of \(Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\) to a protected
Hamiltonian normal slice at a brane stack and the trace measurement
\(u_f\mapsto J(f)\) on that slice.

\paragraph{Not an all-bidegree Moyal/Weyl/radial closure.}  The
componentwise quantum coefficient comparison
(Theorem~\ref{thm:componentwise-quantum-coefficient-comparison}) is
proved in a finite certificate range -- balanced diagonal through
\((6,6)\), all non-edge bidegrees through total degree \(13\),
selected total-degree-\(14\) cases, and one-moving-letter edge strips
-- and the all-bidegree statement is the conditional radial-bicomplex
acyclicity of Theorem~\ref{thm:app-radial-bicomplex-acyclic} with
named obstruction class
\(\Omega^{\mathrm{rad}}_{a,b}\in\operatorname{coker}B_{a,b}\).  No
all-bidegree closure of the Moyal/Weyl/radial comparison is asserted
unconditionally; the first undecided total-degree-\(14\) systems are
\((6,8)\), \((8,6)\), \((7,7)\).

\paragraph{Not a chain-level CE/PV identity at the strict full-disk endpoint.}
The CE/PV comparison is a five-rung ladder
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-ce-pv-koszul-criterion}): the cochain
identity (Rung 1) is unconditional; the \(P_0\)-bracket clause (Rung
2) is conditional on bracket-admissibility; the Maurer--Cartan kernel
clause (Rung 3) is conditional on kernel-admissibility; the
bar-cobar/enveloping clause (Rung 4) is conditional on Tate /
nilpotent / weighted admissibility data with conilpotent endpoint and
\(\varprojlim^1\)-vanishing; the open-trace \(A_\infty\)-Koszul clause
(Rung 5) is conditional on an acceptance datum.  The strict full
formal-disk product/direct-sum endpoint carries none of the
\(P_0\)-bracket, kernel, bar-cobar, or open-Koszul conclusions
without these admissibility hypotheses.  Statements claiming a
``chain-level CE/PV identity'' or a strict factorization-centre
identification at the unmodified formal polydisk are not made; the
admissible weighted-Tate target is the minimal RG-stable habitat in
which the higher rungs hold (Theorem~\ref{thm:wt-canonical-habitat}).

\paragraph{Not a curve vertex-algebra or chiral-algebra identification.}
The native holomorphic object on the formal symplectic disk is a
two-complex-dimensional Hamiltonian holomorphic factorization algebra,
not a one-complex-dimensional vertex / OPE / Zhu chiral algebra.  Any
curve chiral-algebra reduction requires additional restriction data
(reduced direction, residue pushforward, induced BV pairing,
brane image, anomaly matching) supplied externally; the manuscript
records the warning at the matrix microscope statement and does not
specialise to a curve VOA without that data.

\paragraph{Not unitarity in the Hilbert-space sense.}  ``Unitarity'' in
this manuscript is the BV replacement: the protected sector imposes
classical and quantum master equations, locality, and factorization
associativity, not Hilbert-space positivity.  No Hilbert-space inner
product, no positive-definite physical-state norm, and no
representation-theoretic unitarity statement is constructed.

\paragraph{Not a complete quantum theory.}  Moyal/Weyl quantization is
a measurement compatibility test in the sense of
Principle~6 (\S\ref{sec:organizing-principles}): it asks whether Weyl
ordering and brane trace measurement commute, with finite-certificate
passes and named obstruction classes outside the certificate range.
The full quantum theory of the local model -- a complete BV
quantization satisfying QME beyond order \(\hbar\), an all-graph
Costello renormalization, a chain-level quantum factorization centre,
or a quantum modular trace -- is not constructed; each upgrade is a
transport problem in the deformation-theoretic sense of
Principle~7, with controlling obstruction class named in
\S\ref{ssec:tate-residual-resolution} and Appendix~\ref{app:master-deformation-complex}.

\medskip\noindent
The cross-volume firewall theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cross-volume-firewall}) governs every external use
of the local theorem.  The local theorem contributes exactly the
transported formal-disk stalk
\(\chi_{\mathrm{op}}^{-1}\Phi^{\mathrm{Ham}}_{\mathrm{fact}}\chi_{\mathrm{cl}}\)
of an external target morphism, and only after a matched-conventions
datum has been supplied and the universal Koszul-descent acceptance
class \(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{UKD}}(\mathfrak C_{\mathrm{tar}})\)
has been null-homotoped.  A nonzero coordinate of
\(\operatorname{Ob}_{\mathrm{UKD}}\) is the precise mechanism of the
target that fails.
