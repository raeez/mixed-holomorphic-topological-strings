\chapter*{This repository and the programme}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{This repository and the programme}

This monograph is the integrated treatment shared by the mathematics
repositories: one canonical spine, byte-identical wherever it is carried,
together with the audit and obstruction chapters merged in from the
ordered-chiral reconstruction.

The programme has three volumes. \textbf{Volume I} is ordered chiral
geometry, in \texttt{chiral-bar-cobar}. \textbf{Volume II} is the mixed
holomorphic--topological theory, in \texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2}.
\textbf{Volume III} is Calabi--Yau quantum groups, in
\texttt{calabi-yau-quantum-groups}. The universal chiral BV and Einstein
completion accompanies them without yet having a repository of its own.

This repository is \emph{not} one of the three volumes. It carries
\emph{Remarks on Mixed Holomorphic--Topological Strings}, a standalone
manuscript on formal local Hamiltonian BF theory, reduced CE and PV
central operations, and a Dirac brane probe on
$\mathbb R^2_{\mathrm{top}}\times\mathbb C^2_{\mathrm{hol}}$.

Its subject and the subject of the spine's mixed holomorphic--topological
volume overlap: both treat Hamiltonian jets, shifted cotangent fields, and
brane observables on the formal symplectic plane. Whether the standalone
manuscript is superseded by that volume, or refines it on the trace
sector, is a mathematical judgment that has not been made. Both are
present: the integrated monograph is built here, and the standalone
manuscript still builds under its own target. Neither has been discarded
on the strength of a guess.
